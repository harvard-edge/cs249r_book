{\large\bfseries Volume IV margin stack --- six treatments, true type and true width}\\[2pt]
{\footnotesize All set in the book's Palatino (\texttt{newpxtext}) at the real
\texttt{marginparwidth} of 1.25\,in. Shading is the \emph{memory} chapter, brain tier active.}

\vspace{9mm}
\newcommand{\slot}[3]{\begin{minipage}[t][5.6cm][t]{3.55cm}%
  #1\par\vspace{3mm}{\footnotesize\bfseries #2}\par
  {\fontsize{7}{8.6}\selectfont #3}\end{minipage}}
\noindent
\slot{\dOne}{D1 Filled ladder}{The vol1 form ported straight over. Every tier
carries ink, so the active tier has to win on value alone.}\hfill
\slot{\dTwo}{D2 Outline, one active}{Only the active tier is filled. The other
three become a quiet frame. Widened to 3.05\,cm by dropping the spine.}\hfill
\slot{\dThree}{D3 Rule and text}{No boxes at all. A hairline spine, names, and one
accent tick. Least ink on the page.}

\vspace{6mm}
\noindent
\slot{\dFour}{D4 Lollipop axis}{Position on an axis rather than a stack of
blocks. Reads as a sequence, which may be the wrong metaphor for layers.}\hfill
\slot{\dFive}{D5 Chip and label}{A colour chip carries the state, the name sits
in clear space beside it. Handles long tier names without compromise.}\hfill
\slot{\dSix}{D6 Solid column}{Contiguous bands, no gaps. Reads most like a single
stacked object rather than four separate chips.}

\vspace{4mm}
\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}\par\vspace{2mm}
{\footnotesize\textbf{The constraint the real font exposed.} ``Nervous System''
in Palatino \texttt{\textbackslash scriptsize}\,bold measures 2.55\,cm. The vol1
rung is 2.60\,cm wide and gives 0.12\,cm to its label inset, so vol4's longest
tier name does not fit vol1's geometry --- it was only the wrong prototype font
that made it look as though it did. D2 and D6 answer this by taking the full
3.05\,cm and giving up the cross-cutting spine; D3, D4 and D5 answer it by moving
the label out of the box entirely.}
