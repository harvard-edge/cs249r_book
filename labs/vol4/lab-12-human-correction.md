## Experiment

If studying learning from correction, record a human intervention or corrective demonstration, revise the dataset or preprocessing, retrain or fine-tune a compact policy, and repeat the same frozen physical trial set. Keep the previous policy as a control. If repairing the system instead, change one documented component and compare the same trial set; identify whether the learned policy itself changed. A controller-rule change alone does not establish learning from correction.

## Notebook evidence and exit check

Record the intervention or repair log, changed artifact revision, and paired before/after physical results including failures. The bench review checks whether the team can attribute the observed change to a documented revision or report that the evidence is inconclusive. This exercises **C7, C15**.

**Carry forward:** before/after physical results and a clear account of what changed. Keep the raw trace and artifact revision so the next lab can reconstruct this result.
