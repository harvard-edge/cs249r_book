# Lab 4 — Can an episode reconstruct what happened?

**Status:** Draft student lab brief. Andrea will try this exercise on the kit and record the student artifact, time required, starter materials, and any changes in the [feasibility plan](feasibility-plan.md). Staff will set motion limits and reset instructions from the tested station before releasing the handout.

**Read before class:** [Chapter 5: Physical Data](../../books/vol4/05_data/05_data.qmd); [Chapter 8: Sensor Perception](../../books/vol4/08_perception/08_perception.qmd).

**Start from:** Lab 3 action schema and teleoperation input.

## Experiment

Teleoperate guarded reaches and a few soft-block moves. Record images, joint readback, instruction or goal, four action taps, and observed outcomes. Replay one episode; deliberately introduce a missing frame or timing gap.

## Submit and exit check

Submit a replayable LeRobot episode, linked MCU log, timing alignment rule, and dataset card. Pass when another team can reconstruct the action that followed a selected observation and identify the corrupted episode. This exercises **C3, C7**.

**Carry forward:** replayable episode and dataset card. Keep the raw trace and artifact revision so the next lab can reconstruct this result.
