# Enumerated Sampling Frame and Split Hygiene for the ML Systems Taxonomy

This document specifies the five-stratum sampling frame, provenance-family clustering rules, and split allocation protocol (`dev`, `held_out_test`, and `reserve`) synthesized from `Method-A` (§3), `Method-B` (§3), and audited `Method-C` (§3.2), together with the executed Phase 3 allocation (`taxonomy/method-study/preregistration.yaml`) and post-Phase-6 global cluster-burn ledger (`taxonomy/method-study/burned-clusters.json`) for `P4` and `Q4`.

*Note: Sampling-frame strata `S1`–`S5` denote source populations and are distinct from Stack area codes `S1`–`S4`.*

---

## 1. Five Stratified Source Populations (`S1`–`S5`)

Every candidate concept and every coverage or indexing evaluation unit is drawn from an enumerated frame partitioned into five source populations (`Method-A` §3.1, `Method-B` §3.1). Source stratum records **where an excerpt comes from**; warrant type under ANSI/NISO Z39.19 §5.3.5.1–§5.3.5.3 is recorded separately so operational proxies are not conflated with direct user search logs or organizational term-preference mandates:

| Stratum ID | Stratum Name | Unit of Sampling | Inclusion Criteria & Warrant Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`S1`** | **Textbooks & Reference Monographs** | Chapter / major section (`1` work per unit prospectively) | Published or open-access textbooks covering ML systems architecture, distributed training/serving, hardware acceleration, or safety/reliability engineering (both external monographs and project-internal `MLSysBook` chapters). Provides **literary & curriculum warrant** (`Z39.19 §5.3.5.1`; internal `MLSysBook` chapters carry `origin: internal`). |
| **`S2`** | **University Courses** | Course syllabus (lecture module + assignment/lab scope; `1` course per unit prospectively) | Graduate or upper-division undergraduate ML systems courses at accredited universities with public lecture schedules and reading lists, stratified across hardware/compilers, systems/networking, and safe/reliable deployment (`Method-A` §3.2). Provides **external literary & pedagogical warrant** (except Harvard `CS249r`, which shares `MLSysBook` authorship and carries `origin: internal`). |
| **`S3`** | **Peer-Reviewed Systems & ML Venues** | Paper (`1` paper per unit prospectively: title, abstract, architecture/evaluation passage, or blind venue topic entry) | Peer-reviewed papers from systems conferences (`MLSys`, `OSDI`, `SOSP`, `NSDI`, `ASPLOS`, `ISCA`, `EuroSys`, `CAV`, `AISec`) and systems/safety tracks of ML/NLP venues (`NeurIPS`, `ICML`, `ICLR`, `EMNLP`, `FAccT`, `SATML`) within the six-calendar-year study window (`2021`–`2026` inclusive at study freeze) plus pre-registered foundational papers (`>500` citations in Google Scholar / OpenAlex or standard `S2` syllabus adoption). Provides **external literary warrant**. |
| **`S4`** | **Practitioner Artifacts, Postmortems & Diagnostic Scenarios** | Engineering design doc, incident postmortem, runtime spec, or diagnostic scenario | Public production engineering design guides, open-source serving/runtime documentation (`Triton`, `Ray Serve`, `ONNX Runtime` — used solely to extract underlying mechanisms, never product concepts), frontier deployment governance policies, and `StaffML` diagnostic scenarios (`origin: internal`). Provides **operational literary warrant** and (for `StaffML` tag counts across the `10,716`-question bank) an **assignment-frequency proxy**, not direct user search/request warrant (`Z39.19 §5.3.5.3`). |
| **`S5`** | **Standards, Assurance Guidance & Benchmark Specifications** | Standard clause / assurance guidance module / benchmark scenario specification | Published technical standards, public assurance guidance reports, and benchmark specifications (`NIST AI 100-1`, `NIST AI 600-1`, `ISO 26262`, `ISO 21448`, `ISO/IEC 42001`, `ISO/PAS 8800`, `UL 4600`, `OWASP Top 10 for LLM`, `MITRE ATLAS`, `MLPerf Inference/Training/Tiny`). Provides **normative literary warrant** (`Z39.19 §5.3.5.1`), not internal organizational preferred-term mandates (`Z39.19 §5.3.5.2`) unless term-form adoption is separately logged. |

### Cross-Stratum Scale Representation (`Method-C` §3.2, `Method-B` §3.2)
To prevent datacenter LLM serving from crowding out resource-constrained or classical ML systems, the frame requires representation across execution scales where applicable to an area:
- **Datacenter / Cloud Scale** (multi-node accelerator cluster serving, training, and fleet scheduling)
- **Enterprise / Single-Node / Workstation** (gateway/microservice orchestration, CPU/single-accelerator inference, retrieval and compound pipelines)
- **Edge / On-Device / Embedded (`TinyML`)** (MCU/NPU/mobile quantization, static arena memory planning, battery/thermal envelopes)

## 2. Global Provenance-Family Clustering (`provenance_family_id`)

Before stratification, split assignment, or warrant counting, every unit in `<campaign>/sampling-manifest.json` receives an explicit `unit_id`, `provenance_family_id`, `stratum` (`S1`–`S5`), `split` (`dev`, `held_out_test`, `reserve`), `origin` (`external` | `internal`), `editor_affiliated` (`true` | `false`), and `source_type` (`prose` | `heading`, with `passage_kind: primary_excerpt | curated_summary` on prose units and `authors` or `institutional_author: true` on external units) (`Method-A` §2.1 & §3.3, `Method-B` §2.2 & §3.4):

1. **Shared Authorship, Institutional Organization, & Global `work_id` Lineage (`compute_lineage_components` in `binder/cli/core/taxonomy_schema.py`)**: Let $A_i$ denote the normalized set of **all listed personal authors** of external source $i$ (`authors` array in `sampling-manifest.json` and per-work `work_rosters` in `exposed_cluster_metadata`, mandatory on every non-institutional `origin: external` unit; institutional standards without personal author rosters set `institutional_author: true` and `institutional_organization` / `institutional_organizations` on prospective units, seeded across all `49` external canonical clusters in `exposed_cluster_metadata` in `burned-clusters.json` `schema_version: 3`, alongside a global `1 work_id <-> 1 canonical provenance_family_id` bijection rejecting any work split across multiple effective clusters). Two external sources or clusters are linked using **separated personal-author and institutional-organization denominators** when personal author overlap satisfies $|A_i \cap A_j| / \min(|A_i|, |A_j|) \ge 0.50$ (across cluster rosters or any per-work `work_rosters` entry) **or** when their normalized `institutional_organizations` sets share any technical committee or standards body ($|O_i \cap O_j| \ge 1$; `pf_iso42001_pas8800` and `pf_iso_automotive_ul4600` union via shared `ISO/TC 22/SC 32`), and connected components (transitive closure computed identically by `compute_lineage_components` across `reconcile.py`, `apply.py`, `taxonomy_schema.py`, and `build_study_receipts.py`) form a single `effective_lineage_component_id` across all strata and areas.
2. **Paper–System–Benchmark–Checklist Lineage, Course Disambiguation, & Prospective 1-Work-Per-Unit Rule**: A research paper, its companion open-source documentation, tutorials derived from it, and any checklist row summarizing that same work share one `provenance_family_id` (`cluster_merges` in `taxonomy/method-study/burned-clusters.json`: `pf_cov_courses_2026_AIE -> pf_huyen_dmls`, `pf_cov_courses_2026_C868 -> pf_cmu_llmsys`, `pf_cov_courses_2026_DMLS -> pf_huyen_dmls`, `pf_cov_courses_2026_HAR -> pf_internal_mlsysbook`, `pf_cov_courses_2026_M594 -> pf_mit_han_tinyml`, `pf_cov_courses_2026_MLS26 -> pf_cov_mlsys_25_26_mlsys2026`, `pf_cov_courses_2026_NIST -> pf_nist_ai_rmf`, `pf_cov_courses_2026_S329 -> pf_huyen_dmls`, `pf_cov_courses_2026_S336 -> pf_stanford_cs336`, `pf_cov_courses_2026_UT378 -> pf_ut_cs378`). Note the explicit course-code disambiguations in `burned-clusters.json` (`courses_2026_code_legend`): `S329` in `courses-and-standards-2026.yml` is Stanford `CS 329S` (*Machine Learning Systems Design*, Chip Huyen, merging into `pf_huyen_dmls`), distinct from `pf_stanford_cs329t` (Stanford `CS 329T` *Trustworthy Machine Learning*, Koyejo & Hashimoto); and `B294` in `courses-and-standards-2026.yml` is UC Berkeley `CS 294-162` (*Machine Learning Systems*, Stoica & Gonzalez, which author-unions with `pf_vllm_sosp23` / `pf_sglang_neurips24` / `pf_slora_mlsys24`), distinct from `pf_berkeley_aisafety` (UC Berkeley `CS 294-267` *AI Safety, Alignment, and Control*, Steinhardt / Song / Hendrycks, which author-unions with `pf_cov_courses_2026_BAG` and `pf_amodei_concrete_safety`). **Historical Phase 3 bundling vs. prospective rule:** in the executed Phase 3 `P4`+`Q4` curation (`preregistration.yaml`), four curated `Q4` prose units bundled paired foundational works (`Q4-S3-SIMPLEX-CBF-01` pairing Sha 2001 *Simplex* and Ames et al. 2019 *Control Barrier Functions*; `Q4-S3-NN-VERIFICATION-01` pairing Katz et al. ## 3. Split Allocation Protocol and Post-Phase-6 Cluster-Burn State

1. **Prospective Refresh Targets (`60%` / `25%` / `15%`)**:
   - In periodic refreshes (`.claude/workflows/taxonomy-refresh.md`, `taxonomy/METHOD.md`), global external `provenance_family_id` clusters are assigned once before construction with target cluster proportions of **`60%` `dev`** (visible to builders alongside internal `dev` corpora), **`25%` `held_out_test`** (sealed external clusters for Step 5b evaluation), and **`15%` `reserve`** (sequestered external clusters for replenishing burned `held_out_test` slices alongside fresh post-cutoff external sources). Multi-area clusters are assigned as whole clusters prior to per-area tallies.
2. **Executed Phase 3 Allocation in the Two-Area Study (`preregistration.yaml`, `seed = 20261103`)**:
   - In the completed Phase 3 `P4`+`Q4` study (`382` units across `97` pre-deduplication `provenance_family_id` clusters [`95` deduplicated clusters under the `2` Phase 3 merges `pf_cov_courses_2026_DMLS -> pf_huyen_dmls` and `pf_cov_courses_2026_NIST -> pf_nist_ai_rmf`, and **`88` deduplicated canonical clusters** (`39` internal + `49` external; **`41` effective lineage components = `1` internal + `40` external** after `< 50%` personal-author and `institutional_organizations` overlap transitive closure) under the full `10` work-level `cluster_merges` audited in Phase 7]; `0` raw cross-split clusters at Phase 3, `3` canonical cross-split clusters under the `10` merges [`pf_stanford_cs336`, `pf_cov_mlsys_25_26_mlsys2026`, `pf_ut_cs378`]), `build_phase3_preregistration.py` combined pre-designated benchmark splits (`fixed_split`) with a deterministic cell-wise draw (`seed = 20261103`) over movable `(area, stratum)` cells of size $n$ (`1` cluster to `held_out_test` when $n \ge 3$, `1` cluster to `reserve` when $n \ge 4$, and the remaining $n - \mathbb{I}(n \ge 3) - \mathbb{I}(n \ge 4)$ clusters to `dev`).
   - Because single-passage curated sources each formed a 1-unit `dev` cluster while `held_out_test` and `reserve` held larger venue/question-bank clusters, the **achieved Phase 3 raw cluster split was `76 / 97` (`78.4%`) `dev`, `15 / 97` (`15.5%`) `held_out_test`, and `6 / 97` (`6.2%`) `reserve`**, and the **achieved unit split was `200 / 382` (`52.4%`) `dev`, `126 / 382` (`33.0%`) `held_out_test`, and `56 / 382` (`14.7%`) `reserve`**:
     - **Raw clusters by split (`97` total in Phase 3, after classifying `pf_cov_courses_2026_HAR` as internal)**: `76` `dev` (`47` `P4`-only, `23` `Q4`-only, `6` `P4`/`Q4`-shared; `44` `external`, `32` `internal`), `15` `held_out_test` (`8` `P4`-only, `6` `Q4`-only, `1` `P4`/`Q4`-shared; `10` `external` [`9` deduplicated external lineages], `5` `internal`), and `6` `reserve` (`3` `P4`-only, `3` `Q4`-only; `4` `external`, `2` `internal`).
     - **Units by split, area, and origin (`382` total in Phase 3, after classifying `HAR` as internal)**: `dev` = `200` (`142` `P4` [`94` `external`, `48` `internal`], `58` `Q4` [`14` `external`, `44` `internal`]); `held_out_test` = `126` (`116` `P4` [`75` `external`, `41` `internal`], `10` `Q4` [`4` `external`, `6` `internal`]); `reserve` = `56` (`47` `P4` [`46` `external`, `1` `internal`], `9` `Q4` [`2` `external`, `7` `internal`]).
