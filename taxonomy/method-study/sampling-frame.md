# Enumerated Sampling Frame and Split Hygiene for the ML Systems Taxonomy

This document specifies the five-stratum sampling frame, provenance-family clustering rules, and split allocation protocol (`dev`, `held_out_test`, and `reserve`) synthesized from `Method-A` (§3), `Method-B` (§3), and audited `Method-C` (§3.2), together with the executed Phase 3 allocation (`taxonomy/method-study/preregistration.yaml`) and post-Phase-6 global cluster-burn ledger (`taxonomy/method-study/burned-clusters.json`) for `P4` and `Q4`.

*Note: Sampling-frame strata `S1`–`S5` denote source populations and are distinct from Stack area codes `S1`–`S4`.*

---

## 1. Five Stratified Source Populations (`S1`–`S5`)

Every candidate concept and every coverage or indexing evaluation unit is drawn from an enumerated frame partitioned into five source populations (`Method-A` §3.1, `Method-B` §3.1). Source stratum records **where an excerpt comes from**; warrant type under ANSI/NISO Z39.19 §5.3.5.1–§5.3.5.3 is recorded separately so operational proxies are not conflated with direct user search logs or organizational term-preference mandates:

| Stratum ID | Stratum Name | Unit of Sampling | Inclusion Criteria & Warrant Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`S1`** | **Textbooks & Reference Monographs** | Chapter / major section (`1` work per unit prospectively) | Published or open-access textbooks covering ML systems architecture, distributed training/serving, hardware acceleration, or safety/reliability engineering (both external monographs and project-internal `MLSysBook` chapters). Provides **literary & curriculum warrant** (`Z39.19 §5.3.5.1`; internal `MLSysBook` chapters carry `origin: internal`). |
| **`S2`** | **University Courses** | Course syllabus (lecture module + assignment/lab scope; `1` course per unit prospectively) | Graduate or upper-division undergraduate ML systems courses at accredited universities with public lecture schedules and reading lists, stratified across hardware/compilers, systems/networking, and safe/reliable deployment (`Method-A` §3.2). Provides **external literary & pedagogical warrant** (except Harvard `CS249r`, which shares `MLSysBook` authorship and carries `origin: internal`). |
| **`S3`** | **Peer-Reviewed Systems & ML Venues** | Paper (`1` paper per unit prospectively: title, abstract, architecture/evaluation passage, or blind venue topic entry) | Peer-reviewed papers from systems conferences (`MLSys`, `OSDI`, `SOSP`, `NSDI`, `ASPLOS`, `ISCA`, `EuroSys`, `CAV`, `AISec`) and systems/safety tracks of ML/NLP venues (`NeurIPS`, `ICML`, `ICLR`, `EMNLP`, `FAccT`, `SATML`) within the six-calendar-year study window (`2021`–`2026` inclusive at study freeze) plus pre-registered foundational papers (`>500` citations in Google Scholar / OpenAlex or standard `S2` syllabus adoption). Provides **external literary warrant**. |
| **`S4`** | **Practitioner Artifacts, Postmortems & Diagnostic Scenarios** | Engineering design doc, incident postmortem, runtime spec, or diagnostic scenario | Public production engineering design guides, open-source serving/runtime documentation (`Triton`, `Ray Serve`, `ONNX Runtime` — used solely to extract underlying mechanisms, never product concepts), frontier deployment governance policies, and `StaffML` diagnostic scenarios (`origin: internal`). Provides **operational literary warrant** and (for `StaffML` tag counts across the `10,716`-question bank) an **assignment-frequency proxy**, not direct user search/request warrant (`Z39.19 §5.3.5.3`). |
| **`S5`** | **Standards, Assurance Guidance & Benchmark Specifications** | Standard clause / assurance guidance module / benchmark scenario specification | Published technical standards, public assurance guidance reports, and benchmark specifications (`NIST AI 100-1`, `NIST AI 600-1`, `ISO 26262`, `ISO 21448`, `ISO/IEC 42001`, `ISO/PAS 8800`, `UL 4600`, `OWASP Top 10 for LLM`, `MITRE ATLAS`, `MLPerf Inference/Training/Tiny`). Provides **normative literary warrant** (`Z39.19 §5.3.5.1`), not internal organizational preferred-term mandates (`Z39.19 §5.3.5.2`) unless term-form adoption is separately logged. |

### Cross-Stratum Scale Representation (`Method-C` §3.2, `Method-B` §3.2)
To prevent datacenter LLM serving from crowding out resource-constrained or classical ML systems, the frame requires representation across execution scales where applicable to an area:
- **Datacenter / Cloud Scale** (multi-node accelerator cluster serving, training, and fleet scheduling)
- **Enterprise / Single-Node / Workstation** (gateway/microservice orchestration, CPU/single-accelerator inference, retrieval and compound pipelines)
- **Edge / On-Device / Embedded (`TinyML`)** (MCU/NPU/mobile quantization, static arena memory planning, battery/thermal envelopes)

## 2. Global Provenance-Family Clustering (`provenance_family_id`)

Before stratification, split assignment, or warrant counting, every unit in `<campaign>/sampling-manifest.json` receives an explicit `unit_id`, `provenance_family_id`, `stratum` (`S1`–`S5`), `split` (`dev`, `held_out_test`, `reserve`), `origin` (`external` | `internal`), `editor_affiliated` (`true` | `false`), and `source_type` (`prose` | `heading`) (`Method-A` §2.1 & §3.3, `Method-B` §2.2 & §3.4):

1. **Shared Authorship / Lab Lineage (Deterministic `< 50%` Overlap & Transitive Closure)**: Let $A_i$ denote the normalized first and last (primary and senior) authors of external source $i$ (`authors` array in `sampling-manifest.json`). Two sources $i, j$ are linked when $|A_i \cap A_j| / \min(|A_i|, |A_j|) \ge 0.50$ (so distinct effective lineages have `< 50%` author overlap), and connected components (transitive closure, computed directly by `_count_effective_lineages` in `reconcile.py`) count as a single effective `provenance_family_id` across all strata and areas.
2. **Paper–System–Benchmark–Checklist Lineage & Prospective 1-Work-Per-Unit Rule**: A research paper, its companion open-source documentation, tutorials derived from it, and any checklist row summarizing that same work share one `provenance_family_id` (for example, Kwon et al. SOSP 2023 PagedAttention + vLLM architecture docs = one cluster; Huyen 2022 prose excerpts + `courses-and-standards-2026` `DMLS` topic rows = `pf_huyen_dmls`; `NIST AI RMF` prose + `courses-and-standards-2026` `NIST` row = `pf_nist_ai_rmf`). **Historical Phase 3 bundling vs. prospective rule:** in the executed Phase 3 `P4`+`Q4` curation (`preregistration.yaml`), four curated `Q4` prose units bundled paired foundational works (`Q4-S3-SIMPLEX-CBF-01` pairing Sha 2001 *Simplex* and Ames et al. 2019 *Control Barrier Functions*; `Q4-S3-NN-VERIFICATION-01` pairing Katz et al. CAV 2017 *Reluplex* and Liu et al. verification survey; `Q4-S3-RAND-DRIVING-01` pairing Kalra & Paddock RAND RR-1478 and Corso et al. *JAIR* 2021; `Q4-S5-ISO26262-21448-UL4600`), `Q4-S1-CONCRETE-SAFETY-01` placed Amodei et al. 2016 in `S1`, and `Q4-COV-courses-and-standards-2026-191` inherited `S2` from `courses-and-standards-2026.yml` before deduplicating into `pf_nist_ai_rmf` (`1` effective lineage either way). **Prospectively across all new area frames**, every manifest unit represents **at most `1` primary work** assigned to its intrinsic source stratum (`S1`–`S5`) rather than the host checklist file's stratum.
3. **Project-Internal Corpora (`pf_internal_mlsysbook`) & Editor-Affiliated Consortium Exclusion (`editor_affiliated: true`)**:
   - **Prospective single internal lineage (`pf_internal_mlsysbook`, `origin: internal`, `split: dev`)**: Prospectively across all refresh campaigns, all project-internal sources (`MLSysBook` chapters, `Harvard CS249r` course materials, `StaffML` diagnostic banks, and single-lab papers where the taxonomy Editor is a primary or senior author) collapse into a **single internal lineage `pf_internal_mlsysbook`** (`origin: internal`, restricted exclusively to `split: dev`). Any number of `origin: internal` units supplies `0` external lineages under Gate 2 and cannot satisfy Gate 5's external verbatim-quote check.
   - **Editor-affiliated multi-institutional standards (`editor_affiliated: true`)**: Multi-institutional consortium benchmark or standard specifications where the taxonomy Editor is a primary/lead author (`pf_mlperf_inference` [`P4-S5-MLPERF-INF-01`, Reddi et al. ISCA 2020]) remain `S5` normative specifications (`origin: external`) for domain context, but carry **`editor_affiliated: true`** in `sampling-manifest.json` and `taxonomy/method-study/burned-clusters.json`. `reconcile.py` excludes `editor_affiliated: true` clusters from satisfying Gate 2's independent external-lineage floor and Gate 5's external verbatim-attestation floor (`MLPerf` itself also remains excluded from concepts and aliases under Gate 3).
   - **Historical Phase 3 internal sub-unit IDs & StaffML holdout trace**: In the executed Phase 3 `P4`+`Q4` study (`preregistration.yaml`), internal units carried per-chapter (`pf_mlsysbook_<vol>/<chapter>`) and per-sample (`pf_staffml_dev_*`, `pf_staffml_heldout_*`) IDs with `0` `provenance_family_id` clusters crossing splits, and were **excluded from external generalization claims**:
     1. All `7` held-out `MLSysBook` chapter units were excluded from Phase 6 coverage (`COV` evaluated only `79` `origin: external` `S2`/`S3`/`S5` units across `10` raw clusters / `9` deduplicated external lineages after merging `pf_cov_courses_2026_NIST -> pf_nist_ai_rmf`).
     2. Direct audit of the `40` `S4` `StaffML` `held_out_test` indexing units (`38` in `taxonomy/agreement/staffml-300-heldout-2026-10.yml` + `2` in `staffml-300-2026-10.yml` [`mlops-167`, `safety-003`], originally tagged at `0.2-draft` [`714`/`727` concepts]) against `staffml/vault/data/taxonomy-tagging-2026-10.yaml` confirms that **`0 / 40` (`0/38` + `0/2`) appeared in `proposed_concepts`, `subject_only_questions`, or `adjudication`**, while **`13 / 40` (`12/38` + `1/2`) appeared in `review_queue`** of the Oct 2026 whole-bank (`10,716`-question) tagging pass. Accordingly, those `38` items are reported solely as a **tagging-rule calibration holdout** (`tagging_rule_holdout_38_staffml_2026_10` in `study-receipts-summary.json`), whereas **only external 2026 `systems-venues-2026` units were unexposed to `0.4-draft` construction**. In post-Phase-6 campaigns (`bucket-a-campaign/sampling-manifest.json`) and prospective refreshes, all `MLSysBook`, `CS249r`, and `StaffML` units reside in `dev`.

**Mechanical enforcement (`reconcile.py` `schema_version: 4`, `stage_reviewer_workspace.py`, & `burned-clusters.json`)**:
- `reconcile.py` requires `<campaign>/sampling-manifest.json` whenever `concepts_add` or **any** `add_aliases` / `aliases` (`synonym`, `quasi-synonym`, `acronym`, `upward-posting`) appear; rejects duplicate JSON keys or `unit_id`s; enforces `origin == "internal" => split == "dev"`, `burned-clusters.json`, `editor_affiliated` exclusion, `< 50%` author-overlap closure (`_count_effective_lineages`), and exact `verbatim_quote` substring verification against cited non-editor-affiliated external `dev` unit passages. `stage_reviewer_workspace.py` stages an external `/tmp` read-only workspace across `review`, `crosscheck`, `audit`, and `guides` modes containing only `held_out: false` checklists, `split == "dev"` manifest units/chapters, and Markdown docs with non-`dev` cluster/unit lines redacted.

## 3. Split Allocation Protocol and Post-Phase-6 Cluster-Burn State

1. **Prospective Refresh Targets (`60%` / `25%` / `15%`)**:
   - In periodic refreshes (`.claude/workflows/taxonomy-refresh.md`, `taxonomy/METHOD.md`), global external `provenance_family_id` clusters are assigned once before construction with target cluster proportions of **`60%` `dev`** (visible to builders alongside internal `dev` corpora), **`25%` `held_out_test`** (sealed external clusters for Step 5b evaluation), and **`15%` `reserve`** (sequestered external clusters for replenishing burned `held_out_test` slices alongside fresh post-cutoff external sources). Multi-area clusters are assigned as whole clusters prior to per-area tallies.
2. **Executed Phase 3 Allocation in the Two-Area Study (`preregistration.yaml`, `seed = 20261103`)**:
   - In the completed Phase 3 `P4`+`Q4` study (`382` units across `97` pre-deduplication `provenance_family_id` clusters [`95` deduplicated clusters after merging `pf_cov_courses_2026_DMLS -> pf_huyen_dmls` and `pf_cov_courses_2026_NIST -> pf_nist_ai_rmf`]; `0` pre-deduplication cross-split clusters), `build_phase3_preregistration.py` combined pre-designated benchmark splits (`fixed_split`) with a deterministic cell-wise draw (`seed = 20261103`) over movable `(area, stratum)` cells of size $n$ (`1` cluster to `held_out_test` when $n \ge 3$, `1` cluster to `reserve` when $n \ge 4$, and the remaining $n - \mathbb{I}(n \ge 3) - \mathbb{I}(n \ge 4)$ clusters to `dev`).
   - Because single-passage curated sources each formed a 1-unit `dev` cluster while `held_out_test` and `reserve` held larger venue/question-bank clusters, the **achieved Phase 3 pre-deduplication cluster split was `76 / 97` (`78.4%`) `dev`, `15 / 97` (`15.5%`) `held_out_test`, and `6 / 97` (`6.2%`) `reserve`**, and the **achieved unit split was `200 / 382` (`52.4%`) `dev`, `126 / 382` (`33.0%`) `held_out_test`, and `56 / 382` (`14.7%`) `reserve`**:
     - **Pre-deduplication clusters by split (`97` total in Phase 3)**: `76` `dev` (`47` `P4`-only, `23` `Q4`-only, `6` `P4`/`Q4`-shared; `45` `external`, `31` `internal`), `15` `held_out_test` (`8` `P4`-only, `6` `Q4`-only, `1` `P4`/`Q4`-shared; `10` `external` [`9` deduplicated external lineages], `5` `internal`), and `6` `reserve` (`3` `P4`-only, `3` `Q4`-only; `4` `external`, `2` `internal`).
     - **Units by split, area, and origin (`382` total in Phase 3)**: `dev` = `200` (`142` `P4` [`95` `external`, `47` `internal`], `58` `Q4` [`14` `external`, `44` `internal`]); `held_out_test` = `126` (`116` `P4` [`75` `external`, `41` `internal`], `10` `Q4` [`4` `external`, `6` `internal`]); `reserve` = `56` (`47` `P4` [`46` `external`, `1` `internal`], `9` `Q4` [`2` `external`, `7` `internal`]).
3. **Post-Phase-6 Global Cluster-Burn State (`taxonomy/method-study/burned-clusters.json` & `bucket-a-campaign/sampling-manifest.json`)**:
   - Inspecting the `15` Phase 6 `held_out_test` clusters (`126` units) during the `2026-11-02` comparison burned them globally, including **all `4` venue clusters (`osdi2026`, `nsdi2026`, `eurosys2026`, `asplos2026`) of `taxonomy/coverage/systems-venues-2026.yml`** (now marked `held_out: false` across the entire repository).
   - Furthermore, while `MLSys 2026` (`pf_cov_mlsys_25_26_mlsys2026`, `45` units from `mlsys-papers-2025-2026.yml`), `pf_cov_courses_2026_USC` (`1` unit from `courses-and-standards-2026.yml`), and `8` `MLSysBook` chapter units were unexposed to the blind `Vocab-X` rebuild during Phase 6, they came from `held_out: false` checklists or internal `MLSysBook` chapters already exposed to `0.4-draft` (`Vocab-Y`).
   - Reconciling work-level duplicate cluster IDs (`pf_cov_courses_2026_DMLS -> pf_huyen_dmls` and `pf_cov_courses_2026_NIST -> pf_nist_ai_rmf`) across the **exact `382` pre-registered Phase 3 units** (with zero post-registration unit additions) and recording all exposed/burned clusters in the append-only ledger `taxonomy/method-study/burned-clusters.json` yields **`382` units across `95` deduplicated clusters in `bucket-a-campaign/sampling-manifest.json`: `380` `dev` units (`93` deduplicated clusters), `0` `held_out_test` units, and `2` unexposed external `reserve` units (`pf_ut_cs378` [`P4-S2-UT378-01`] and `pf_iso42001_pas8800` [`Q4-S5-ISO42001-PAS8800`])**. Replenishing `held_out_test` for the next refresh therefore requires fresh post-cutoff external clusters (such as `MLSys 2027` and `2027` systems venues), never `mlsys-papers-2025-2026.yml` or `systems-venues-2026.yml`.
