## 2. Concept Admission, Warrant, and Boundary Rules

**Source provenance**: Synthesized from `Method-B` §2 (`D2` cross-family mean `4.50/5.0`, all-judge `4.333/5.0`) and `Method-A` §2 (`D2` cross-family mean `4.50/5.0`, all-judge `4.667/5.0`).

### 2.1 Multi-Lineage Warrant and Heading-Only Ban (Enacted Gate 2)
Following ANSI/NISO Z39.19-2005 §5.3.5 (and conceptually aligned with ISO 25964-1 concept warrant):
1. **Two-Lineage `dev` Warrant Rule (`0` single-source exceptions)**: Every new concept in `concepts_add` requires structured `warrant_sources` from **at least two distinct `provenance_family_id` clusters** in the `dev` split across `S1`–`S5`, including **at least one non-editor-affiliated `origin: external` lineage** (`editor_affiliated: false`; non-`MLSysBook`, non-`Harvard CS249r`, non-`StaffML`) and **at least one `source_type: prose` unit** containing explanatory text defining the mechanism (`reconcile.py`). While `Method-B` §2.1 explored a single-source curriculum/standard exception, the enacted protocol (`METHOD.md` Gate 2) allows **zero single-source concept admissions**: single-lineage mechanisms are recorded in `gaps:` until a second independent lineage appears.
2. **Authorship, Institutional Organization, & Global `work_id` Clustering**: Two sources merge into a single effective `provenance_family_id` lineage (`compute_lineage_components` in `binder/cli/core/taxonomy_schema.py`, enforcing a global `1 work_id <-> 1 canonical provenance_family_id` bijection) when:
   - Their listed personal author sets overlap by $\ge 50\%$ (`|A_i \cap A_j| / \min(|A_i|, |A_j|) \ge 0.50` across all listed authors) or their `institutional_organizations` share a technical committee or standards body (`burned-clusters.json` `schema_version: 3`, transitive closure shared across `reconcile.py`, `apply.py`, `taxonomy_schema.py`, and `build_study_receipts.py`; `Method-A` §2.1, `[P]`),
   - One work is a companion repository, documentation site, lecture adaptation, or tutorial authored by the creators of the other, or
   - Both belong to project-internal corpora (prospectively collapsed into a single internal lineage `pf_internal_mlsysbook` with `origin: internal` and `split: dev`, alongside historical Phase 3 per-chapter/per-sample `pf_mlsysbook_*` / `pf_harvard_cs249r_*` / `pf_staffml_*` IDs; multi-institutional consortium standards led by the Editor such as `pf_mlperf_inference` carry `editor_affiliated: true` and cannot satisfy the external-lineage independence floor).
3. **Heading-Only Warrant Ban & Contextual Discovery Exclusion**: A syllabus or venue-checklist topic title (`source_type: heading`) may serve as the corroborating second lineage when paired with a `source_type: prose` source that defines the mechanism, but bare headings alone (`0` prose sources) cannot mint a concept or justify extrapolated mechanisms, and contextual discovery leads (`held_out: false` checklists and `taxonomy-tagging-dev-leads.yaml`) never satisfy Gate 2 `warrant_sources` or Gate 5 `checked_dev_units`.

### 2.2 Named Entity, Product, Benchmark, and SKU Exclusion Gate (Enacted Gate 3)
Commercial products, multi-mechanism serving engines/frameworks, hardware SKUs, model releases, and benchmark suite families (`vLLM`, `TensorRT-LLM`, `SGLang`, `DeepSpeed`, `PyTorch`, `H100`, `B200`, `MI300X`, `MLPerf` [maintained by MLCommons], `benchmark suite`) are **excluded as concepts** (project scope policy inspired by `Z39.19 §6.3.3` and `§8.2`; `Method-A` §2.2, `Method-B` §2.4):
- **Mechanism extraction**: Extract the underlying architectural mechanism (`vLLM` $\rightarrow$ `mlsys.paged-kv` and `mlsys.continuous-batching`; `ZeRO` $\rightarrow$ `mlsys.state-sharding`).
- **Alias restriction**: Only a named algorithm or single-mechanism implementation that is 1-to-1 synonymous with one mechanism and independently satisfies Gate 5's equivalence, homograph, and `primary_excerpt` attestation rules (`primary_source_excerpt_path` under `<campaign>/sources/`) may be added as a prospective `alias` (all `7` pre-sidecar `0.4-draft` concepts / `8` legacy aliases lacking `primary_excerpt` sidecar attestation — `assurance case` on `mlsys.safety-case`, `XGBoost` on `mlsys.tree-ensemble`, `grammar-guided decoding` on `mlsys.constrained-decoding`, `ZeRO` and `FSDP` on `mlsys.state-sharding`, `PagedAttention` on `mlsys.paged-kv`, `CUDA Graphs` on `mlsys.graph-replay`, and `CUDA streams` on `mlsys.device-streams` — carry status `pre-sidecar-legacy-queued-for-v1-audit` in `grandfathered_draft_alias_exceptions` in `term-provenance.yml`). Multi-mechanism frameworks and benchmark suite families (`vLLM`, `TensorRT-LLM`, `SGLang`, `MLPerf`, `benchmark suite`) are excluded from both concepts and aliases (`MULTI_MECHANISM_SUITE_ALIASES` in `reconcile.py`).
- **No `BTI` (`instance-of`) relation**: Individual named standards or open interchange formats enter only as general architectural mechanisms when warranted across $\ge 2$ lineages; `BTI` is excluded from the relation schema.

### 2.3 Operational 4-Part Concept-vs-Alias Witness & `Z39.19 §8.2` Equivalence Gate (Enacted Gate 5)
To decide whether candidate $C$ is a separate `Concept`, a typed `alias` on existing concept $P$, or a `gaps:` entry (`Method-A` §2.3, `Method-B` §1 Step 10 & §2.5, enacted in `METHOD.md` Gate 5):
1. **Criterion (a) — Distinct System Invariant / Envelope**: Does $C$ introduce a distinct system invariant, resource envelope, failure boundary, analytical causal model, or measurement semantics not already fixed by $P$?
2. **Criterion (b) — Independent Decision Consequence / Trade-Off**: Does $C$ carry an independent failure mode, trade-off, or engineering decision consequence not identical to $P$?
3. **Criterion (c) — Taxonomic / Causal Profile**: Is $C$ either a valid `all-and-some` (`specializes`) child of $P$ with its own differentiating properties or an endpoint of a causal/associative edge not inherited from $P$?
4. **Criterion (d) — Structured Differential Indexing Witness & Two-Lineage Warrant**: Can the proposer supply a 6-key `witness_justification` (`compared_concept`, `criterion_a`/`b`/`c`, and distinct `scenario_a` and `scenario_b`) **and** $\ge 2$-lineage `dev` warrant (`Gate 2`)?

**Enacted three-way decision & tiebreak rule**:
- **Separate `Concept`**: Admitted when Criteria (a)–(d) and Gate 2 ($\ge 2$ `dev` lineages) all hold.
- **Unwarranted distinct mechanism $\rightarrow$ `gaps:` (never `aliases`)**: When a candidate denotes a substantively distinct mechanism or methodology under Criteria (a)–(c) (for example, `adaptive stress testing`) **but lacks two-lineage `dev` warrant**, it routes to `gaps:`, **never** to `aliases`. Likewise, constituent execution phases (`prefill phase` vs. `decode phase`), distinct analytical methodologies (`FMEA`, `FTA`, `STPA` under `Hazard analysis`), planning artifacts (`preregistered analysis plan`), and method-produced artifacts (`unsafe control action` under `STPA`) are never aliased onto a parent.
- **Typed `alias` (`synonym`, `quasi-synonym`, `acronym`, or `< 2`-lineage `upward-posting`)**: Permitted only when `<campaign>/sampling-manifest.json` is verified, the surface term is word-bounded attested as an exact `verbatim_quote` substring in a cited non-editor-affiliated external `dev` unit with `source_type: prose` and `passage_kind: primary_excerpt` (with `primary_source_excerpt_path` under `<campaign>/sources/` verified by SHA-256 on prospective units, never a `curated_summary` digest — which led to withdrawing candidate `grammar-constrained decoding` after checking `arXiv:2312.07104`), disambiguated against cross-area homographs (`Z39.19 §5.3.1, §6.2.1`), not globally tombstoned across other concepts (`removed_aliases`), and either denotes the same mechanism (`synonym` / `acronym` / `Z39.19 §8.2.3` `quasi-synonym`) or is a valid `all-and-some` narrower variant (`Z39.19 §8.2.4` `upward-posting`) with **`dev_lineages_requiring_separation` $\in \{0, 1\}$ (`< 2` `dev` lineages)**. All four alias types require `source` ($\ge 10$ chars), manifest-verified `checked_dev_units`, `scope_note` ($\ge 15$ chars), and word-bounded primary-excerpt `verbatim_quote` in `reconcile.py`.

### 2.4 Compound Term Factoring vs. Pre-Coordination (Enacted Gate 4, `Z39.19 §7`)
A multi-word compound term $C$ is admitted as a pre-coordinated concept only when (`Method-A` §2.3, `Method-B` §2.5):
1. It names a lexicalized algorithm, protocol, or system mechanism with non-compositional behavior or its own failure mode/trade-off (`speculative decoding`, `continuous batching`, `activation checkpointing`), **and**
2. It is institutionalized as a unit across **$\ge 2$ distinct `provenance_family_id` lineages** in `dev` (`[P]`, aligned with Gate 2 in `METHOD.md`; `Method-A` originally proposed $\ge 3$ before calibration to $\ge 2`).

Generic **`metric × workload`** combinations (`inference throughput`, `inference energy cost`, `training power`) are factored into post-coordination rather than admitted, except for the explicit concept-scoped stage-specific structural metric allowlist (`STAGE_METRIC_ALLOWED_TERMS` in `reconcile.py`: `SLO goodput` on `mlsys.slo-goodput`, `TTFT` / `time to first token` on `mlsys.ttft`, `TPOT` / `time per output token` on `mlsys.tpot`, `MFU` / `model FLOPs utilization` on `mlsys.model-flops-utilization`, `training goodput` / `ML goodput` on `mlsys.training-goodput`, `request throughput` / `QPS` / `queries per second` / `tokens per second` on `mlsys.request-throughput`, `latency percentiles` / `p50` / `p99` / `percentile latency` on `mlsys.latency-percentiles`, `ITL` / `inter-token latency` on `mlsys.inter-token-latency`, `MBU` / `model bandwidth utilization` on `mlsys.model-bandwidth-utilization`).

## 4. Structural Invariants, Relationships, and Lifecycle Governance

**Source provenance**: Synthesized from `Method-B` §4 (`D4` cross-family mean `4.50/5.0`, all-judge `4.667/5.0`) and `Method-A` §4 (`D4` cross-family mean `4.25/5.0`, all-judge `4.50/5.0`).

### 4.1 Polyhierarchy with Single Primary Home (`home`)
- Every concept declares exactly one primary subject row (`home`), forming a clean partition across `101` subjects in `20` areas (`Method-A` §4.1, `Method-B` §4.2; post-move `placement_examples` home consistency enforced by `reconcile.py`). Cross-area relevance uses `secondary_concepts` with an explicit `rationale`.
- Concept-to-concept `specializes` (`rdfs:subPropertyOf skos:broader`) forms a cycle-free DAG verified by the 3-step **generic (`BTG`) `all-and-some` test** (`Z39.19 §8.3.1`). Because ML system components participate in multiple assemblies, `part-of` is modeled as a cycle-checked associative relation exported under `skos:related` (`Z39.19 §8.3.3.2`), and `BTI` is not used.

### 4.2 Controlled 17-Verb Relationships & Extension-Preserving Definitions
- Relationships use the closed 17-verb LinkML vocabulary (`specializes` + 16 associative/causal/evaluation/composition verbs under `skos:related`), disjoint from `specializes` transitive closure (`SKOS S27` / `Z39.19 §8.4`), with mandatory `predicate` and `conditions` strings verified in both forward and inverse literal readings; every active concept must maintain post-patch incident degree $\ge 2$ (`reconcile.py`).
- In-place `concepts_update.definition` edits require **`definition_change_kind: editorial-clarification`**, an explicit `edge_impact_note`, exact `checked_incident_relationships` matching all incident relationship IDs, and $\ge 2$-family `extension_unchanged: true` cross-check verification (`reconcile.py` & `term-provenance.yml`) verifying that every incident relationship remains literally true without shifting extension; extension-shifting changes require `concepts_retire` + `concepts_add`.

### 4.3 Non-Circular Genus–Differentia Definitions & Compiler Sidecar Gates
- Every concept `definition` is a single non-circular genus-differentia sentence stating what kind of mechanism, structure, failure mode, or metric it is and what it changes in the system (`Z39.19 §6.2.2`; post-patch `(effective_label, effective_defn)` circularity enforced by `reconcile.py`).
- `./binder/binder concepts check` enforces LinkML schema conformance, monotonic SemVer ordering (supporting `0.x-draft`, `1.x-draft` maintenance branches, `1.0-rcN`, and `1.x.y`), `SKOS S14` (`prefLabel` cardinality) plus global case-folded preferred/entry-term uniqueness across active and retired concepts (`Z39.19 §5.3.1`), `SKOS S13` (`prefLabel`/`altLabel` disjointness), hierarchical DAG acyclicity, polarity consistency, and **`validate_term_provenance_sidecar`** (`schema_version == 2`, deterministic replay of both taxonomy state and top-level provenance metadata via `replay_provenance_step` from `taxonomy/method-study/genesis-0.4-baseline.json.gz` across every `applied_campaigns` entry across `apply.py` [including versioned `guides_replace`] and `-draft`-only `guides_apply.py` [which forbids `version` changes completely], `final_yaml_canonical_json_sha256` binding, `taxonomy_sha256`, `non_guides_taxonomy_sha256`, `sidecar_content_sha256` self-digest, global removed-alias tombstones, permanent retired-string reservation to `replaced_by`, governed `attest_existing_aliases` backfill, and raw-bundle `verify_release_governance_receipt` on non-draft/`1.0` releases — detecting un-reconciled YAML or provenance tampering in-repo, paired with git history and CI diff review against `origin/dev` for validator code integrity).

### 4.4 Permanent Identities, Zero-Extension-Shift Parent Rule, and Retirement Schema
Concept IDs (`mlsys.*`) are permanent and never reused (`Method-A` §4.6, `Method-B` §4.7–4.8, `METHOD.md`, `.claude/rules/taxonomy/content.md`):
- **Label refinement (unchanged extension)**: Update `label` and retain the former label in `aliases` (with `alias_metadata`).
- **Zero-extension-shift parent ID vs. concept split / narrowing rule**:
  - *Adding a `specializes` child while preserving the parent's extension (`0%` extension shift on parent)*: When a narrower sub-mechanism is minted as a new `specializes` child while the broader parent's own extension and every incident relationship remain unchanged (`definition_change_kind: editorial-clarification`), keep the existing `mlsys.*` ID on the broader parent, mint a new `mlsys.*` ID for the narrower child via `concepts_add`, and re-review downstream `StaffML` and `mlsysbook-alignment.yml` tags to migrate items specifically teaching the narrower child.
  - *Any narrowing, broadening, or splitting of an existing concept's extension*: Whenever a concept's own extension narrows, broadens, or splits into peer mechanisms (so existing references to the legacy ID no longer denote the same extension; `Method-A` §4.6 had explored an `< 20%` tolerance, which the enacted protocol tightened to **zero extension shift** in `METHOD.md` Gate 4 and §4.2 above), **retire the legacy `mlsys.*` ID** (`concepts_retire`, optionally setting `migrate_hierarchy_and_views: true` and pointing `replaced_by` to the primary redirect in `0.x-draft`; former labels and aliases remain permanently reserved to that `replaced_by` target), mint new `mlsys.*` IDs via `concepts_add`, and retag all downstream `StaffML` and `mlsysbook-alignment.yml` consumers before closing the campaign.
- **Enacted `0.4-draft` `RetiredConcept` vs. prospective `1.0` fields**: In `0.4-draft` (`mlsys-taxonomy.linkml.yml` and `apply.py`), `RetiredConcept` persists `id`, former `label`, former `aliases`, transitive-resolved active `replaced_by`, `version`, and `reason`. Adding machine-typed `resolution_class` (`auto`, `broaden`, `review`, `split`) and multivalued `split_into` slots is recorded as a **prospective `1.0` LinkML schema extension** for external board review (`candidate-experts.md`).

## 5. Evaluation Protocol, Empirical Calibration Findings, and Anti-Gaming Controls

**Source provenance**: Synthesized from `Method-B` §5–6 (`D5` cross-family mean `4.75/5.0` [all-judge `4.667`], `D6` `4.75/5.0` [`4.667`], `D7` `4.75/5.0` [`4.667`]) and `Method-A` §5–6 (`D5` `4.00/5.0` [`4.167`], `D6` `4.25/5.0` [`4.333`], `D7` `4.00/5.0` [`4.167`]).

### 5.1 Two-Pass Blind Coverage, Expressibility vs. Exact Estimators, & Seeded-Gap Limitation
To test vocabulary completeness on unexposed `origin: external` `held_out_test` clusters (`Method-A` §5.6, `Method-B` §5.3):
- **Pass 1 (Vocabulary-Blind Topic Extraction)**: Extract central mechanisms from external held-out units in an isolated directory with no access to the taxonomy.
- **Pass 2 (Two-Family Expressibility Scoring — Two Distinct Consensus Estimators)**: Two distinct model families score each unit (`Exact = 1.0` single concept/alias, `Post-coord = 0.5` conjunction of `2`–`3` concepts without specificity loss, `Missing = 0.0`), co-reporting **three** distinct binary metrics plus `10,000`-resample deduplicated-lineage cluster-bootstrap 95% CIs:
  1. **Consensus expressibility (`both judges >= 0.5`, i.e., `Exact` or `Post-coord` in both judges)**: `36.7%` (`29/79`) on `Vocab-Y` vs. `10.1%` (`8/79`) on `Vocab-X` in Phase 6 (`diff = -26.6` pp; `9`-lineage deduplicated cluster-bootstrap 95% CI `[-45.1, -6.8]` pp, `10`-raw-cluster CI `[-44.9, -6.7]` pp; `36.8%` [`28/76`] vs. `10.5%` [`8/76`] on `dev_disjoint_76_external`; `38.4%` [`28/73`] vs. `11.0%` [`8/73`] on `unexposed_73_systems_venues_2026`). **Headline Step 5b non-regression gate**: paired cluster-bootstrap 95% lower bound `>= -5.0` pp on shared unexposed external units (`[P]`).
  2. **Consensus exact single-concept coverage (`both judges == 1.0`)**: `19.0%` (`15/79`) on `Vocab-Y` vs. `2.5%` (`2/79`) on `Vocab-X` in Phase 6 (`diff = -16.5` pp across all `79` units; `19.2%` [`14/73`] vs. `2.7%` [`2/73`], `diff = -16.4` pp from `2/73 - 14/73 = -16.44` pp on `unexposed_73_systems_venues_2026`; co-reported alongside consensus expressibility so post-coordination reliance is transparent).
  3. **Any-judge expressibility (`either judge >= 0.5`)**: `72.2%` (`57/79`) on `Vocab-Y` vs. `36.7%` (`29/79`) on `Vocab-X`, paired with binary coverage judge $\kappa$ (`0.366` vs. `0.325`).
- **Seeded-Gap Sensitivity Audit & Empirical Phase 6 Limitation (`Method-A` §5.6)**: `Method-A` proposed removing $k$ concepts (`k = 10` in Phase 6) and requiring Pass 2 to degrade $\ge 85\%$ (`[P]`) of baseline-covered judgments citing those concepts. **Empirical Phase 6 result (`study-receipts-summary.json`):** unrestricted LLM judges degraded only **`24 / 40 = 60.0%`** of affected judgments on `Vocab-Y` and **`16 / 29 = 55.2%`** on `Vocab-X` — **falling short of the $\ge 85\%$ target** because judges substituted broader parent/neighbor concepts or `0.5` post-coordination. Consequently, `taxonomy-refresh.md` Step 5b reports seeded-gap sensitivity as a mandatory diagnostic audit during `0.x-draft` until negative-example prompt calibration achieves $\ge 85.0\%$ sensitivity prior to `1.0`.

### 5.2 Multi-Rater Indexing Agreement + Specificity Counter-Metrics
On held-out scenarios indexed independently by two blind taggers (`1` primary subject, `0`–`3` concepts):
1. **Primary subject agreement**: Cohen's $\kappa$ on single-pair LLM runs (`release floor >= 0.70`), and both orientation-invariant Krippendorff's nominal $\alpha_{\text{subject}} \ge 0.65$ and pair-stratified $\bar{\kappa}_{\text{subject}} \ge 0.65$ on the 6-pair (`12`-human) pre-`1.0` study.
2. **Concept-set consistency**: Both conditional (`both non-empty`) and all-item ($J(\emptyset,\emptyset)=1$, $J(A,\emptyset)=0$) **Hooper (1965)** and **Rolling (1981)** overlap (`LLM floors >= 40% / >= 57%` conditional, `>= 35% / >= 50%` all-item), paired with empty-assignment rate ($\le 20\%$).
3. **Chance-corrected set agreement**: **Krippendorff's $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$** using Passonneau's (2006) MASI distance $d_{\text{MASI}}(A,B) = 1 - J(A,B)\cdot M(A,B)$ (`LLM floor >= 0.40`).
4. **Anti-Gaming Specificity Counter-Metrics (`Method-B` §6.7, `Method-C` §6.4)**:
   - **Leaf-concept specificity ratio**: Share of assigned concept tags whose concept has `0` incoming `specializes` children (in Phase 6 `IDX`, **`Vocab-X` scored higher than `Vocab-Y`: `86.1%` [`186/216`] vs. `80.9%` [`148/183`]**).
   - **Max single-concept share ($p_{\max}$)**: $\le 15.0\%$ (**`Vocab-X` achieved lower concentration than `Vocab-Y`: `6.94%` vs. `9.84%`**).
   - **Effective vocabulary utilization**: Distinct concepts assigned divided by active area concepts (`Vocab-X` = `55 / 111 = 49.5%` vs. `Vocab-Y` = `44 / 125 = 35.2%`).
