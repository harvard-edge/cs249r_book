# External Editorial Board Charter and Human Evaluation Protocol (Z39.19 §11.1.3.1, §11.2.2, §11.3.1.1)

Status: public governance charter and role specification for version `0.4-draft` (`2026-11-02`). **This document defines seat qualifications, area coverage obligations, appointment and conflict-of-interest rules, and pre-registered human evaluation specifications only; no external board has yet been convened, no individual has been contacted or asked to endorse the ML Systems Taxonomy, and unconsented personal names are intentionally excluded from the public repository.**

---

## 1. Purpose and Historical Context

Under **ANSI/NISO Z39.19-2005 (R2010) §11.1.3.1** (*Committee Approach*), **§11.2.2** (*Evaluation Criteria*), and **§11.3.1.1** (*Addition of Terms*), a controlled vocabulary intended for community reuse combines empirical corpus extraction with review by domain subject specialists and controlled-vocabulary specialists.

In `0.4-draft`, construction and verification used language-model passes under deterministic reconciliation and LinkML compilation gates, with final decisions made by a single human Editor (Vijay Janapa Reddi): specifically, the `0.4-draft` refresh ran Round 1 as seven parallel lenses in one model family (`claude-fable-5-1`) followed by a three-family Round 2 cross-check and independent audit (`gpt-6.1-sol`, `gemini-4-argon-high`, `claude-opus-5-5`), while three-family blind discovery and reconciliation were evaluated on the pre-registered Phase 3–7 two-area study (`P4` and `Q4`) and codified for future refreshes.

To transition from an `editorial draft` (`0.x-draft`) to a citable community release (`1.0`), the project requires review by a **7-seat external editorial board** backed by mandatory sub-area specialist reviewers covering all `20` taxonomy areas (`101` subjects, `878` concepts) and a **fixed 12-indexer (`6`-pair) dual-human inter-indexer consistency study**.

## 3. Appointment Independence, Recusal, Quorum, and Binding Board-Rejection Exclusion Rule

1. **Independent appointment chain, non-Editor administrative convenor, and standing-roster vs. active-voting co-authorship caps**:
   - **Deterministic initial convenor selection, ex-officio nominating panel, & open-nomination fallback**: Outreach to form the ex-officio nominating panel is administered by an **independent non-Editor administrative convenor** selected deterministically — without Editor discretion — by inviting, in reverse-chronological order of most recently completed chair or co-chair term across the fixed standards-committee precedence sequence `(1) NISO Z39.19 / Information Discovery & Interchange Topic Committee -> (2) ISO/TC 46/SC 9/WG 8 (ISO 25964) -> (3) W3C SKOS Working Group -> (4) DCMI Usage Board` (with any same-date term-completion tie broken by ascending `SHA-256(NIST Randomness Beacon pulse at outreach announcement || candidate ORCID/URI)`), the first willing scholar from an institution disjoint from the Editor's institution who has zero co-authorship or joint grant/consortium leadership with the Editor in the trailing 5 years (**neither the Editor nor any member of the Editor's lab or institution plays any role in selecting, ranking, contacting, or filtering the convenor or the nominating panel**; if all four historical rosters are exhausted after `21` calendar days, a public `14`-day call for active `ISKO` / `ASIS&T` SIG/CR officers meeting the identical independence criteria is resolved by the same NIST Randomness Beacon hash tiebreak). **Mandatory pre-outreach publication**: the administrative convenor's signed COI declaration, eligibility check, and selection trace must be committed to the public governance archive **before** any nominating-panel invitation is sent. Before any board invitations issue, that convenor forms an independent 3-scholar nominating panel ex officio by inviting, in deterministic reverse-chronological order of service, the most recent willing program or general chairs of (i) `MLSys` (fallback: `EuroSys` / `NSDI`), (ii) `OSDI` or `SOSP` (fallback: `ASPLOS` / `ISCA`), and (iii) an active `NISO` / `DCMI` / `W3C` knowledge-organization or SKOS working group (fallback: `ISKO` / `ASIS&T` SIG/CR chairs), each a distinct natural person disjoint from the convenor, from a distinct institution disjoint from the Editor's institution, and having zero co-authorship or joint grant/consortium leadership with the Editor in the trailing 5 years. **Deterministic open-nomination fallback**: if any of the `3` nominating seats remains unfilled after `30` calendar days across both primary and fallback venue lists, the administrative convenor opens a public `21`-day community/self-nomination call for scholars meeting the seat's domain and independence criteria and selects among verified-eligible nominees via a pre-announced public randomness beacon (`SHA-256` of the first NIST Randomness Beacon pulse after the `21`-day window closes). That 3-scholar nominating panel appoints by $\ge 2/3$ vote an **external academic steering advisor** (tenured faculty member or standards-body working-group chair disjoint from the convenor and nominating panel, from an institution disjoint from the Editor's institution, with zero co-authorship or joint grant/consortium leadership with the Editor in the trailing 5 years).
   - **Independent `V1`/`V2` and domain Chair nomination, candidate-specific recusal, & appointing-committee vacancy backfill**: Every appointing voter (including the steering advisor, `V1`/`V2` Chairs and Alternates, and any nominating-panel substitute) is bound by the **identical COI disclosure and candidate-specific recusal rules** in §3.2 (and may never vote on their own appointment or on a candidate from their own institution or trailing-5-year co-author network). Whenever the steering advisor is conflicted or unavailable on a candidate appointment or ad-hoc voter selection, an unconflicted member of the 3-scholar ex-officio nominating panel (selected in deterministic seat order `(iii) -> (i) -> (ii)`) substitutes for the steering advisor. The external academic steering advisor (or unconflicted nominating-panel substitute, confirmed by at least `2` unconflicted nominating-panel members if `V1`/`V2` are not yet seated) nominates and seats `V1` and `V2` Chairs and Alternates (all `4` must be distinct natural persons disjoint from the convenor and nominating panel, from distinct non-Editor institutions, with zero co-authorship with the Editor in the trailing 5 years; the Editor may surface scope context in writing but holds no appointment or veto power). Once seated, the 3-voter appointing committee **`{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}`** appoints all domain Chairs, `T1`/`T2` Co-Chairs, Alternates, the `13` mandatory sub-area specialists, any ad-hoc escalation voters, and the `12` human indexers by a $\ge 2/3$ vote (`>= 2` of `3` eligible unconflicted voters concurring). **Deterministic appointing-committee vacancy backfill & 3-voter halt floor**: if `V1 Chair` is conflicted or vacant on a candidate, `V1 Alternate` substitutes; if **both** `V1 Chair` and `V1 Alternate` are conflicted, replacement proceeds in deterministic order `(a) unconflicted V2 Alternate (if not already serving on the 3-voter committee) -> (b) unconflicted member of the reconvened 3-scholar ex-officio nominating panel in seat order (iii) -> (i) -> (ii) not already substituting on that vote` (symmetric order if both `V2 Chair` and `V2 Alternate` are conflicted: `unconflicted V1 Alternate -> unconflicted nominating-panel member in order (iii) -> (i) -> (ii)`). **Mandatory 3-voter appointment floor**: every appointment vote requires **at least `3` eligible unconflicted distinct-person voters**; if fewer than `3` unconflicted voters are available after exhausting those substitutions, all appointments halt until the ex-officio nominating panel seats an unconflicted temporary external proxy voter (non-Editor institution, zero Editor co-authorship in the trailing 5 years), paired with mandatory verification that distinct-person, institutional, and co-authorship caps hold before review packets issue.
   - **Standing-roster caps vs. dynamic active-voting caps**: Every seat pairs a Chair (or `T1`/`T2` Co-Chair) and Alternate from distinct institutions:
     - Across the **16-person standing roster** (`6` Chairs + `2` `T1`/`T2` Co-Chairs + `8` Alternates): at most **`2` standing-roster members** (`<= 1` Chair/Co-Chair and `<= 1` Alternate) may share a primary or secondary institution with the Editor, and at most **`3` domain standing-roster members** (`<= 2` Chairs/Co-Chairs and `<= 1` Alternate) may have co-authored a publication or benchmark specification with the Editor in the trailing 5 years.
     - On **every active vote** — re-verified dynamically on full-board item votes (including reduced-$N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ votes), `1.0` release sign-off votes, and 3-voter escalation panels, counting any Alternate acting in a voting capacity and counting Seat `T`'s `T1` and `T2` holders individually — at most **`1` active voting holder** may share a primary or secondary institution with the Editor, and at most **`2` active domain voting holders** (`<= 1` on a 3-voter escalation panel) may have co-authored a publication or benchmark specification with the Editor in the trailing 5 years. If substituting an Alternate would breach an active-voting cap, on an item vote that Alternate is skipped in favor of the unconflicted 3-voter panel below (or next eligible circular seat), and on a `1.0` release sign-off vote (or when $N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ would drop below `5`) `{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}` appoint by $\ge 2/3$ vote an unconflicted ad-hoc external voter with zero Editor co-authorship and a non-Editor institution to represent that seat so `7/7` quorum and active-voting caps hold simultaneously.
2. **Mandatory conflict-of-interest disclosure, specialist substitution, and deterministic 3-voter escalation**:
   - Before reviewing a packet, each Chair, Co-Chair, Alternate, and sub-area specialist discloses (a) co-authorship or joint consortium/working-group leadership with the Editor within the trailing 5 years, (b) authorship or leadership of frameworks, papers, or standards tied to candidate mechanisms, and (c) commercial vendor affiliations.
   - Any participant who authored a cited warrant source, led an associated framework/standard, or co-authored the topic with the Editor **recuses** from evaluating or voting on the admission, preferred label, definition, or aliasing of that item (multi-mechanism suites and benchmark consortia such as `vLLM` and `MLPerf` remain categorically excluded under Gate 3). If any of the `13` mandatory sub-area specialists (`F3`, `S4`, `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, `P5`, `Q1`, `Q2`, `Q3`, `Q4a`, `Q4b`, `Q5`, `Q6`) is conflicted on an item in their sub-area, `{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}` appoint an unconflicted substitute specialist (distinct natural person) for those items so no sub-area item goes unreviewed.
   - **One vote per seat & unconflicted substitution**: When a Chair recuses on an item, voting authority for that seat transfers to that seat's unconflicted Alternate (in Seat `T`, from `T1` Co-Chair to `T1` Alternate, or `T2` Co-Chair to `T2` Alternate); each of the `7` seats casts at most one vote.
   - **Deterministic 3-voter unconflicted escalation panel**: If **both** Chair and Alternate on a domain seat $X \in \{F, S, P, Q\}$ are conflicted on an item — or in Seat `T`, if **both `T1` Co-Chair and `T1` Alternate** are conflicted on a `T1`-scoped item, **both `T2` Co-Chair and `T2` Alternate** are conflicted on a `T2`-scoped item, or a cross-`T1`/`T2` item leaves either half without an unconflicted holder — `V1` convenes a deterministic **3-voter unconflicted panel** (satisfying the active-voting caps of `<= 1` Editor-institution voter and `<= 1` trailing-5-year Editor co-author across the `3` panelists) comprising:
     1. `V1` Chair (or `V1` Alternate if `V1` Chair is conflicted, or `V2` Chair / `V2` Alternate if both `V1` holders are conflicted; if **all `4` vocabulary holders across `V1` and `V2`** are conflicted on that item, the external academic steering advisor appoints an ad-hoc unconflicted external KOS/SKOS specialist voter from a non-Editor institution with zero Editor co-authorship in the trailing 5 years before the panel convenes), plus
     2. The first **`2` unconflicted domain seats** scanning forward from $X$ in the fixed circular order **`F -> S -> P -> Q -> T -> F`** whose participation preserves the panel's active-voting caps, represented by each seat's unconflicted Chair (or unconflicted Alternate if its Chair is conflicted; when Seat `T` serves as an escalation panelist, unconflicted `T1` and `T2` holders cast a single joint vote if they concur, or the scan advances to the next unconflicted domain seat in circular order if either half of `T` is conflicted or split).
     Each panelist casts one vote (`>= 2` of `3` required to approve, zero 1–1 ties). **Fallback halt rule**: if fewer than `2` eligible unconflicted domain seats exist across the board on an item (or if both `V1` holders are conflicted on a `V1`-scoped standards question, or both `V2` holders on a `V2`-scoped SKOS question), `{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}` (via the unconflicted members plus steering advisor) must appoint unconflicted ad-hoc external specialist voter(s) (distinct natural persons, non-Editor institution, zero Editor co-authorship in the trailing 5 years) before that item can be approved; otherwise the item is withheld from `1.0`.
   - **Recusal denominator in full-board item votes**: If a full-board item vote has $N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ unconflicted seats satisfying the active-voting caps (after Alternate or ad-hoc substitution), valid action requires $N_{\text{unconflicted}} \ge 5$ and affirmative votes from at least $\lceil (N_{\text{unconflicted}} + 1) / 2 \rceil$ unconflicted seats (an explicit `abstain` by an unconflicted seat stays in the denominator).
3. **Full-domain `1.0` quorum and binding board-rejection exclusion rule**:
   - **`1.0` Quorum**: Release sign-off requires **all `7` seats participating** (each via its unconflicted Chair or Alternate — ensuring **all `5` domain group seats `F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, `T` and both `V1` and `V2` are represented**) plus completion of all mandatory sub-area specialist reports in §2.
   - **Release approval threshold**: A `1.0` citable release requires `>= 5` of `7` seats voting `approve`, including affirmative votes from **both `V1` and `V2`** within their respective scopes.
   - **Binding board-rejection exclusion rule**: While the Editor holds repository maintenance authority over `0.x-draft` experimental branches, **no concept, alias, relationship, lifecycle schema change, or conformance claim rejected by a board majority (or rejected within scope by `V1`, `V2`, or its responsible `T1`/`T2` holder) may ship inside a `1.0` release**. Rejected items must either be revised and pass renewed review by the rejecting seat(s)/majority or be excluded/retired before `1.0` tags are cut, with all dispositions recorded in `taxonomy/CHANGELOG.md`.

   - *Zero-Warrant Queue (`27` concepts across the taxonomy)*: verify external literary warrant in `S1`–`S5` or recommend retirement (`concepts_retire` with `replaced_by`) or typed aliasing.
2. **Vocabulary & SKOS Specialist Packets (`V1` and `V2`)**:

3. **Dual-Human Inter-Indexer Consistency Study (`Z39.19 §11.2.2`, `V1`-approved pre-registration required before recruitment)**:
   - **Fixed `12`-indexer (`6`-pair) staffing & Editor-independence exclusion**: Two independent human indexers (PhD candidates, postdocs, or staff ML systems engineers who did not author the taxonomy, are distinct natural persons disjoint from all `29` standing/specialist board roles, the steering advisor, and any ad-hoc escalation voters, are **not current or former PhD/postdoc advisees, lab members, or institutional direct reports of the Editor**, and have **zero co-authorship with the Editor within the trailing `36` months**) are recruited per expertise stratum — `F` (`3` areas), `S` (`4` areas), `P` (`5` areas), `Q` (`6` areas), `T1` (`1` area), and `T2` (`1` area) — totaling **`6 × 2 = 12` distinct indexers** (`0` overlap across pairs). **The `6`-pair design is fixed** (changing pair count would alter the `40 × 6` cross-group anchor incidence matrix and `8`-rater statistics below, and is disallowed without a new `V1`-approved pre-registration prior to recruitment).
   - **Area-balanced allocation & uniform `effective_lineage_component_id` independence unit (`30` Stage 1 calibration + `240` unique Stage 2 items = `12` per area across all `20` areas)**:
     - *Global `effective_lineage_component_id` assignment*: Before any Stage 1 or Stage 2 sampling, `compute_lineage_components` (`binder/cli/core/taxonomy_schema.py`) resolves all transitive `cluster_merges`, enforces the global `1 work_id <-> 1 canonical provenance_family_id` bijection, and computes connected components under `< 50%` personal-author overlap (`|A_i \cap A_j| / \min(|A_i|, |A_j|) \ge 0.50`) and shared `institutional_organizations` overlap across the full candidate pool plus `burned-clusters.json` (`schema_version: 3`), assigning every external unit a deterministic **`effective_lineage_component_id` (`eff_lineage:<lexicographically_smallest_canonical_pf>`)** (for example, in the `382`-unit Phase 3–7 study, `88` canonical `provenance_family_id` clusters = `39` internal + `49` external collapse to `41` effective lineage components = `1` internal + `40` external). **All three** independence boundaries below — Stage 1/Stage 2/burned-`dev` split disjointness, the within-area Stage 2 sampling cap, and the `10,000`-resample global cluster bootstrap — operate uniformly on **`effective_lineage_component_id`**, and study receipts report both canonical `provenance_family_id` count ($M_{\text{canon}}$) and `effective_lineage_component_id` count ($M_{\text{eff}}$) separately.
     1. *Stage 1 (Calibration, `30` items = `5` per pair, component-disjoint from Stage 2 and burned `dev`)*: Drawn from unexposed non-editor-affiliated `origin: external` sources **completely disjoint by `effective_lineage_component_id` from Stage 2's evaluation components** and disjoint from every `effective_lineage_component_id` containing any burned cluster in `burned-clusters.json`.    - **Executable weighted multi-pair estimators, global `effective_lineage_component_id` bootstrap, mandatory unit-test fixtures, and `1.0` acceptance floors**:
     - *Headline `240`-item primary-pair design & `effective_lineage_component_id` bootstrap weights*: Headline release-gate point estimates evaluate the **fixed `240` primary-pair item assignments** (`N = 240`, `r_i = 2` raters per item from pre-designated primary pair $P_g = (r_{g,1}, r_{g,2})$, `12` items/area $\times$ `20` areas across `12` distinct `effective_lineage_component_id`s per area).      - *Exact weighted Krippendorff's $\alpha$ (`101`-subject nominal $\alpha_{\text{subject}}$ and set-valued $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$)*: Let $\delta(v, v') \in [0, 1]$ denote the linear disagreement metric entering Krippendorff's coincidence sum directly (never squared a second time): $\delta_{\text{nom}}(c, k) = \mathbb{I}[c \ne k]$ on `101`-subject IDs for $\alpha_{\text{subject}}$, and Passonneau's linear MASI distance $\delta_{\text{MASI}}(A, B) = 1 - J(A, B)\,M(A, B)$ on concept sets (including $\emptyset$, with $\delta_{\text{MASI}}(\emptyset, \emptyset) = 0$ and $\delta_{\text{MASI}}(A, \emptyset) = 1$ for $A \ne \emptyset$, matching `build_study_receipts.py` and `reconcile.py`) for $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$. Over items $i \in \{1,\dots,N\}$ with $r_i \ge 2$ raters ($r_i = 2$ on the $N = 240$ primary-pair items; $r_i = 8$ on the $N = 40$ anchor items with $W_{i,\text{anchor}} = 40\,\frac{1/20}{\sum_{j \in \text{anchor}(a(i))} w_j^*} w_i^*$), define weighted pairable value count $N_{\text{val}} = \sum_{i=1}^N W_i\,r_i$ ($N_{\text{val}} = 480$ on the `240` paired items; $N_{\text{val}} = 320$ on the `40` `8`-rater anchors), weighted coincidence entry $o_{ck} = \sum_{i=1}^N W_i \frac{n_{ic}(n_{ik} - \mathbb{I}[c = k])}{r_i - 1}$ where $n_{ic} = \sum_{u=1}^{r_i} \mathbb{I}[v_{iu} = c]$, value marginal $n_c = \sum_k o_{ck} = \sum_{i=1}^N W_i\,n_{ic}$ ($\sum_c n_c = N_{\text{val}}$), weighted observed disagreement $D_o = \frac{1}{N_{\text{val}}} \sum_{c \ne k} o_{ck}\,\delta(c, k) = \frac{1}{N_{\text{val}}} \sum_{i=1}^N \frac{W_i}{r_i - 1} \sum_{1 \le u \ne u' \le r_i} \delta(v_{iu}, v_{iu'})$, and finite-sample expected disagreement $D_e = \frac{1}{N_{\text{val}}(N_{\text{val}} - 1)} \sum_{c \ne k} n_c\,n_k\,\delta(c, k)$, yielding $\alpha = 1 - D_o / D_e$. **Pair-stratified Cohen's $\bar{\kappa}_{\text{subject}}$**: within each group $g \in \{F, S, P, Q, T1, T2\}$ ($N_g = \sum_{i \in I_g} W_i = n_g$), $p_{o,g} = \frac{1}{N_g}\sum_{i \in I_g} W_i\,\mathbb{I}[v_{i1} = v_{i2}]$, $p_{e,g} = \sum_s \bigl(\frac{1}{N_g}\sum_{i \in I_g} W_i\,\mathbb{I}[v_{i1}=s]\bigr)\bigl(\frac{1}{N_g}\sum_{i \in I_g} W_i\,\mathbb{I}[v_{i2}=s]\bigr)$, $\kappa_g = (p_{o,g} - p_{e,g}) / (1 - p_{e,g})$, and $\bar{\kappa}_{\text{subject}} = \sum_g (N_g / N)\,\kappa_g$. **Degenerate-marginal convention**: if $D_e = 0$ (or $p_{e,g} = 1$, or $\bar{P}_e = 1$ in Fleiss' $\kappa$), define $\alpha = 1.0$ ($\kappa = 1.0$) when $D_o = 0$ ($p_{o,g} = 1$) and $0.0$ when $D_o > 0$, flagging any zero-variance stratum explicitly in the study report.

On the `240` primary-pair items, with $h_i = |A_{i1} \cap A_{i2}| / |A_{i1} \cup A_{i2}|$ and $q_i = 2|A_{i1} \cap A_{i2}| / (|A_{i1}| + |A_{i2}|)$ on $\mathcal{B} = \{i : A_{i1} \ne \emptyset \land A_{i2} \ne \emptyset\}$ (extended to all `240` items via $h_i = q_i = 1$ when $A_{i1} = A_{i2} = \emptyset$ and $0$ when exactly one is empty), **conditional Hooper and Rolling** are $(\sum_{i \in \mathcal{B}} W_i h_i) / (\sum_{i \in \mathcal{B}} W_i)$ and $(\sum_{i \in \mathcal{B}} W_i q_i) / (\sum_{i \in \mathcal{B}} W_i)$, and **all-item Hooper and Rolling** divide by $\sum_{i=1}^{240} W_i = 240$ (co-reported alongside equal-area `20`-area macro-averages). **Leaf-concept specificity ratio** is the share of assigned concept tags whose concept has **`0` incoming `specializes` children** in `ml-systems-taxonomy.yml`. **Four mandatory reference-implementation unit-test fixtures** must pass before Stage 2 unsealing: (1) *orientation-swap invariance* (swapping $(v_{i1}, v_{i2})$ on any item subset leaves $\alpha_{\text{subject}}$, $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$, Hooper, Rolling, and $\kappa_{\text{Fleiss}}$ invariant to $< 10^{-12}$), (2) *uniform-weight identity* ($W_i \equiv 1$ equals unweighted $\alpha_{\text{subject}}$, $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$, $\bar{\kappa}_{\text{subject}}$, Hooper, and Rolling to $< 10^{-12}$), (3) *integer cluster-replication equivalence* (unstratified integer weights $W_i = k_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$ with $\sum_{i=1}^N k_i = N$ equal unweighted $\alpha_{\text{subject}}$ and $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$ on the physically duplicated $N$-item dataset to $< 10^{-12}$), and (4) *zero-variance boundary* ($D_e = 0, D_o = 0 \implies \alpha = 1.0$; $p_{e,g} = 1, p_{o,g} = 1 \implies \kappa_g = 1.0$).
