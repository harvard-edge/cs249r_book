## 1. Purpose and Standard Grounding

Under **ANSI/NISO Z39.19-2005 (R2010) §11.1.3.1** (*Committee Approach*) and **§11.3.1.1** (*Addition of Terms* — editorial review of candidate additions), a controlled vocabulary intended for community reuse benefits from combining empirical term extraction with review by domain subject experts and controlled-vocabulary specialists.

In `0.4-draft`, candidate discovery, triangulation, cross-checking, and publish-readiness auditing have been executed by three independent language-model families (`claude-opus-5-5`, `gpt-6-astra` / `gpt-6.1-sol`, `gemini-4-argon-high`) under deterministic reconciliation and LinkML compilation gates, with final decisions made by a single human Editor. To transition from an `editorial draft` to a citable community release (`1.0`), the project defines a seven-seat external review board (supplemented by targeted area reviewers for sub-areas outside a Chair's primary specialty):

- **Five Group Seats (`F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, `T`)**: paired domain experts per top-level taxonomy group from distinct institutions (acting as Co-Chairs for `T1` Agentic and `T2` Embodied in Group `T`), responsible for reviewing subject boundaries, home-rule placements, the 16 `specializes` edges where prior model audits split, and the 27-concept zero-warrant queue.
- **Two Controlled-Vocabulary & Knowledge-Organization Specialists (`V1`, `V2`)**: information science and thesaurus/SKOS specialists responsible for reviewing ANSI/NISO Z39.19 / ISO 25964-1 clause conformance, the dual-human inter-indexer consistency protocol (`Hooper`, `Rolling`, $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$, subject $\kappa$), and SKOS/OWL crosswalks (`ACM CCS`, `CS2023`, `SFIA 9`, `ESCO`).

## 3. Governance Safeguards and Conflict-of-Interest Policy

1. **Institutional independence**: Every seat pairs primary/alternate (or co-chair) candidates from distinct institutions so no single university lab or vendor controls a group's vocabulary.
2. **Direct coverage of the two-area empirical study (`P4` & `Q4`)**:
   - **Group P** (Stoica / Zhang) covers the `P4` (`serving`) mechanisms evaluated in Phases 3–6 (`mlsys.paged-kv`, `mlsys.prefill-decode`, `mlsys.speculative-decoding`, `mlsys.compound-workflow-pipelining`, `mlsys.attention-ffn-disaggregation`).
   - **Group Q** (Seshia / Wu) covers the `Q4` (`safety-assurance`) mechanisms evaluated in Phases 3–6 (`mlsys.operating-domain`, `mlsys.simplex-architecture`, `mlsys.control-barrier-function`, `mlsys.neural-network-verification`, `mlsys.safety-case`, `mlsys.tolerable-risk`).
3. **Mandatory conflict-of-interest disclosure and item-level recusal**: Before reviewing a packet, each board member discloses co-authorship, open-source project leadership, or commercial ties with the Editor or with candidate mechanisms in their packet (for example, `PagedAttention`/vLLM in `P4`, `TVM`/`MLC-LLM` in `S2`, or `MLPerf` in `Q1`). A reviewer recuses from voting on admission, naming, or aliasing of mechanisms originating from their own publications or products; those items are adjudicated by the paired Co-Chair/Alternate together with `V1`.
4. **Complementary domain and vocabulary roles (`Z39.19 §11.1.3.1`)**: Subject specialists verify engineering accuracy and curriculum/production warrant, while `V1` and `V2` enforce equivalence control, pre- vs. post-coordination, and hierarchical transitivity (`all-and-some` test).

## 4. Scoped Work Packages for Board Review (When Authorized)

To keep external expert review structured and auditable, board members receive role-specific packets generated from the compiled release (`taxonomy/downloads/`):

1. **Domain Group Packets (`F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, and `T1`+`T2` jointly)**:
   - *Subject & Concept Inventory* (`12`–`25` subjects, `90`–`240` concepts in their group): flag any missing `must`-have concept backed by $\ge 2$ provenance families, any product/benchmark masquerading as a concept (`Gate 3`), or any misplaced home subject.
   - *Split Hierarchy Queue (`16` edges total across the taxonomy, ~`3` per group)*: decide each contested `specializes` edge using the 3-step `all-and-some` test (`Z39.19 §8.3.1`).
   - *Zero-Warrant Queue (`27` concepts total across the taxonomy, ~`5` per group)*: confirm external literary warrant (`S1`–`S4`) or recommend retirement/aliasing into a parent concept.
2. **Vocabulary & SKOS Specialist Packets (`V1` and `V2`)**:
   - *`V1` (Z39.19 / ISO 25964 Packet)*: audit `taxonomy/METHOD.md` clause mappings, review the 93 legacy compound labels joined by "and"/"versus"/"vs." against `Z39.19 §7`, audit upward-posted entry terms (`§8.2.4`), and sign off on the pre-registered human inter-indexer study protocol before data collection.
   - *`V2` (SKOS / RDF / Crosswalk Packet)*: audit `mlsys-taxonomy.ttl`, `mlsys-taxonomy-relations.ttl`, `mlsys-taxonomy.dcat.ttl`, URI dereferencing, conditional-relation representation, and the SKOS mapping tables (`ACM CCS`, `CS2023`, `SFIA 9`, `ESCO`).
3. **Dual-Human Inter-Indexer Study (project release requirement implementing `Z39.19 §11.2.2`'s evaluation recommendation)**:
   - Conducted in two stages so calibration never contaminates evaluation: first, a **5-item pilot calibration pass** per group to freeze indexer instructions; second, a **fresh stratified evaluation sample** drawn from uninspected `reserve` / post-cutoff sources across all five groups (disjoint from the inspected `P4`+`Q4` study pool; for Group `T`, stratified across `T1` and `T2` with a matched second domain indexer per sub-area, totaling 10 domain indexers across the 5 groups).
   - Two human indexers independently assign 1 home subject and 0–3 concepts per item (permitting subject-only tags when no specific concept fits, per `taxonomy/README.md`), reporting `Hooper`, `Rolling`, $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$, subject $\kappa$, bootstrap 95% confidence intervals, empty-set rates, and max single-concept concentration ($p_{\max} \le 15\%$).
