# External Editorial Board Charter and Human Evaluation Protocol (Z39.19 §11.1.3.1, §11.2.2, §11.3.1.1)

Status: public governance charter and role specification for version `0.4-draft` (`2026-11-02`). **This document defines seat qualifications, area coverage obligations, appointment and conflict-of-interest rules, and pre-registered human evaluation specifications only; no external board has yet been convened, no individual has been contacted or asked to endorse the ML Systems Taxonomy, and unconsented personal names are intentionally excluded from the public repository.**

---

## 1. Purpose and Historical Context

Under **ANSI/NISO Z39.19-2005 (R2010) §11.1.3.1** (*Committee Approach*), **§11.2.2** (*Evaluation Criteria*), and **§11.3.1.1** (*Addition of Terms*), a controlled vocabulary intended for community reuse combines empirical corpus extraction with review by domain subject specialists and controlled-vocabulary specialists.

In `0.4-draft`, construction and verification used language-model passes under deterministic reconciliation and LinkML compilation gates, with final decisions made by a single human Editor (Vijay Janapa Reddi): specifically, the `0.4-draft` refresh ran Round 1 as seven parallel lenses in one model family (`claude-fable-5-1`) followed by a three-family Round 2 cross-check and independent audit (`gpt-6.1-sol`, `gemini-4-argon-high`, `claude-opus-5-5`), while three-family blind discovery and reconciliation were evaluated on the pre-registered Phase 3–7 two-area study (`P4` and `Q4`) and codified for future refreshes.

To transition from an `editorial draft` (`0.x-draft`) to a citable community release (`1.0`), the project requires review by a **7-seat external editorial board** backed by mandatory sub-area specialist reviewers covering all `20` taxonomy areas (`101` subjects, `878` concepts) and a **12-indexer dual-human inter-indexer consistency study**.

## 3. Appointment Independence, Recusal, Quorum, and Binding Board-Rejection Exclusion Rule

1. **Appointment independence and active-voting-roster co-authorship caps**:
   - `V1` and `V2` Chairs and Alternates are nominated jointly by the Editor and an external academic steering advisor with zero co-authorship with the Editor in the trailing 5 years (`V1` and `V2` Chairs and Alternates must likewise have zero co-authorship with the Editor in the trailing 5 years). `V1` co-verifies that all domain Chair, Co-Chair, Alternate, and sub-area specialist invitations satisfy institutional and co-authorship independence before review packets issue.
   - Every seat pairs a Chair (or `T1`/`T2` Co-Chair) and Alternate from distinct institutions. Across **both the standing roster (all Chairs, both `T1`/`T2` Co-Chairs, and all Alternates) and the active voting roster on every item or release vote** (counting any Alternate acting in a voting capacity and counting Seat `T`'s `T1` and `T2` holders individually): at most **`1` active voting holder** across the `7` seats may share a primary or secondary institution with the Editor, and at most **`2` active domain voting holders** may have co-authored a publication or benchmark specification with the Editor in the trailing 5 years. If substituting an Alternate would breach either cap on a vote, that Alternate is ineligible on that vote and the item escalates to the unconflicted 3-voter panel below.
2. **Mandatory conflict-of-interest disclosure, specialist substitution, and deterministic 3-voter escalation**:
   - Before reviewing a packet, each Chair, Co-Chair, Alternate, and sub-area specialist discloses (a) co-authorship or joint consortium/working-group leadership with the Editor within the trailing 5 years, (b) authorship or leadership of frameworks, papers, or standards tied to candidate mechanisms, and (c) commercial vendor affiliations.
   - Any participant who authored a cited warrant source, led an associated framework/standard, or co-authored the topic with the Editor **recuses** from evaluating or voting on the admission, preferred label, definition, or aliasing of that item (multi-mechanism suites and benchmark consortia such as `vLLM` and `MLPerf` remain categorically excluded under Gate 3). If a mandatory sub-area specialist (`F3`, `S4`, `P1`–`P3`, `Q1`–`Q6`) is conflicted on an item in their sub-area, `V1` appoints an unconflicted substitute specialist for those items so no sub-area item goes unreviewed.
   - **One vote per seat & unconflicted substitution**: When a Chair recuses on an item, voting authority for that seat transfers to that seat's unconflicted Alternate (in Seat `T`, from `T1` Co-Chair to `T1` Alternate, or `T2` Co-Chair to `T2` Alternate); each of the `7` seats casts at most one vote.
   - **Deterministic 3-voter unconflicted escalation panel**: If **both** Chair and Alternate on a domain seat $X \in \{F, S, P, Q, T\}$ are conflicted on an item, `V1` convenes a deterministic **3-voter unconflicted panel** comprising:
     1. `V1` Chair (or `V1` Alternate if `V1` Chair is conflicted, or `V2` Chair / `V2` Alternate if both `V1` holders are conflicted), plus
     2. The first **`2` unconflicted domain seats** scanning forward from $X$ in the fixed circular order **`F -> S -> P -> Q -> T -> F`**, represented by each seat's unconflicted Chair (or unconflicted Alternate if its Chair is conflicted; when Seat `T` serves as an escalation panelist, unconflicted `T1` and `T2` holders cast a single joint vote if they concur, or `V1` advances to the next unconflicted domain seat in circular order if either half of `T` is conflicted or split).
     Each panelist casts one vote (`>= 2` of `3` required to approve, zero 1–1 ties). **Fallback halt rule**: if fewer than `2` unconflicted domain seats exist across the board on an item (or if both `V1` holders are conflicted on a `V1`-scoped standards question, or both `V2` holders on a `V2`-scoped SKOS question), `V1` and the external steering advisor must appoint unconflicted ad-hoc external specialist voter(s) (zero Editor co-authorship in the trailing 5 years) before that item can be approved; otherwise the item is withheld from `1.0`.
   - **Recusal denominator in full-board item votes**: If a full-board item vote has $N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ unconflicted seats (after Alternate substitution), valid action requires $N_{\text{unconflicted}} \ge 5$ and affirmative votes from at least $\lceil (N_{\text{unconflicted}} + 1) / 2 \rceil$ unconflicted seats (an explicit `abstain` by an unconflicted seat stays in the denominator).
3. **Full-domain `1.0` quorum and binding board-rejection exclusion rule**:
   - **`1.0` Quorum**: Release sign-off requires **all `7` seats participating** (each via its unconflicted Chair or Alternate — ensuring **all `5` domain group seats `F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, `T` and both `V1` and `V2` are represented**) plus completion of all mandatory sub-area specialist reports in §2.
   - **Release approval threshold**: A `1.0` citable release requires `>= 5` of `7` seats voting `approve`, including affirmative votes from **both `V1` and `V2`** within their respective scopes.
   - **Binding board-rejection exclusion rule**: While the Editor holds repository maintenance authority over `0.x-draft` experimental branches, **no concept, alias, relationship, lifecycle schema change, or conformance claim rejected by a board majority (or rejected within scope by `V1`, `V2`, or its responsible `T1`/`T2` holder) may ship inside a `1.0` release**. Rejected items must either be revised and pass renewed review by the rejecting seat(s)/majority or be excluded/retired before `1.0` tags are cut, with all dispositions recorded in `taxonomy/CHANGELOG.md`.

## 4. Scoped Work Packages and Pre-Registered Dual-Human Indexing Design

1. **Domain Group Packets (`F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, and `T1`+`T2`, plus mandatory sub-area specialists)**:
   - *Subject & Concept Inventory* (**`10`–`34` subjects** and **`77`–`301` primary concepts** [`106`–`336` including secondary placements] per group in `0.4-draft`): audit missing `must`-have mechanisms backed by $\ge 2$ independent `provenance_family_id` lineages (`>= 1` `origin: external`), product/suite leaks (`Gate 3`), and Home-Rule placements.
   - *Split Hierarchy Queue (`16` edges across the taxonomy)*: adjudicate each `specializes` edge where prior model audits split using the 3-step `all-and-some` test (`Z39.19 §8.3.1`).
   - *Zero-Warrant Queue (`27` concepts across the taxonomy)*: verify external literary warrant in `S1`–`S5` or recommend retirement (`concepts_retire` with `replaced_by`) or typed aliasing.
2. **Vocabulary & SKOS Specialist Packets (`V1` and `V2`)**:
   - *`V1` (Z39.19 / ISO 25964 Packet)*: verify `taxonomy/METHOD.md` clause mappings against primary standard texts (specifically auditing all ISO 25964-1/-2 pinpoints); review the **`94` legacy compound concept labels** matching punctuation-safe `\b(?:and|versus|vs)\b\.?` (`95` including `\bor\b`, plus `16` legacy entry terms) against `Z39.19 §7`; audit legacy `aliases` across all `1,254` entry terms (including broader `assurance case` on `mlsys.safety-case`, method-artifact `unsafe control action` / `UCA` on `mlsys.stpa`, and library name `XGBoost`) against `Z39.19 §8.2` and Gate 3; review prospective `1.0` `RetiredConcept` schema slots (`resolution_class`, `split_into`); and approve the Stage 2 human indexing pre-registration before recruitment.
   - *`V2` (SKOS / RDF / Crosswalk Packet)*: verify `mlsys-taxonomy.ttl`, `mlsys-taxonomy-relations.ttl`, `mlsys-taxonomy.dcat.ttl`, URI dereferencing, conditional-edge RDF caveats, and SKOS crosswalks (`ACM CCS`, `CS2023`, `SFIA 9`, `ESCO`).
3. **Dual-Human Inter-Indexer Consistency Study (`Z39.19 §11.2.2`, `V1`-approved pre-registration required before recruitment)**:
   - **Staffing (`12` distinct domain indexers across `6` pairs)**: Two independent human indexers (PhD candidates, postdocs, or staff ML systems engineers who did not author the taxonomy and are separate from voting Chairs) are recruited per expertise stratum — `F` (`3` areas), `S` (`4` areas), `P` (`5` areas), `Q` (`6` areas), `T1` (`1` area), and `T2` (`1` area) — totaling **`6 × 2 = 12` distinct core indexers** (`0` overlap between `T1` and `T2`; optionally splitting `P` into `P-a`/`P-b` and `Q` into `Q-a`/`Q-b` if capping individual workload below `68` items).
   - **Area-balanced allocation (`30` Stage 1 calibration + `240` unique Stage 2 items = `12` per area across all `20` areas)**:
     1. *Stage 1 (Calibration, `30` items = `5` per pair)*: Indexers practice applying `tagging_rules` (`taxonomy/README.md` across all `101` subjects), reconcile rule misunderstandings with `V1`, and freeze the indexing guide. Stage 1 items are excluded from Stage 2 scoring.
     2. *Stage 2 Primary Allocation (`12` unique items/area $\times$ `20` areas = **`240` unique items**)*: Drawn from unexposed `origin: external` post-cutoff / `reserve` clusters (disjoint from the burned `P4`+`Q4` study pool). Allocating **by area first (`12` items in every one of the `20` areas)** guarantees $\ge 10$ items/area everywhere (including `P`'s `5` areas and `Q`'s `6` areas) and provides a `4`-item buffer per area above the $\ge 8$ jointly-indexed per-area floor (`80%` non-empty $\times 12 = 9.6 \ge 8$). Primary-pair item counts sum to `240`: `F = 36` (`3×12`), `S = 48` (`4×12`), `P = 60` (`5×12`), `Q = 72` (`6×12`), `T1 = 12` (`1×12`), `T2 = 12` (`1×12`).
     3. *Balanced `40`-Anchor Cross-Group Design (`2` anchors/area = `40` anchor items $\times$ `3` secondary pairs = **`120` secondary evaluations**; **`360` total pair-evaluations**)*: From each area's `12` items, `2` stratified items (`40` anchor items total: `F = 6`, `S = 8`, `P = 10`, `Q = 12`, `T1 = 2`, `T2 = 2`) are each routed to **`3` deterministic non-primary pairs** using the balanced incidence table below so **every one of the `6` pairs receives exactly `20` incoming cross-group anchor items** (`row sum = 3 × anchors`, `column sum = 20` for every receiver pair):
        - `Q` (`12` anchors) $\rightarrow$ `{F: 8, S: 7, P: 7, Q: 0, T1: 7, T2: 7}` (`36` secondary evaluations)
        - `P` (`10` anchors) $\rightarrow$ `{F: 6, S: 6, P: 0, Q: 6, T1: 6, T2: 6}` (`30` secondary evaluations)
        - `S` (`8` anchors) $\rightarrow$ `{F: 4, S: 0, P: 5, Q: 5, T1: 5, T2: 5}` (`24` secondary evaluations)
        - `F` (`6` anchors) $\rightarrow$ `{F: 0, S: 5, P: 5, Q: 5, T1: 1, T2: 2}` (`18` secondary evaluations)
        - `T1` (`2` anchors) $\rightarrow$ `{F: 1, S: 1, P: 2, Q: 2, T1: 0, T2: 0}` (`6` secondary evaluations)
        - `T2` (`2` anchors) $\rightarrow$ `{F: 1, S: 1, P: 1, Q: 2, T1: 1, T2: 0}` (`6` secondary evaluations)
        **Group and area headers are masked on all item packets**, items are shuffled within each pair's packet (`total evaluations per pair`: `F = 56`, `S = 68`, `P = 80`, `Q = 92`, `T1 = 32`, `T2 = 32`; `sum = 360`), and indexers choose freely from all `101` subjects and `878` concepts (`1` primary subject, `0`–`3` concepts).
   - **Deduplication rule, estimators, specificity definition, and numerical `1.0` acceptance floors**:
     - *Headline `240`-item primary-pair estimator & stratified macro-average*: Headline release-gate point estimates evaluate the **fixed `240` primary-pair item assignments** (`1` pre-designated primary pair per item, `12` items/area $\times$ `20` areas, preventing anchor duplication bias), reporting both the pooled `240`-item micro-average and the equal-area-weighted `20`-area macro-average (which matches the `240`-item micro-average on all-item averages because every area has exactly `12` items), with **`10,000`-resample cluster-level bootstrap 95% CIs by `provenance_family_id`**.
     - *Cross-pair `40`-anchor multi-rater reliability*: Reported separately across the `40` anchor items (`4` pairs = `8` human indexers per anchor item: `1` primary + `3` secondary pairs), computing `8`-rater Fleiss' $\kappa$ on `101`-subject assignment and `8`-rater Krippendorff's $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$ on concept sets to measure cross-group Home-Rule boundary stability without inflating the `240`-item headline sample.
     - *Empty-set & unified leaf-specificity definitions*: Conditional Hooper and Rolling average over `both non-empty` primary-pair items; **all-item Hooper and Rolling** evaluate all `240` items under $J(\emptyset, \emptyset) = \text{Dice}(\emptyset, \emptyset) = 1$ and $J(A, \emptyset) = \text{Dice}(A, \emptyset) = 0$ for $A \neq \emptyset$. **Leaf-concept specificity ratio** is defined uniformly as the share of assigned concept tags whose concept has **`0` incoming `specializes` children** in `ml-systems-taxonomy.yml`.
     - *Pre-registered `1.0` human consistency floors (on the `240` primary-pair items)*: pooled 101-subject $\kappa \ge 0.65$, conditional Hooper $\ge 45.0\%$ (all-item Hooper $\ge 40.0\%$), conditional Rolling $\ge 60.0\%$ (all-item Rolling $\ge 55.0\%$), pooled $\alpha_{\text{MASI}} \ge 0.45$, empty-assignment rate $\le 20.0\%$ ($\ge 192$ of `240` items jointly non-empty and $\ge 8$ of `12` items jointly non-empty in every one of the `20` areas), and max single-concept share $p_{\max} \le 15.0\%$. If any floor or per-area minimum fails, `1.0` release halts, offending subject scope notes or tagging rules are adjudicated on a separate calibration slice, and Stage 2 is rerun on fresh unexposed clusters.
