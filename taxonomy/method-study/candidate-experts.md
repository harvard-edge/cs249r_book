# External Editorial Board Charter and Human Evaluation Protocol (Z39.19 §11.1.3.1, §11.2.2, §11.3.1.1)

Status: public governance charter and role specification for version `0.4-draft` (`2026-11-02`). **This document defines seat qualifications, area coverage obligations, appointment and conflict-of-interest rules, and pre-registered human evaluation specifications only; no external board has yet been convened, no individual has been contacted or asked to endorse the ML Systems Taxonomy, and unconsented personal names are intentionally excluded from the public repository.**

---

## 1. Purpose and Historical Context

Under **ANSI/NISO Z39.19-2005 (R2010) §11.1.3.1** (*Committee Approach*), **§11.2.2** (*Evaluation Criteria*), and **§11.3.1.1** (*Addition of Terms*), a controlled vocabulary intended for community reuse combines empirical corpus extraction with review by domain subject specialists and controlled-vocabulary specialists.

In `0.4-draft`, construction and verification used language-model passes under deterministic reconciliation and LinkML compilation gates, with final decisions made by a single human Editor (Vijay Janapa Reddi): specifically, the `0.4-draft` refresh ran Round 1 as seven parallel lenses in one model family (`claude-fable-5-1`) followed by a three-family Round 2 cross-check and independent audit (`gpt-6.1-sol`, `gemini-4-argon-high`, `claude-opus-5-5`), while three-family blind discovery and reconciliation were evaluated on the pre-registered Phase 3–7 two-area study (`P4` and `Q4`) and codified for future refreshes.

To transition from an `editorial draft` (`0.x-draft`) to a citable community release (`1.0`), the project requires review by a **7-seat external editorial board** (`13` mandatory dedicated sub-area specialists covering `12` areas [`Q4a`+`Q4b` in `Q4`] plus Chair/Co-Chair/Alternate coverage of the remaining `8` areas [`F1`, `F2`, `S1`–`S3`, `P4`, `T1`, `T2`] across all `20` taxonomy areas / `101` subjects / `878` concepts) and a **fixed 12-indexer (`6`-pair) dual-human inter-indexer consistency study**.

## 3. Appointment Independence, Recusal, Quorum, and Binding Board-Rejection Exclusion Rule

1. **Independent appointment chain, non-Editor administrative convenor, and standing-roster vs. active-voting co-authorship caps**:
   - **Deterministic initial convenor selection, ex-officio nominating panel, & open-nomination fallback**: Outreach to form the ex-officio nominating panel is administered by an **independent non-Editor administrative convenor** selected deterministically — without Editor discretion — by inviting, in reverse-chronological order of most recently completed chair or co-chair term across the fixed standards-committee precedence sequence `(1) NISO Z39.19 / Information Discovery & Interchange Topic Committee -> (2) ISO/TC 46/SC 9/WG 8 (ISO 25964) -> (3) W3C SKOS Working Group -> (4) DCMI Usage Board` (with any same-date term-completion tie broken by ascending `SHA-256(NIST Randomness Beacon pulse at outreach announcement || candidate ORCID/URI)`), the first willing scholar from an institution disjoint from the Editor's institution who has zero co-authorship or joint grant/consortium leadership with the Editor in the trailing 5 years (**neither the Editor nor any member of the Editor's lab or institution plays any role in selecting, ranking, contacting, or filtering the convenor or the nominating panel**; if all four historical rosters are exhausted after `21` calendar days, a public `14`-day call for active `ISKO` / `ASIS&T` SIG/CR officers meeting the identical independence criteria is resolved by the same NIST Randomness Beacon hash tiebreak). **Mandatory pre-outreach publication**: the administrative convenor's signed COI declaration, eligibility check, and selection trace must be committed to the public governance archive **before** any nominating-panel invitation is sent. Before any board invitations issue, that convenor forms an independent 3-scholar nominating panel ex officio by inviting, in deterministic reverse-chronological order of service, the most recent willing program or general chairs of (i) `MLSys` (fallback: `EuroSys` / `NSDI`), (ii) `OSDI` or `SOSP` (fallback: `ASPLOS` / `ISCA`), and (iii) an active `NISO` / `DCMI` / `W3C` knowledge-organization or SKOS working group (fallback: `ISKO` / `ASIS&T` SIG/CR chairs), each a distinct natural person disjoint from the convenor, from a distinct institution disjoint from the Editor's institution, and having zero co-authorship or joint grant/consortium leadership with the Editor in the trailing 5 years. **Deterministic open-nomination fallback**: if any of the `3` nominating seats remains unfilled after `30` calendar days across both primary and fallback venue lists, the administrative convenor opens a public `21`-day community/self-nomination call for scholars meeting the seat's domain and independence criteria and selects among verified-eligible nominees via a pre-announced public randomness beacon (`SHA-256` of the first NIST Randomness Beacon pulse after the `21`-day window closes). That 3-scholar nominating panel appoints by $\ge 2/3$ vote an **external academic steering advisor** (tenured faculty member or standards-body working-group chair disjoint from the convenor and nominating panel, from an institution disjoint from the Editor's institution, with zero co-authorship or joint grant/consortium leadership with the Editor in the trailing 5 years).
   - **Independent `V1`/`V2` and domain Chair nomination, candidate-specific recusal, & appointing-committee vacancy backfill**: Every appointing voter (including the steering advisor, `V1`/`V2` Chairs and Alternates, and any nominating-panel or temporary-proxy substitute) is bound by the **identical COI disclosure and candidate-specific recusal rules** in §3.2 (and may never vote on their own appointment or on a candidate sharing any of their institutions or trailing-5-year co-author networks). Whenever the steering advisor is conflicted or unavailable on a candidate appointment or ad-hoc voter selection, an unconflicted member of the 3-scholar ex-officio nominating panel (selected in deterministic seat order `(iii) -> (i) -> (ii)`) substitutes for the steering advisor. To seat `V1` and `V2` Chairs and Alternates before `V1`/`V2` themselves exist, each of the `4` `V1`/`V2` positions (`V1:chair`, `V1:alternate`, `V2:chair`, `V2:alternate`) requires a recorded **`>= 3`-voter appointment vote** ($\ge 2/3$ affirmative votes across $\ge 3$ eligible unconflicted distinct-person voters: the `steering_advisor` plus at least `2` unconflicted `nominating_panel` members, or all `3` unconflicted `nominating_panel` members if the `steering_advisor` is conflicted on that candidate; all `4` appointees must be distinct natural persons disjoint from the convenor and nominating panel, from `4` distinct non-Editor institutions, with zero co-authorship with the Editor in the trailing 5 years; the Editor may surface scope context in writing but holds no appointment or veto power). Once `V1` and `V2` are seated, the 3-voter appointing committee **`{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}`** appoints all domain Chairs, `T1`/`T2` Co-Chairs, Alternates, the `13` mandatory sub-area specialists, any ad-hoc escalation voters (`adhoc_voters`), and the `12` human indexers by a $\ge 2/3$ vote (`>= 2` of `3` eligible unconflicted voters concurring across `>= 3` eligible unconflicted distinct-person voters). **Deterministic appointing-committee vacancy backfill & 3-voter halt floor**: if `V1 Chair` is conflicted or vacant on a candidate, `V1 Alternate` substitutes; if **both** `V1 Chair` and `V1 Alternate` are conflicted, replacement proceeds in deterministic order `(a) unconflicted V2 Alternate (if not already serving on the 3-voter committee) -> (b) unconflicted member of the reconvened 3-scholar ex-officio nominating panel in seat order (iii) -> (i) -> (ii) not already substituting on that vote` (symmetric order if both `V2 Chair` and `V2 Alternate` are conflicted: `unconflicted V1 Alternate -> unconflicted nominating-panel member in order (iii) -> (i) -> (ii)`). **Mandatory 3-voter appointment floor**: every appointment vote (`steering_advisor`, all `16` standing roster slots, all `13` specialists, any `substitute_specialists`, all `12` indexers, and any `adhoc_voters`) requires **at least `3` eligible unconflicted distinct-person voters** (`len(voters) >= 3`, `approve / len(voters) >= 2/3`); if fewer than `3` unconflicted voters are available after exhausting those substitutions, all appointments halt until the ex-officio nominating panel (`>= 2` of `3` unconflicted members) seats an unconflicted `temporary_proxies` voter (non-Editor institution, zero Editor co-authorship in the trailing 5 years), paired with mandatory verification that distinct-person, institutional, and co-authorship caps hold before review packets issue.
   - **Standing-roster caps, non-Editor single-institution diversity caps, & dynamic active-voting caps**: Every seat pairs a Chair (or `T1`/`T2` Co-Chair) and Alternate with disjoint `institutions` sets (`0` shared primary or secondary institutions within any seat pair, and `0` shared institutions across `T1:cochair`, `T1:alternate`, `T2:cochair`, `T2:alternate`):
     - Across the **16-person standing roster** (`6` Chairs + `2` `T1`/`T2` Co-Chairs + `8` Alternates): at most **`2` standing-roster members** (`<= 1` Chair/Co-Chair and `<= 1` Alternate, and `0` on `V1`/`V2`) may share a primary or secondary institution with the Editor; at most **`2` standing-roster members** (`<= 1` Chair/Co-Chair) may share **any single non-Editor institution**; and at most **`3` domain standing-roster members** (`<= 2` Chairs/Co-Chairs and `<= 1` Alternate, and `0` on `V1`/`V2`) may have co-authored a publication or benchmark specification with the Editor in the trailing 5 years.
     - Across the **13 mandatory sub-area specialists** and **12 human indexers**: at most **`1` specialist** (`<= 3` from any single non-Editor institution) and **`0` human indexers** (`<= 2` from any single non-Editor institution, and `<= 1` per indexing pair so paired raters never share an institution) may share a primary or secondary institution with the Editor; at most **`3` specialists** and **`0` human indexers** may have co-authored with the Editor in the trailing 5 years; and **`0` human indexers** may be current or former PhD/postdoc advisees, lab members, or direct reports of the Editor (`editor_advisee_or_lab_or_report == false`).
     - On **every active vote** — re-verified dynamically on full-board item votes (including reduced-$N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ votes), `1.0` release sign-off votes (`8` active voting individuals across `F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, `T1`, `T2`, `V1`, `V2`), and 3-voter escalation panels, counting any Alternate or ad-hoc voter acting in a voting capacity and counting Seat `T`'s `T1` and `T2` holders individually — at most **`1` active voting holder** may share a primary or secondary institution with the Editor, at most **`2` active voting holders** (`<= 1` on a 3-voter escalation panel) may share **any single non-Editor institution**, and at most **`2` active domain voting holders** (`<= 1` on a 3-voter escalation panel, `0` on `V1`/`V2`) may have co-authored a publication or benchmark specification with the Editor in the trailing 5 years. If substituting an Alternate would breach an active-voting cap, on an item vote that Alternate is skipped in favor of the unconflicted 3-voter panel below (or next eligible circular seat), and on a `1.0` release sign-off vote (or when $N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ would drop below `5`) `{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}` appoint by $\ge 2/3$ vote an unconflicted ad-hoc external voter with zero Editor co-authorship and a non-Editor institution to represent that seat so `7/7` quorum and active-voting caps hold simultaneously.
2. **Mandatory conflict-of-interest disclosure, specialist substitution, and deterministic 3-voter escalation**:
   - Before reviewing a packet, each Chair, Co-Chair, Alternate, and sub-area specialist discloses (a) co-authorship or joint consortium/working-group leadership with the Editor within the trailing 5 years, (b) authorship or leadership of frameworks, papers, or standards tied to candidate mechanisms, and (c) commercial vendor affiliations.
   - Any participant who authored a cited warrant source, led an associated framework/standard, or co-authored the topic with the Editor **recuses** from evaluating or voting on the admission, preferred label, definition, or aliasing of that item (multi-mechanism suites and benchmark consortia such as `vLLM` and `MLPerf` remain categorically excluded under Gate 3). If any of the `13` mandatory sub-area specialists (`F3`, `S4`, `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, `P5`, `Q1`, `Q2`, `Q3`, `Q4a`, `Q4b`, `Q5`, `Q6`) is conflicted on an item in their sub-area, `{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}` appoint an unconflicted substitute specialist (distinct natural person) for those items so no sub-area item goes unreviewed.
   - **One vote per seat & unconflicted substitution**: When a Chair recuses on an item, voting authority for that seat transfers to that seat's unconflicted Alternate (in Seat `T`, from `T1` Co-Chair to `T1` Alternate, or `T2` Co-Chair to `T2` Alternate); each of the `7` seats casts at most one vote.
   - **Deterministic 3-voter unconflicted escalation panel**: If **both** Chair and Alternate on a domain seat $X \in \{F, S, P, Q\}$ are conflicted on an item — or in Seat `T`, if **both `T1` Co-Chair and `T1` Alternate** are conflicted on a `T1`-scoped item, **both `T2` Co-Chair and `T2` Alternate** are conflicted on a `T2`-scoped item, or a cross-`T1`/`T2` item leaves either half without an unconflicted holder — `V1` convenes a deterministic **3-voter unconflicted panel** (satisfying the active-voting caps of `<= 1` Editor-institution voter and `<= 1` trailing-5-year Editor co-author across the `3` panelists) comprising:
     1. `V1` Chair (or `V1` Alternate if `V1` Chair is conflicted, or `V2` Chair / `V2` Alternate if both `V1` holders are conflicted; if **all `4` vocabulary holders across `V1` and `V2`** are conflicted on that item, the external academic steering advisor appoints an ad-hoc unconflicted external KOS/SKOS specialist voter from a non-Editor institution with zero Editor co-authorship in the trailing 5 years before the panel convenes), plus
     2. The first **`2` unconflicted domain seats** scanning forward from $X$ in the fixed circular order **`F -> S -> P -> Q -> T -> F`** whose participation preserves the panel's active-voting caps, represented by each seat's unconflicted Chair (or unconflicted Alternate if its Chair is conflicted; when Seat `T` serves as an escalation panelist, unconflicted `T1` and `T2` holders cast a single joint vote if they concur, or the scan advances to the next unconflicted domain seat in circular order if either half of `T` is conflicted or split).
     Each panelist casts one vote (`>= 2` of `3` required to approve, zero 1–1 ties). **Fallback halt rule**: if fewer than `2` eligible unconflicted domain seats exist across the board on an item (or if both `V1` holders are conflicted on a `V1`-scoped standards question, or both `V2` holders on a `V2`-scoped SKOS question), `{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}` (via the unconflicted members plus steering advisor) must appoint unconflicted ad-hoc external specialist voter(s) (distinct natural persons, non-Editor institution, zero Editor co-authorship in the trailing 5 years) before that item can be approved; otherwise the item is withheld from `1.0`.
   - **Recusal denominator in full-board item votes**: If a full-board item vote has $N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ unconflicted seats satisfying the active-voting caps (after Alternate or ad-hoc substitution), valid action requires $N_{\text{unconflicted}} \ge 5$ and affirmative votes from at least $\lceil (N_{\text{unconflicted}} + 1) / 2 \rceil$ unconflicted seats (an explicit `abstain` by an unconflicted seat stays in the denominator).
3. **Full-domain `1.0` quorum and binding board-rejection exclusion rule**:
   - **`1.0` Quorum**: Release sign-off requires **all `7` seats participating** (each via its unconflicted Chair or Alternate — ensuring **all `5` domain group seats `F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, `T` and both `V1` and `V2` are represented**) plus completion of all mandatory sub-area specialist reports in §2.
   - **Release approval threshold**: A `1.0` citable release requires `>= 5` of `7` seats voting `approve`, including affirmative votes from **both `V1` and `V2`** within their respective scopes.
   - **Binding board-rejection exclusion rule**: While the Editor holds repository maintenance authority over `0.x-draft` experimental branches, **no concept, alias, relationship, lifecycle schema change, or conformance claim rejected by a board majority (or rejected within scope by `V1`, `V2`, or its responsible `T1`/`T2` holder) may ship inside a `1.0` release**. Rejected items must either be revised and pass renewed review by the rejecting seat(s)/majority or be excluded/retired before `1.0` tags are cut, with all dispositions recorded in `taxonomy/CHANGELOG.md`.
