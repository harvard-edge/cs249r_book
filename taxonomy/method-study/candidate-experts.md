# External Editorial Board Charter and Human Evaluation Protocol (Z39.19 §11.1.3.1, §11.2.2, §11.3.1.1)

Status: public governance charter and role specification for version `0.4-draft` (`2026-11-02`). **This document defines seat qualifications, area coverage obligations, appointment and conflict-of-interest rules, and pre-registered human evaluation specifications only; no external board has yet been convened, no individual has been contacted or asked to endorse the ML Systems Taxonomy, and unconsented personal names are intentionally excluded from the public repository.**

---

## 1. Purpose and Historical Context

Under **ANSI/NISO Z39.19-2005 (R2010) §11.1.3.1** (*Committee Approach*), **§11.2.2** (*Evaluation Criteria*), and **§11.3.1.1** (*Addition of Terms*), a controlled vocabulary intended for community reuse combines empirical corpus extraction with review by domain subject specialists and controlled-vocabulary specialists.

In `0.4-draft`, construction and verification used language-model passes under deterministic reconciliation and LinkML compilation gates, with final decisions made by a single human Editor (Vijay Janapa Reddi): specifically, the `0.4-draft` refresh ran Round 1 as seven parallel lenses in one model family (`claude-fable-5-1`) followed by a three-family Round 2 cross-check and independent audit (`gpt-6.1-sol`, `gemini-4-argon-high`, `claude-opus-5-5`), while three-family blind discovery and reconciliation were evaluated on the pre-registered Phase 3–7 two-area study (`P4` and `Q4`) and codified for future refreshes.

To transition from an `editorial draft` (`0.x-draft`) to a citable community release (`1.0`), the project requires review by a **7-seat external editorial board** backed by mandatory sub-area specialist reviewers covering all `20` taxonomy areas (`101` subjects, `878` concepts) and a **12-indexer dual-human inter-indexer consistency study**.

## 3. Appointment Independence, Recusal, Quorum, and Binding Rejection Policy

1. **Appointment independence and co-authorship caps**:
   - `V1` and `V2` Chairs and Alternates are nominated jointly by the Editor and an external academic steering advisor with zero co-authorship with the Editor in the trailing 5 years (`V1` and `V2` holders must likewise have zero co-authorship with the Editor in the trailing 5 years). `V1` co-verifies that the domain Chair, Alternate, and specialist invitations satisfy institutional independence before review packets issue.
   - Every seat pairs a Chair and Alternate from distinct institutions; at most `1` voting Chair across the `7` seats may share a primary or secondary institution with the Editor, and at most `2` domain Chairs may have co-authored a publication or benchmark specification with the Editor in the trailing 5 years.
2. **Mandatory conflict-of-interest disclosure, single-vote substitution, and 3-voter escalation**:
   - Before reviewing a packet, each participant discloses (a) co-authorship or joint consortium/working-group leadership with the Editor within the trailing 5 years, (b) authorship or leadership of frameworks, papers, or standards tied to candidate mechanisms, and (c) commercial vendor affiliations.
   - Any reviewer who authored a cited warrant source, led an associated framework/standard, or co-authored the topic with the Editor **recuses from voting** on the admission, preferred label, definition, or aliasing of that item (multi-mechanism suites and benchmark consortia such as `vLLM` and `MLPerf` remain categorically excluded under Gate 3).
   - **One vote per seat & unconflicted substitution**: When a Chair recuses on an item, voting authority for that seat transfers to that seat's unconflicted Alternate (in Seat `T`, from `T1` Co-Chair to `T1` Alternate, or `T2` Co-Chair to `T2` Alternate); each of the `7` seats casts at most one vote.
   - **3-voter unconflicted escalation panel**: If **both** Chair and Alternate on a domain seat are conflicted on an item, item-level adjudication transfers to a **3-voter unconflicted panel** comprising `V1` (or `V1` Alternate if `V1` Chair is conflicted, or `V2` Chair if both `V1` holders are conflicted) plus `2` unconflicted adjacent Group Chairs (preserving one vote per panelist, decided by `>= 2` of `3` vote with zero 1–1 ties). If both `V1` Chair and `V1` Alternate are conflicted on a `V1`-scoped standards question (or both `V2` holders on `V2`), an unconflicted ad-hoc vocabulary/SKOS specialist must be appointed before that item can be approved.
   - **Recusal denominator in full-board item votes**: If a full-board item vote has $N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ unconflicted seats (after Alternate substitution), valid action requires $N_{\text{unconflicted}} \ge 5$ and affirmative votes from at least $\lceil (N_{\text{unconflicted}} + 1) / 2 \rceil$ unconflicted seats (an explicit `abstain` by an unconflicted seat stays in the denominator).
3. **Full-domain `1.0` quorum and binding rejection rule**:
   - **`1.0` Quorum**: Release sign-off requires **all `7` seats participating** (each via its unconflicted Chair or Alternate — ensuring **all `5` domain group seats `F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, `T` and both `V1` and `V2` are represented**) plus completion of all mandatory sub-area specialist reports in §2.
   - **Release approval threshold**: A `1.0` citable release requires `>= 5` of `7` seats voting `approve`, including affirmative votes from **both `V1` and `V2`** within their respective scopes.
   - **Binding exclusion of board-rejected items from `1.0`**: While the Editor holds repository maintenance authority over `0.x-draft` experimental branches, **no concept, alias, relationship, or conformance claim rejected by a board majority (or rejected within scope by `V1`, `V2`, or its responsible `T1`/`T2` specialist) may ship inside a `1.0` release**. Rejected items must either be revised and pass renewed review by the rejecting seat(s)/majority or be excluded/retired before `1.0` tags are cut, with all dispositions recorded in `taxonomy/CHANGELOG.md`.

## 4. Scoped Work Packages and Pre-Registered Dual-Human Indexing Design

1. **Domain Group Packets (`F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, and `T1`+`T2`, plus mandatory sub-area specialists)**:
   - *Subject & Concept Inventory* (**`10`–`34` subjects** and **`77`–`301` primary concepts** [`106`–`336` including secondary placements] per group in `0.4-draft`): audit missing `must`-have mechanisms backed by $\ge 2$ independent `provenance_family_id` lineages (`>= 1` `origin: external`), product/suite leaks (`Gate 3`), and Home-Rule placements.
   - *Split Hierarchy Queue (`16` edges across the taxonomy)*: adjudicate each `specializes` edge where prior model audits split using the 3-step `all-and-some` test (`Z39.19 §8.3.1`).
   - *Zero-Warrant Queue (`27` concepts across the taxonomy)*: verify external literary warrant in `S1`–`S5` or recommend retirement (`concepts_retire` with `replaced_by`) or upward-posted aliasing.
2. **Vocabulary & SKOS Specialist Packets (`V1` and `V2`)**:
   - *`V1` (Z39.19 / ISO 25964 Packet)*: verify `taxonomy/METHOD.md` clause mappings against primary standard texts; review the **`94` legacy compound concept labels** matching punctuation-safe `\b(?:and|versus|vs)\b\.?` (`95` including `\bor\b`, plus `16` legacy entry terms — `8` more `vs.` labels than the `86` matched by the earlier `\b(and|versus|vs\.)\b` scan) against `Z39.19 §7`; audit legacy `aliases` across all `1,254` entry terms (including broader `assurance case` on `mlsys.safety-case`, method-artifact `unsafe control action` / `UCA` on `mlsys.stpa`, and library name `XGBoost`) against `Z39.19 §8.2` and Gate 3; and approve the Stage 2 human indexing pre-registration before recruitment.
   - *`V2` (SKOS / RDF / Crosswalk Packet)*: verify `mlsys-taxonomy.ttl`, `mlsys-taxonomy-relations.ttl`, `mlsys-taxonomy.dcat.ttl`, URI dereferencing, conditional-edge RDF caveats, and SKOS crosswalks (`ACM CCS`, `CS2023`, `SFIA 9`, `ESCO`).
3. **Dual-Human Inter-Indexer Consistency Study (`Z39.19 §11.2.2`, `V1`-approved pre-registration required before recruitment)**:
   - **Staffing (`12` distinct domain indexers)**: Two independent human indexers (PhD candidates, postdocs, or staff ML systems engineers who did not author the taxonomy and are separate from voting Chairs) are recruited per expertise stratum — `F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, `T1`, and `T2` — totaling **`6 × 2 = 12` distinct indexers** (`0` overlap between `T1` and `T2`).
   - **Cross-group blinding & sample size (`30` Stage 1 calibration + `240` unique Stage 2 evaluation items)**:
     1. *Stage 1 (Calibration, `30` items = `5` per pair)*: Indexers practice applying `tagging_rules` (`taxonomy/README.md` across all `101` subjects), reconcile rule misunderstandings with `V1`, and freeze the indexing guide. Stage 1 items are excluded from Stage 2 scoring.
     2. *Stage 2 (Blinded Evaluation, `240` unique uninspected items across all `20` areas, $\ge 10$ items per area)*: Drawn from uninspected `origin: external` post-cutoff / `reserve` clusters (disjoint from the burned `P4`+`Q4` study pool). To prevent group-stratum cueing and test cross-group Home-Rule boundaries, **group and area headers are masked on item packets** and every indexer pair tags **`60` shuffled items** (`40` items from their primary expertise stratum + `20` cross-group boundary/anchor items shared across pairs, choosing freely from all `101` subjects and `878` concepts, yielding `6 × 60 = 360` dual-tagged item pairs across the `240` unique items).
   - **Aggregation, bootstrap unit, empty-set conventions, and numerical `1.0` acceptance floors**:
     - Each indexer assigns `1` primary subject and `0`–`3` concepts per item. Metrics are computed both pooled across all `360` dual-tagged pairs (deduplicated to `240` primary-pair items for headline point estimates, plus cross-pair anchor reliability across the `40` shared items and min/max across the `6` pairs) with **`10,000`-resample cluster-level bootstrap 95% CIs by `provenance_family_id`**.
     - *Empty-set & specificity definitions*: Conditional Hooper and Rolling average over `both non-empty` items; **all-item Hooper and Rolling** evaluate all `240` items under $J(\emptyset, \emptyset) = \text{Dice}(\emptyset, \emptyset) = 1$ and $J(A, \emptyset) = \text{Dice}(A, \emptyset) = 0$ for $A \neq \emptyset$. **Leaf-concept specificity ratio** is the share of assigned concept tags whose concept has `0` incoming `specializes` children (or where no narrower `specializes` child applies).
     - *Pre-registered `1.0` human consistency floors*: pooled 101-subject $\kappa \ge 0.65$, conditional Hooper $\ge 45.0\%$ (all-item Hooper $\ge 40.0\%$), conditional Rolling $\ge 60.0\%$ (all-item Rolling $\ge 55.0\%$), pooled $\alpha_{\text{MASI}} \ge 0.45$, empty-assignment rate $\le 20.0\%$ ($\ge 192$ of `240` items jointly non-empty and $\ge 8$ jointly indexed items in every one of the `20` areas), and max single-concept share $p_{\max} \le 15.0\%$. If any floor or per-area minimum fails, `1.0` release halts, offending subject scope notes or tagging rules are adjudicated on a separate calibration slice, and Stage 2 is rerun on fresh uninspected clusters.
