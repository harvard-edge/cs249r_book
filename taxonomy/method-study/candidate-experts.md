# External Editorial Board Charter and Human Evaluation Protocol (Z39.19 §11.1.3.1, §11.2.2, §11.3.1.1)

Status: public governance charter and role specification for version `0.4-draft` (`2026-11-02`). **This document defines seat qualifications, area coverage obligations, appointment and conflict-of-interest rules, and pre-registered human evaluation specifications only; no external board has yet been convened, no individual has been contacted or asked to endorse the ML Systems Taxonomy, and unconsented personal names are intentionally excluded from the public repository.**

---

## 1. Purpose and Historical Context

Under **ANSI/NISO Z39.19-2005 (R2010) §11.1.3.1** (*Committee Approach*), **§11.2.2** (*Evaluation Criteria*), and **§11.3.1.1** (*Addition of Terms*), a controlled vocabulary intended for community reuse combines empirical corpus extraction with review by domain subject specialists and controlled-vocabulary specialists.

In `0.4-draft`, construction and verification used language-model passes under deterministic reconciliation and LinkML compilation gates, with final decisions made by a single human Editor (Vijay Janapa Reddi): specifically, the `0.4-draft` refresh ran Round 1 as seven parallel lenses in one model family (`claude-fable-5-1`) followed by a three-family Round 2 cross-check and independent audit (`gpt-6.1-sol`, `gemini-4-argon-high`, `claude-opus-5-5`), while three-family blind discovery and reconciliation were evaluated on the pre-registered Phase 3–7 two-area study (`P4` and `Q4`) and codified for future refreshes.

To transition from an `editorial draft` (`0.x-draft`) to a citable community release (`1.0`), the project requires review by a **7-seat external editorial board** backed by mandatory sub-area specialist reviewers covering all `20` taxonomy areas (`101` subjects, `878` concepts) and a **fixed 12-indexer (`6`-pair) dual-human inter-indexer consistency study**.

## 3. Appointment Independence, Recusal, Quorum, and Binding Board-Rejection Exclusion Rule

1. **Independent appointment chain, non-Editor administrative convenor, and standing-roster vs. active-voting co-authorship caps**:
   - **Independent non-Editor convenor, ex-officio nominating panel, & open-nomination fallback**: Outreach to form the ex-officio nominating panel is administered by an **independent non-Editor administrative convenor** (an unconflicted active `NISO` / `DCMI` / `W3C` working-group co-chair or academic society open-science officer from an institution disjoint from the Editor's institution, with zero co-authorship or joint grant/consortium leadership with the Editor in the trailing 5 years; **neither the Editor nor any member of the Editor's lab or institution plays any role in selecting, contacting, or filtering the nominating panel**). Before any board invitations issue, that convenor forms an independent 3-scholar nominating panel ex officio by inviting, in deterministic reverse-chronological order of service, the most recent willing program or general chairs of (i) `MLSys` (fallback: `EuroSys` / `NSDI`), (ii) `OSDI` or `SOSP` (fallback: `ASPLOS` / `ISCA`), and (iii) an active `NISO` / `DCMI` / `W3C` knowledge-organization or SKOS working group (fallback: `ISKO` / `ASIS&T` SIG/CR chairs), each from a distinct institution disjoint from the Editor's institution and each having zero co-authorship or joint grant/consortium leadership with the Editor in the trailing 5 years. **Deterministic open-nomination fallback**: if any of the `3` nominating seats remains unfilled after `30` calendar days across both primary and fallback venue lists, the administrative convenor opens a public `21`-day community/self-nomination call for scholars meeting the seat's domain and independence criteria and selects among verified-eligible nominees via a pre-announced public randomness beacon (`SHA-256` of the first NIST Randomness Beacon pulse after the `21`-day window closes). That 3-scholar nominating panel appoints by $\ge 2/3$ vote an **external academic steering advisor** (tenured faculty member or standards-body working-group chair from an institution disjoint from the Editor's institution, with zero co-authorship or joint grant/consortium leadership with the Editor in the trailing 5 years).
   - **Independent `V1`/`V2` and domain Chair nomination & steering-advisor recusal rule**: The external academic steering advisor is bound by the **identical COI disclosure and item/candidate recusal rules** in §3.2; whenever the steering advisor is conflicted or unavailable on a candidate appointment or ad-hoc voter selection, an unconflicted member of the 3-scholar ex-officio nominating panel (selected in deterministic seat order `(iii) -> (i) -> (ii)`) substitutes for the steering advisor so the 3-person appointing committee retains `3` unconflicted voters. The external academic steering advisor (or unconflicted nominating-panel substitute) nominates `V1` and `V2` Chairs and Alternates (all `4` must be distinct natural persons from distinct non-Editor institutions with zero co-authorship with the Editor in the trailing 5 years; the Editor may surface scope context in writing but holds no appointment or veto power). Once seated, **`{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor (or unconflicted substitute)}`** appoint all domain Chairs, `T1`/`T2` Co-Chairs, Alternates, and the `13` mandatory sub-area specialists by a $\ge 2/3$ vote (`>= 2` of `3` unconflicted members concurring; if `V1 Chair` or `V2 Chair` is conflicted on a candidate, their unconflicted `V1`/`V2` Alternate acts on that appointment, paired with mandatory verification by both `V1` and the steering advisor that distinct-person, institutional, and co-authorship caps hold before review packets issue).
   - **Standing-roster caps vs. dynamic active-voting caps**: Every seat pairs a Chair (or `T1`/`T2` Co-Chair) and Alternate from distinct institutions:
     - Across the **16-person standing roster** (`6` Chairs + `2` `T1`/`T2` Co-Chairs + `8` Alternates): at most **`2` standing-roster members** (`<= 1` Chair/Co-Chair and `<= 1` Alternate) may share a primary or secondary institution with the Editor, and at most **`3` domain standing-roster members** (`<= 2` Chairs/Co-Chairs and `<= 1` Alternate) may have co-authored a publication or benchmark specification with the Editor in the trailing 5 years.
     - On **every active vote** — re-verified dynamically on full-board item votes (including reduced-$N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ votes), `1.0` release sign-off votes, and 3-voter escalation panels, counting any Alternate acting in a voting capacity and counting Seat `T`'s `T1` and `T2` holders individually — at most **`1` active voting holder** may share a primary or secondary institution with the Editor, and at most **`2` active domain voting holders** (`<= 1` on a 3-voter escalation panel) may have co-authored a publication or benchmark specification with the Editor in the trailing 5 years. If substituting an Alternate would breach an active-voting cap, on an item vote that Alternate is skipped in favor of the unconflicted 3-voter panel below (or next eligible circular seat), and on a `1.0` release sign-off vote (or when $N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ would drop below `5`) `{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}` appoint by $\ge 2/3$ vote an unconflicted ad-hoc external voter with zero Editor co-authorship and a non-Editor institution to represent that seat so `7/7` quorum and active-voting caps hold simultaneously.
2. **Mandatory conflict-of-interest disclosure, specialist substitution, and deterministic 3-voter escalation**:
   - Before reviewing a packet, each Chair, Co-Chair, Alternate, and sub-area specialist discloses (a) co-authorship or joint consortium/working-group leadership with the Editor within the trailing 5 years, (b) authorship or leadership of frameworks, papers, or standards tied to candidate mechanisms, and (c) commercial vendor affiliations.
   - Any participant who authored a cited warrant source, led an associated framework/standard, or co-authored the topic with the Editor **recuses** from evaluating or voting on the admission, preferred label, definition, or aliasing of that item (multi-mechanism suites and benchmark consortia such as `vLLM` and `MLPerf` remain categorically excluded under Gate 3). If any of the `13` mandatory sub-area specialists (`F3`, `S4`, `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, `P5`, `Q1`, `Q2`, `Q3`, `Q4a`, `Q4b`, `Q5`, `Q6`) is conflicted on an item in their sub-area, `{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}` appoint an unconflicted substitute specialist (distinct natural person) for those items so no sub-area item goes unreviewed.
   - **One vote per seat & unconflicted substitution**: When a Chair recuses on an item, voting authority for that seat transfers to that seat's unconflicted Alternate (in Seat `T`, from `T1` Co-Chair to `T1` Alternate, or `T2` Co-Chair to `T2` Alternate); each of the `7` seats casts at most one vote.
   - **Deterministic 3-voter unconflicted escalation panel**: If **both** Chair and Alternate on a domain seat $X \in \{F, S, P, Q\}$ are conflicted on an item — or in Seat `T`, if **both `T1` Co-Chair and `T1` Alternate** are conflicted on a `T1`-scoped item, **both `T2` Co-Chair and `T2` Alternate** are conflicted on a `T2`-scoped item, or a cross-`T1`/`T2` item leaves either half without an unconflicted holder — `V1` convenes a deterministic **3-voter unconflicted panel** (satisfying the active-voting caps of `<= 1` Editor-institution voter and `<= 1` trailing-5-year Editor co-author across the `3` panelists) comprising:
     1. `V1` Chair (or `V1` Alternate if `V1` Chair is conflicted, or `V2` Chair / `V2` Alternate if both `V1` holders are conflicted; if **all `4` vocabulary holders across `V1` and `V2`** are conflicted on that item, the external academic steering advisor appoints an ad-hoc unconflicted external KOS/SKOS specialist voter from a non-Editor institution with zero Editor co-authorship in the trailing 5 years before the panel convenes), plus
     2. The first **`2` unconflicted domain seats** scanning forward from $X$ in the fixed circular order **`F -> S -> P -> Q -> T -> F`** whose participation preserves the panel's active-voting caps, represented by each seat's unconflicted Chair (or unconflicted Alternate if its Chair is conflicted; when Seat `T` serves as an escalation panelist, unconflicted `T1` and `T2` holders cast a single joint vote if they concur, or the scan advances to the next unconflicted domain seat in circular order if either half of `T` is conflicted or split).
     Each panelist casts one vote (`>= 2` of `3` required to approve, zero 1–1 ties). **Fallback halt rule**: if fewer than `2` eligible unconflicted domain seats exist across the board on an item (or if both `V1` holders are conflicted on a `V1`-scoped standards question, or both `V2` holders on a `V2`-scoped SKOS question), `{V1 Chair, V2 Chair, external academic steering advisor}` (via the unconflicted members plus steering advisor) must appoint unconflicted ad-hoc external specialist voter(s) (distinct natural persons, non-Editor institution, zero Editor co-authorship in the trailing 5 years) before that item can be approved; otherwise the item is withheld from `1.0`.
   - **Recusal denominator in full-board item votes**: If a full-board item vote has $N_{\text{unconflicted}}$ unconflicted seats satisfying the active-voting caps (after Alternate or ad-hoc substitution), valid action requires $N_{\text{unconflicted}} \ge 5$ and affirmative votes from at least $\lceil (N_{\text{unconflicted}} + 1) / 2 \rceil$ unconflicted seats (an explicit `abstain` by an unconflicted seat stays in the denominator).
3. **Full-domain `1.0` quorum and binding board-rejection exclusion rule**:
   - **`1.0` Quorum**: Release sign-off requires **all `7` seats participating** (each via its unconflicted Chair or Alternate — ensuring **all `5` domain group seats `F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, `T` and both `V1` and `V2` are represented**) plus completion of all mandatory sub-area specialist reports in §2.
   - **Release approval threshold**: A `1.0` citable release requires `>= 5` of `7` seats voting `approve`, including affirmative votes from **both `V1` and `V2`** within their respective scopes.
   - **Binding board-rejection exclusion rule**: While the Editor holds repository maintenance authority over `0.x-draft` experimental branches, **no concept, alias, relationship, lifecycle schema change, or conformance claim rejected by a board majority (or rejected within scope by `V1`, `V2`, or its responsible `T1`/`T2` holder) may ship inside a `1.0` release**. Rejected items must either be revised and pass renewed review by the rejecting seat(s)/majority or be excluded/retired before `1.0` tags are cut, with all dispositions recorded in `taxonomy/CHANGELOG.md`.

## 4. Scoped Work Packages and Pre-Registered Dual-Human Indexing Design

1. **Domain Group Packets (`F`, `S`, `P`, `Q`, and `T1`+`T2`, plus mandatory sub-area specialists)**:
   - *Subject & Concept Inventory* (**`10`–`34` subjects** and **`77`–`301` primary concepts** [`106`–`336` including secondary placements] per group in `0.4-draft`): audit missing `must`-have mechanisms backed by $\ge 2$ independent `provenance_family_id` lineages (`>= 1` non-editor-affiliated `origin: external`), product/suite leaks (`Gate 3`), and Home-Rule placements.
   - *Split Hierarchy Queue (`16` edges across the taxonomy)*: adjudicate each `specializes` edge where prior model audits split using the 3-step `all-and-some` test (`Z39.19 §8.3.1`).
   - *Zero-Warrant Queue (`27` concepts across the taxonomy)*: verify external literary warrant in `S1`–`S5` or recommend retirement (`concepts_retire` with `replaced_by`) or typed aliasing.
2. **Vocabulary & SKOS Specialist Packets (`V1` and `V2`)**:
   - *`V1` (Z39.19 / ISO 25964 Packet)*: verify `taxonomy/METHOD.md` clause mappings against primary standard texts (specifically auditing all ISO 25964-1/-2 pinpoints); review the **`94` legacy compound concept labels** matching punctuation-safe `\b(?:and|versus|vs)\b\.?` (`95` including `\bor\b`) and **`16` legacy compound non-preferred entry terms (`aliases`, `110` combined across all `2,129` preferred labels and aliases)** against `Z39.19 §7`; audit legacy `aliases` across all **`1,251` non-preferred entry terms** (`2,129` total preferred labels + entry terms, including all **`7` pre-sidecar `0.4-draft` concepts / `8` legacy aliases** tracked under `grandfathered_draft_alias_exceptions` in `taxonomy/term-provenance.yml` with status `pre-sidecar-legacy-queued-for-v1-audit` — broader `assurance case` on `mlsys.safety-case`, implementation name `XGBoost` on `mlsys.tree-ensemble`, unattested `grammar-guided decoding` on `mlsys.constrained-decoding`, named algorithm/mechanism aliases `ZeRO` and `FSDP` on `mlsys.state-sharding`, `PagedAttention` on `mlsys.paged-kv`, `CUDA Graphs` on `mlsys.graph-replay`, and `CUDA streams` on `mlsys.device-streams`, each of which must either acquire full Gate 5 `primary_excerpt` attestation or be removed with tombstones prior to `1.0`) against `Z39.19 §8.2` and Gate 3; review prospective `1.0` `RetiredConcept` schema slots (`resolution_class`, `split_into`); and approve the Stage 2 human indexing pre-registration before recruitment.
   - *`V2` (SKOS / RDF / Crosswalk Packet)*: verify `mlsys-taxonomy.ttl`, `mlsys-taxonomy-relations.ttl`, `mlsys-taxonomy.dcat.ttl`, URI dereferencing, conditional-edge RDF caveats, and SKOS crosswalks (`ACM CCS`, `CS2023`, `SFIA 9`, `ESCO`).
3. **Dual-Human Inter-Indexer Consistency Study (`Z39.19 §11.2.2`, `V1`-approved pre-registration required before recruitment)**:
   - **Fixed `12`-indexer (`6`-pair) staffing & Editor-independence exclusion**: Two independent human indexers (PhD candidates, postdocs, or staff ML systems engineers who did not author the taxonomy, are distinct natural persons disjoint from all `29` standing/specialist board roles, the steering advisor, and any ad-hoc escalation voters, are **not current or former PhD/postdoc advisees, lab members, or institutional direct reports of the Editor**, and have **zero co-authorship with the Editor within the trailing `36` months**) are recruited per expertise stratum — `F` (`3` areas), `S` (`4` areas), `P` (`5` areas), `Q` (`6` areas), `T1` (`1` area), and `T2` (`1` area) — totaling **`6 × 2 = 12` distinct indexers** (`0` overlap across pairs). **The `6`-pair design is fixed** (changing pair count would alter the `40 × 6` cross-group anchor incidence matrix and `8`-rater statistics below, and is disallowed without a new `V1`-approved pre-registration prior to recruitment).
   - **Area-balanced allocation (`30` Stage 1 calibration + `240` unique Stage 2 items = `12` per area across all `20` areas; Stage 1 and Stage 2 completely cluster-disjoint)**:
     1. *Stage 1 (Calibration, `30` items = `5` per pair, cluster-disjoint from Stage 2)*: Drawn from unexposed `origin: external` clusters **completely disjoint by `provenance_family_id` from Stage 2's evaluation clusters** (and disjoint from burned `dev` clusters). Indexers practice applying `tagging_rules` (`taxonomy/README.md` across all `101` subjects), reconcile rule misunderstandings with `V1`, and freeze the indexing guide before Stage 2 packets are unsealed. Stage 1 items and clusters are excluded from Stage 2 scoring.
     2. *Stage 2 Primary Allocation (`12` unique items/area $\times$ `20` areas = **`240` unique items**, `<= 1` item per cluster per area)*: Drawn from unexposed non-editor-affiliated `origin: external` post-cutoff / `reserve` clusters (cluster-disjoint from Stage 1 and from `taxonomy/method-study/burned-clusters.json`, sampling **at most `1` item per canonical `provenance_family_id` cluster within any single area** so every area spans `12` distinct clusters). Allocating **by area first (`12` allocated items in every one of the `20` areas)** exceeds the `>= 10` allocated items/area design rule everywhere (including `P`'s `5` areas and `Q`'s `6` areas) and provides a `4`-item buffer per area above the separate `>= 8` jointly non-empty (`both_non_empty`) per-area evaluation floor (`80%` non-empty $\times 12 = 9.6 \ge 8$). Primary-pair item counts sum to `240`: `F = 36` (`3×12`), `S = 48` (`4×12`), `P = 60` (`5×12`), `Q = 72` (`6×12`), `T1 = 12` (`1×12`), `T2 = 12` (`1×12`).
     3. *Deterministic Item-Level `40`-Anchor Cross-Group Routing (`2` anchors/area = `40` anchor items $\times$ `3` secondary pairs = **`120` secondary evaluations**; **`360` total pair-evaluations**)*: Within each of the `20` areas, the first `2` stratified items sorted by `(area_code, unit_id)` are designated as anchors (`40` anchor items total: `Q = 12`, `P = 10`, `S = 8`, `F = 6`, `T1 = 2`, `T2 = 2`). Within each source group $g$ ($k = 0 \dots n_g - 1$ sorted by `(area_code, unit_id)`), item $k$ is routed deterministically to the `3` distinct non-primary pairs having the largest remaining quota in group $g$'s target row vector, breaking ties by canonical pair order `[F, S, P, Q, T1, T2]`, producing the exact item-level `3`-receiver schedule below (`row sum = 3` per anchor item, `column sum = 20` incoming anchor items for every one of the `6` pairs):
        - `Q` (`12` anchors, row sums `{F: 8, S: 7, P: 7, T1: 7, T2: 7}`): `k=0:[F,S,P]`, `k=1:[F,T1,T2]`, `k=2:[F,S,P]`, `k=3:[F,T1,T2]`, `k=4:[S,P,T1]`, `k=5:[F,S,T2]`, `k=6:[P,T1,T2]`, `k=7:[F,S,P]`, `k=8:[F,T1,T2]`, `k=9:[S,P,T1]`, `k=10:[F,S,T2]`, `k=11:[P,T1,T2]` (`36` secondary evaluations).
        - `P` (`10` anchors, row sums `{F: 6, S: 6, Q: 6, T1: 6, T2: 6}`): `k=0:[F,S,Q]`, `k=1:[F,T1,T2]`, `k=2:[S,Q,T1]`, `k=3:[F,S,T2]`, `k=4:[Q,T1,T2]`, `k=5:[F,S,Q]`, `k=6:[F,T1,T2]`, `k=7:[S,Q,T1]`, `k=8:[F,S,T2]`, `k=9:[Q,T1,T2]` (`30` secondary evaluations).

   - **Multi-pair estimators, global post-stratified cluster bootstrap, specificity definition, and `1.0` acceptance floors**:
     - *Headline `240`-item primary-pair estimators, deterministic cluster-bootstrap weight formula, and degenerate-metric rule*: Headline release-gate point estimates evaluate the **fixed `240` primary-pair item assignments** (`1` pre-designated primary pair $P_g = (r_{g,1}, r_{g,2})$ per item, `12` items/area $\times$ `20` areas across `12` distinct clusters per area, preventing anchor duplication bias and within-area cluster concentration). Because `6` distinct rater pairs index disjoint strata, raw pooled Cohen's $\kappa$ would depend on arbitrary rater-1 vs. rater-2 ordering within each pair; accordingly, `101`-subject agreement is evaluated using **both orientation-invariant Krippendorff's nominal $\alpha_{\text{subject}}$ across the `240` paired subject assignments** and the **item-count-weighted pair-stratified Cohen's $\bar{\kappa}_{\text{subject}} = \sum_{g \in \{F,S,P,Q,T1,T2\}} (n_g / 240)\,\kappa_g$**, alongside pooled and equal-area-weighted `20`-area macro-average Hooper, Rolling, and $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$. Because an effective `provenance_family_id` cluster (`< 50%` overlap across all listed authors) can span multiple areas across different groups, all uncertainty intervals use a **pre-registered `10,000`-resample (`seed = 20270101`) global cluster-level bootstrap over the $M$ canonical `provenance_family_id` clusters spanning the `240` items**: in each resample drawing cluster $c_m$ $k_m$ times with replacement ($\sum_{m=1}^M k_m = M$), each item $i \in C_m$ receives cluster-multiplicity weight $w_i^* = k_m$, post-stratified within area $a(i) \in \{1,\dots,20\}$ as $\tilde{w}_i^* = \frac{1/20}{\sum_{j \in \text{area } a(i)} w_j^*} w_i^*$ (which reduces identically to the `1/240` point-estimate weight when $k_m = 1$ for all $m$, redrawing any resample where $\sum_{j \in \text{area } a} w_j^* = 0$ for any area $a$), preserving exact $1/20$ area weights and $n_g / 240$ group weights across every resample. **Degenerate-marginal convention**: if expected disagreement $D_e = 0$ in Krippendorff's $\alpha$ (or expected agreement $p_e = 1$ in Cohen's/Fleiss' $\kappa$) within a stratum or resample, define $\alpha = 1.0$ ($\kappa = 1.0$) when observed disagreement $D_o = 0$ ($p_o = 1$) and $\alpha = 0.0$ ($\kappa = 0.0$) when $D_o > 0$, flagging any zero-variance stratum explicitly in the study report.
     - *Cross-pair `40`-anchor multi-rater reliability*: Reported separately across the `40` anchor items (`4` pairs = `8` human indexers per anchor item: `1` primary + `3` secondary pairs), computing `8`-rater Fleiss' $\kappa$ (and Krippendorff's nominal $\alpha$) on `101`-subject assignment and `8`-rater Krippendorff's $\alpha_{\text{MASI}}$ on concept sets to measure cross-group Home-Rule boundary stability without inflating the `240`-item headline sample.
     - *Empty-set & unified leaf-specificity definitions*: Conditional Hooper and Rolling average over `both non-empty` primary-pair items; **all-item Hooper and Rolling** evaluate all `240` items under $J(\emptyset, \emptyset) = \text{Dice}(\emptyset, \emptyset) = 1$ and $J(A, \emptyset) = \text{Dice}(A, \emptyset) = 0$ for $A \neq \emptyset$. **Leaf-concept specificity ratio** is defined uniformly as the share of assigned concept tags whose concept has **`0` incoming `specializes` children** in `ml-systems-taxonomy.yml`.
     - *Pre-registered `1.0` human consistency floors & single-recalibration cap (on the `240` primary-pair items)*: orientation-invariant `101`-subject $\alpha_{\text{subject}} \ge 0.65$ **and** pair-stratified $\bar{\kappa}_{\text{subject}} \ge 0.65$, conditional Hooper $\ge 45.0\%$ (all-item Hooper $\ge 40.0\%$), conditional Rolling $\ge 60.0\%$ (all-item Rolling $\ge 55.0\%$), pooled $\alpha_{\text{MASI}} \ge 0.45$, empty-assignment rate $\le 20.0\%$ ($\ge 192$ of `240` items jointly non-empty and $\ge 8$ of `12` items jointly non-empty in every one of the `20` areas), and max single-concept share $p_{\max} \le 15.0\%$. If any floor or per-area minimum fails, `1.0` release halts, offending subject scope notes or tagging rules are adjudicated on a separate cluster-disjoint calibration slice, and Stage 2 may be rerun **at most once (`max 1` recalibration rerun)** on fresh unexposed external clusters; if the rerun still misses any floor, `1.0` halts for a full taxonomy structural revision cycle rather than repeated re-testing.
