# Editorial dispositions

These decisions concern an editorial draft. They are source-backed model-assisted judgments, not human consensus. Exact accepted records are replayed in the provenance chain; original review proposals remain historical evidence.

| Matter | Current disposition |
|---|---|
| Coalescing and bank conflicts | Split the compound entry. Retain `mlsys.memory-coalescing` for global-memory coalescing; add `mlsys.shared-memory-bank-conflict` for bank serialization. Give the latter its own bandwidth relationship. Affected StaffML assignments received a scope screen; bank-conflict questions now use the new identity. |

Do not turn one regulatory risk-reduction principle into a universal definition. Remove the unsupported integrity-level determination. |

| Functional insufficiency | Retain the corrected concept. Withdraw the five disputed incident claims because the inspected evidence does not support their specific causal or detection meanings. An isolated semantic node remains accessible through its subject home. Withdrawal does not establish that no valid engineering relationship could be added later. |
| Sampling and stopping terms | Classify individual controls as narrower entry terms. They remain searchable but are not exported as synonyms. |
| Export, encoder, and accelerator-sharing terms | Classify formats/APIs and MIG as implementations, SR-IOV as related, and modality-specific terms as narrower. Preserve retrieval without claiming equivalence. |
| Watermarking terms | Treat text watermarking as narrower than generated-content watermarking. Attest output watermarking as an equivalent term only within the declared generated-content scope. |
| Legacy terms elsewhere | Preserve search entry terms with equivalence explicitly unasserted. Their presence no longer automatically emits SKOS alternative labels. The inventory exposes missing term decisions rather than claiming a completed semantic review. |
| External mappings | No exact match is asserted without target version, concept scope comparison, evidence, and an explicit mapping decision. |
| Historical study | Preserve artifacts and recompute only what their inputs support. Do not use model summaries as evidence of human usability, preregistration, isolation, or community endorsement. |

The boundary and term decisions are in [accepted-boundaries.json](boundary-resolution/accepted-boundaries.json) and [accepted-entry-terms.json](boundary-resolution/accepted-entry-terms.json). The [inventory](inventory.json) records every current concept and relationship, targeted review coverage, lexical diagnostics, sparse concepts, and affected StaffML assignments. The [assignment decisions](boundary-resolution/assignment-decisions.json) preserve before/after tags and hashes; this scope screen does not validate the question answers or their arithmetic. Mechanical completeness is not assertion-level semantic verification.
