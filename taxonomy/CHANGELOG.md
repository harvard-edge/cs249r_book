# ML Systems Taxonomy changelog

Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR-draft` until the first reviewed release (once `1.0` ships, every vocabulary or entry-term edit bumps at least a patch version such as `1.0.1`). Historical process counts narrated in earlier `0.2-draft`, `0.3-draft`, and `0.4-draft` bullets (such as intermediate reviewer proposal totals and historical diff counts) are archival campaign narratives from those earlier commits; metrics recomputed from repository files and study JSON logs carry verified receipt status in `taxonomy/method-study/study-receipts-summary.json`.

**Identifier policy.** Concept identities (`mlsys.kv-cache`) are permanent: they are never renamed or reused. A concept that is merged or withdrawn is listed under `retired` in `taxonomy/ml-systems-taxonomy.yml` with the identity that replaces it, and links to it redirect. Readable codes (`P4.5`) are stable within a minor version and may be renumbered in a new minor or major version; tools should store identities, not codes. Relation identities (`limits`, `reduces`) follow the same rule as concepts.
