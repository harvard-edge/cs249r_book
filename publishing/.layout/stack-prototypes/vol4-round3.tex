{\footnotesize\textbf{Where the reviewers agreed.} Kill the five-step tint ramp for a binary
one. Drop the per-rung icons and ratify vol3's decision as the series standard rather than
letting vol4 become a third variant. Keep the rate line, because vertical position encodes
order but not interval, and these intervals span four orders of magnitude; but subordinate it.
Do not manufacture a rotated spine for family symmetry --- vol4 has no genuine cross-cutting
quantity, and the absence is informative. Reject the lollipop outright: an axis asserts a
continuum with equal steps, which is the wrongest available metaphor for authority tiers.}
