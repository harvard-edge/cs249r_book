Where MLSys·im Clicks in the Textbook

How first-principles physics connects chapters, study sheets, and lecture slides across the Machine Learning Systems series.

MLSys·im is the quantitative engine behind the Machine Learning Systems curriculum. Rather than treating single-chip kernels, distributed training clusters, interactive serving fleets, and cyber-physical control loops as disconnected subjects, MLSys·im models them with the same typed physical quantities—FLOPs, bytes, bandwidth, latency, energy, and failure rates.

Use this map in two ways:

  1. Follow a physical constraint across volumes (Seven Cross-Volume Threads) to see how one equation—such as the Roofline bound or KV-cache byte growth—evolves as systems scale up and out.
  2. Look up any textbook chapter (Chapter-by-Chapter Lookup) to jump straight to its companion MLSys·im solvers, hands-on tutorials, study sheet, and lecture slides.

Suggested workflow: Read the chapter section for the conceptual model → predict which ceiling binds → test your prediction with the paired MLSys·im tutorial or solver → check your understanding with the chapter’s study sheet.
