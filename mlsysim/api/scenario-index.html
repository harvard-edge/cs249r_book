Scenario Classes A–Z

Calculation scenarios bind named inputs to a reusable calculation. These pages document the classes in mlsysim/engine/scenario_definitions/, including their equations, exclusions, examples, and calculated properties. Select instances through mlsysim.Scenarios; the class name describes their shared contract, not a separate registry entry.

Browse scenarios by domain when you know the modeling question; use this alphabetical list when you know a class name.

The generic Scenario scorecard is a different entry point. See Choosing a Python API before selecting one, and the Scenarios Zoo for registry use.

This directory is generated from authored public classes. Inherited methods are documented on BaseScenario; a class page shows the properties and methods that it defines itself.

A

  • AcceleratedFractionScenario: Amdahl’s law with a path gain set by two rates: fraction of the baseline time runs accelerated_rate / baseline_rate times faster.
  • AcceleratorComparison: candidate against baseline: peak, power, and peak per watt.
  • AcceleratorGenerationsScenario: Peak compute against memory bandwidth across accelerators, first to last.
  • AcceleratorInputFeedScenario: accelerators copies of hardware training workload at sustained_rate samples a second each, fed from supply with encoded_sample bytes stored per sample and decoded_sample bytes after decode.
  • AcceleratorPodScenario: A pod envelope of pod.chips copies of accelerator: aggregate peak and memory.
  • AcceptedTaskCostScenario: An attempt of model tokens plus other cost, retried until accepted; optional levers on it.
  • AccuracyDecayScenario: A deployed model read at initial_accuracy and, elapsed later, at later_accuracy; noticed is when someone outside monitoring saw it. decay_rate fits A(t) = A0 exp(-gamma t) through the two readings.
  • AccuracyFrontierScenario: A hypothetical accuracy-against-disparity frontier, floor + span (1 - exp(-rate x)), with named candidate disparities on it.
  • ActivationLogicCostScenario: Logic cost of two activation datapaths, in transistor equivalents (an illustrative budget).
  • ActivationMemoryScenario: Activation memory of workload at a microbatch, under each recomputation strategy.
  • ActiveLearningBudgetScenario: Active learning against a fixed label budget when scoring the pool is charged to the same budget.
  • ActiveLearningRoundsScenario: Active learning from initial_labels adding labels_per_round for rounds rounds, each round paying round_scoring_cost to score the pool, against random labeling that needs random_labels at the same label_price.
  • AdaptationPaybackScenario: A one-time adaptation that cuts each task’s model usage, against its incremental cost.
  • AdmissionOrderScenario: Independent admission checks (pass probability, slot time) run in each of orders.
  • AdversarialTrainingPassesScenario: Work per batch of adversarial training, in standard forward-backward passes (equal cost, no reuse).
  • AgreementDriftScenario: A labeling campaign’s kappa at a baseline and again after elapsed.
  • AlignmentMemoryScenario: PPO and DPO alignment of policy with critic-sized reward and value models.
  • AllReduceScheduleScenario: One buffer reduced by ring, simple tree, and parameter server at each of group_sizes.
  • AllToAllDispatchScenario: One expert-parallel dispatch: total_tokens of hidden_dim values over n_devices holding n_experts.
  • AllocationAxesScenario: One output ceiling spent as one chain, candidates streams, or rounds of feedback.
  • AllocationGrowthScenario: A compute-optimal allocation X_opt ~ C^exponent when compute grows by compute_multiple.
  • AlphaBetaScenario: Named messages on named paths, each path an alpha startup and a beta bandwidth.
  • AlwaysOnDetectorTargetsScenario: An always-on detector’s product contract: what it must catch, how rarely it may wake falsely, what it may occupy on the device, and the data budget and schedule that must deliver it.
  • AmdahlScenario: A sequential request path whose stage is made speedup times faster.
  • AnnFootprintScenario: Resident bytes of a graph index and of product-quantization codes over one corpus.
  • AnnotatorAgreementScenario: Two independent annotators with the same label mix class_shares: chance agreement, and Cohen’s kappa at an observed_agreement.
  • AppendOnlyContextScenario: A trajectory that appends each turn to its prompt and resends the whole history.
  • ApprovalEscrowScenario: Approval escrow for a long-running workload (held resources) and an urgent one (time).
  • ArchitectureChoiceScenario: Single-call designs and a bounded loop under one check, with a human fallback for rejects.
  • ArgmaxNoiseScenario: Argmax over equally good candidates with Gaussian score noise; a rare loophole in the pool.
  • ArgumentConfidenceScenario: A stipulated safety argument: premises required premises at premise_confidence each, and a claim carried by legs independent legs at leg_confidence each.
  • ArmComparisonScenario: Two policies run arm_runs times each, with successes_a and successes_b successes; Fisher’s exact test, and the runs per arm that separate p_high from p_low two-sided at alpha with power.
  • AttemptGroupContrastScenario: attempts independent attempts per fixture at each of pass_rates.
  • AttentionDilutionScenario: One attention head with fixed logits: a target among background keys, optionally look-alikes.
  • AttentionTilingScenario: One head’s attention at seq_len tokens, whole or in square tiles, and what each kernel stores.
  • AutomationBiasScenario: A human reviews an AI’s output; the human accepts miss_acceptance of the AI’s misses.
  • AutomationBreakevenScenario: A pipeline that costs build_time once, against manual_rate of work by hand; the pipeline itself needs upkeep_rate. Break-even is build / (manual - upkeep).
  • AvailabilityScenario: A job that needs all n_components, each up a fraction availability of the time.

B

  • BandwidthDelayScenario: Data a link carries during delay: line rate times delay (for example, a Go-Back-N resend window).
  • BaseScenario: Base for calculation scenarios: a name, a description, and stated inputs.
  • BatchCapChoiceScenario: Two batch caps at one arrival rate, batches waiting until full: throughput against formation wait.
  • BatchDispatchScenario: A dual-trigger batch: it may leave at timeout after its oldest arrival, then waits for a worker.
  • BatchEnergyPerImageScenario: Bytes, arithmetic intensity, and energy per image when a batch shares one weight read.
  • BatchServingCostScenario: One accelerator rented at price, serving batches back to back: cost per request by batch size.
  • BatchVersusStreamingIngestScenario: One event stream ingested by always-on streaming cores or by a batch job each hour.
  • BatchWindowScenario: Requests arriving as a Poisson stream into a batching window of length window.
  • BatchingKneeScenario: Batch service time S(B) = t_0 + B t_req: where throughput turns over.
  • BatteryBudgetScenario: A continuous power_draw against a battery and a required runtime.
  • BeliefExpiryScenario: A belief with initial error, growth law, and task tolerance; optionally admitted at use_age under age_bound.
  • BestOfNPoolScenario: Pools of independent candidates at pass_rate, and a mixed workload with the same average.
  • BiasFeedbackScenario: A gap passed at launch, refit from the next audit, iterated over retraining cycles: gap(k) = launch_gap * factor^k against a release gate (all in percentage points).
  • BinShareShiftScenario: A feature binned at the training quantiles: training shares against recent shares, scored by PSI.
  • BinomialIntervalsScenario: Several success counts out of one number of independent tasks.
  • BlindRetryScenario: Mutating calls retried once after any timeout, with independent request and reply loss.
  • Bm25PairScenario: One query term scored by BM25 in a short and a long document of one corpus.
  • BoundedLiabilityAdmissionScenario: The next model call of a trajectory, tested against its dollar allowance.
  • BrakingBlendScenario: A bumpless transfer into braking on a site’s base at speed, and an arm joint’s blend.
  • BrakingCeilingsScenario: The ceilings on site’s credible deceleration for its loaded machine at the drive limit.
  • BudgetMatchedLabelingScenario: A pool to label at label_price under budget: random labeling spends the budget, active learning reaches the same quality with active_labels.
  • BulkheadAdmissionScenario: One burst of blocking calls to a stalled dependency, uncapped and with a bulkhead.

C

  • CanFdBusScenario: num_axes servo axes on one CAN FD bus, frames_per_axis frames each per cycle.
  • CanaryScheduleScenario: A staged rollout that needs samples_per_variant guardrail samples per stage at request_rate; stages is the configured ladder of (traffic share, duration).
  • CappedSelectionCostScenario: Candidates for one fixed task, an exact checker, and a cap on candidates.
  • CarbonPricedPlacementScenario: A run of it_energy on fleet placed among regions with and without a carbon price.
  • CascadeEnergyScenario: An edge model on every input, escalating a fraction to a cloud model over a radio link.
  • CenteredRewardScenario: Binary terminal rewards centered on a fixed baseline.
  • CertifiedRadiusScenario: Binary randomized smoothing at noise noise_sd, against an l-infinity budget on one input.
  • CheckerPosteriorScenario: A fallible check over candidates, defect_rate of which violate the property.
  • CheckpointIntervalScenario: Checkpoints of workload written at write_bandwidth, against failures every mtbf.
  • CheckpointStormScenario: Every one of replicas writes workload’s full resumable state through one ingest path.
  • CheckpointTraceScenario: Checkpoint strides priced on turns turns that crash after the turns in crashes.
  • ChunkReplacementScenario: Replacing machine’s admitted chunk at site: capture-to-admission latency against the renewal period and the chunk lease, with an interference_tail on the inference pass.
  • ChunkedPrefillScenario: A long prompt advanced chunk_tokens per iteration beside decode.
  • ClearanceQueryScenario: Sphere-hull clearance queries on site.machine’s permission controller, and a snapshot of the stopping corridor.
  • ClickLossScenario: A silent click-through regression from ctr_before to ctr_after at request_rate, caught after detection_time; each click is worth value_per_click.
  • ClockConversionScenario: A sensor clock drifting at drift_rate between syncs every sync_interval, converted under site’s bound.
  • ClosedLoopCampaignScenario: clean_runs failure-free closed-loop trials of a frozen policy in one cell of table on site, each run long with an automated reset, bounded at one-sided confidence.
  • CloudEdgeCostScenario: Serving a network of sites from the cloud (pay per image) or from a device at each site (buy up front).
  • ClusterExpansionScenario: A synchronous job grown from n_devices to factor times as many on the same checkpoint tier.
  • ClusterTrainingTimeScenario: Training workload on n_devices x hardware at one end-to-end utilization (compute-bound).
  • CodecEpochScenario: epoch_bytes decompressed on the host each epoch for epochs epochs, at each codec’s decode_rates.
  • CohortArmRecord: One policy’s outcomes and settled usage over a cohort of attempted tasks.
  • CohortAttemptCostScenario: One configuration’s cohort of tasks, one bounded attempt each, all charges settled.
  • CohortDispositionScenario: A finite cohort of attempts by disposition at a cutoff, its total cost, and the disposition that emits a learning artifact.
  • ColdStartScenario: A new replica: provision, fetch the weight artifact, load and warm up.
  • ColumnProjectionScenario: A model that uses columns_used of columns_total equal-size columns, read from a row or a columnar file.
  • CommandAuthenticationScenario: Remote commands authenticated on a machine’s permission processor, priced against a takeover budget.
  • CompactionCadenceScenario: Compaction between a trigger and a target watermark over a turn budget.
  • CompactionPaybackScenario: One compaction of a billed trajectory: a summary resent fresh, then shorter cached reads.
  • CompilationPaybackScenario: A compiled decode step and the executions that repay its build.
  • CompileBreakevenScenario: Eager against compiled execution: the work that repays compile_time.
  • ComponentExposureScenario: Independent components, each affected with probability (per day, or at one instant).
  • CompressedReadScenario: Compressed shards read at read_bandwidth, decoded on CPU cores at each codec’s output rate.
  • CompressionPaybackScenario: One synchronization compressed by compression_ratio at a codec cost of codec_time.
  • CompressionRunScenario: A run to a quality target with and without gradient compression on several networks.
  • ConfidenceRegimesScenario: Stipulated proposer confidence against task success in several operating regimes.
  • ConfidenceRegionScenario: A Gaussian position estimate with principal standard deviations sigmas: its coverage region against site’s grasp tolerance.
  • ConformalInsetScenario: A split-conformal bound at miss rate alpha from calibration_count residuals.
  • ConservedBudgetScenario: A root budget granted to two children; the second stops with a remote job billed late.
  • ConstraintAccuracyCostScenario: Accuracy before and after a fairness constraint (percent), with the policy’s stated rates.
  • ContactDeadlineScenario: A constant-speed drive into site’s latch plate: when the force ramp k v t reaches the tripwire and the limit, when braking can begin, and the time left, at both approaches.
  • ContactGridScenario: A contact task binned by contact angle and normal force, and the cells each archive occupies.
  • ContextAdmissionScenario: A serialized request (text plus an image charge) against a window with a reserved reply.
  • ContextBudgetScenario: A request budgeted by role, appending each tool result against selecting a fixed share.
  • ContextGrowthScenario: A context window grown from base_tokens to tokens: a kept cache grows with the ratio, attention’s score work and stored scores with its square.
  • ContextInvalidationScenario: A superseded excerpt right after the reused prefix: remove, replace, or append a notice.
  • ContextMemoryCrossingScenario: Three memories of workload as context grows on hardware: fixed weights, a KV cache linear in tokens, and one layer’s stored scores (all heads) quadratic in tokens.
  • ContinuationForkCostScenario: A trajectory whose value is sampled by forking continuations at chosen turns.
  • ContinuousDeratingScenario: A catalog continuous rating carried to a hotter ambient and a worse heat path.
  • ControlledBranchScenario: Fresh continuations from one saved prefix under several branches, counted by outcome; the Wilson bound a clean branch leaves and the clean runs a target_bound needs.
  • ConvBnReluFusionScenario: A convolution, batch norm, and ReLU on one feature map, run as three kernels or one.
  • ConvLayerScenario: One kernel-by-kernel convolution, stride one, over a height-by-width-by-in_channels input to out_channels feature maps; values of bytes_per_value each.
  • CoolingScenario: Coolant flow that carries heat_load at temperature_rise.
  • CoresetRunScenario: workload trained for epochs over dataset on one hardware at utilization, against a keep_fraction coreset scored by one forward pass of workload or proxy.
  • CorpusDenominatorsScenario: A retained pool of record classes (records, stored tokens per record, selected target tokens per record); focus is the class whose shares are read, admitted the classes a route admits.
  • CorpusRepetitionScenario: A training run (ClusterTrainingTimeScenario) set against the unique tokens of its workload’s own corpus.
  • CorpusTierScenario: An audio corpus (dataset) kept on storage tiers at storage_rates and read at read_bandwidths.
  • CorrelatedLogScenario: duration of records logged at rate whose adjacent records have lag-one correlation lag1_correlation: how few independent samples the record count holds.
  • CorrelatedReviewersScenario: Reviewers who each falsely approve with the same marginal rate, independent or correlated.
  • CounterfactualSearchScenario: Replay paths that blame for a failure must search: a whole horizon, or one checked window.
  • CoverageFloorScenario: A keep_fraction coreset drawn without regard to class from pool examples, of which rare_share belong to a rare class that needs at least floor examples.
  • CrashRateDenominatorScenario: A reported before-and-after crash rate and the same comparison on the cohort whose exposure is complete (rates per million miles).
  • CreditWindowScenario: A producer feeding a slower model consumer, unbounded or behind a credit window.
  • CrossEntropyScenario: Cross-entropy losses for named predicted probabilities of the correct class, among classes.
  • CurriculumScheduleScenario: A curriculum that reaches target in curriculum_epochs against baseline_epochs shuffled, with an optional linear warmup of warmup_epochs (epoch j serves j/W of the data).
  • CycleBudgetScenario: Stage sub-budgets of one permission check against site’s declared WCET and deadline.

D

  • DailyUploadScenario: A site’s daily captures sent over its uplink inside a working window, against compact summaries.
  • DataBudgetScenario: A data budget split into shares by category; the labeling share buys labels at label_price plus review_overhead for quality review.
  • DataCostBreakdownScenario: A supervised dataset’s bill: acquisition, labels at label_price, storage_bytes kept for storage_duration at storage_rate, and runs training runs of run_time each on accelerators at accelerator_rate.
  • DataEchoingScenario: A host that prepares host_rate samples a second feeding an accelerator that consumes accelerator_rate, over epochs of examples, with each prepared sample used echo_factor times.
  • DataMovementEnergyScenario: One weight of bytes_per_param used for flops_per_param FLOPs at stated per-op energies.
  • DataParallelAttackSurfaceScenario: workload trained on fleet with dp_degree data-parallel replicas: the gradient each step exchanges, the checkpoint it writes, and the traffic of a robust aggregate that gathers every replica’s gradient instead of a ring AllReduce of their sum.
  • DataParallelIntensityScenario: Communication intensity of one data-parallel worker of workload.
  • DataParallelScaleOutScenario: A data-parallel job of workload on nodes nodes, from a one-device profile.
  • DataScalingReturnScenario: Growing a dataset from base_examples to scaled_examples for accuracy_gain points.
  • DataStallScenario: A batch delivered in io_time against compute_time of compute, in sequence or overlapped.
  • DeadlinePolicyScenario: One candidate, breadth in parallel, or feedback, for one task under one deadline.
  • DecisionChainScenario: links independently kept decision records behind one admitted action, each retrievable with probability p; the whole chain resolves with p^links for each p in link_probabilities.
  • DecodeBudgetScenario: A constructed decode-step budget, cut interval by interval, against a TPOT target.
  • DecodeScenario: One decode step of workload on hardware (weight-streaming bound).
  • DecoderFamiliesScenario: One fresh machine.chunk_horizon-step chunk from backbone, per decoder family.
  • DedicatedBreakEvenScenario: Renting a matched-capability API against owning capacity at a fixed hourly cost.
  • DeficitRoundRobinScenario: Backlogged request classes sharing model calls under weighted deficit round-robin.
  • DelayPhaseMarginScenario: A loop with phase_margin at crossover, closed through a pure delay.
  • DelegationCohortScenario: A single controller and a delegated design on one matched cohort, priced alike.
  • DelegationTokenBreakEvenScenario: Delegation at a token multiple m per attempt: cost per accepted task scales by m p_single / p_multi.
  • DemandReboundScenario: Per-unit energy scaled by a factor (or by a unit-price ratio) while volume scales by another.
  • DemonstrationCampaignScenario: Scheduled sessions of attempt-and-reset cycles: demonstrations kept per session, the campaign that keeps target_kept of them, and the wear it puts on a gear and a cable.
  • DenseLayerScenario: One dense layer: in_features inputs to out_features outputs, bytes_per_weight each.
  • DenseNetworkScenario: A dense network of layer_widths at batch_size: parameters, FLOPs, traffic, memory.
  • DenseScalingSweepScenario: Dense networks between a fixed input and output, swept in hidden width and in depth.
  • DependencyScheduleScenario: A dependency graph on interchangeable slots: aggregate work, the critical path, and both bounds.
  • DesignTradeoffScenario: One design-space move of a knob from baseline to alternative, with an illustrative quality gain from gain_low to gain_high (fractions); costs scale by ratio.
  • DetectionDelayLossScenario: A fault that cuts quality_drop of annual_value until it is detected, found after slow_detection or fast_detection, incidents_per_year times a year.
  • DetectionShortfallScenario: site.machine at speed with a stop sized for a group detected in reference of frames, met by groups detected less often; misses allowed in every frame with probability all_miss_probability, frames independent.
  • DevelopmentSweepScenario: A sweep of candidates runs, each costing relative_cost of the final run.
  • DiagnosticCoverageScenario: Fault-rate strata (FIT) to be left at most target FIT unmitigated, shown at confidence.
  • DisclosurePenaltyScenario: Penalties for an incomplete account: days_withheld at daily_maximum each, plus others (name -> amount), all in USD.
  • DiscountHorizonScenario: One rollout of decisions step apart under discount discount: how far the return reaches, in decisions and in seconds, and the discount that keeps retained_weight of the final reward at the first decision.
  • DiurnalLendingScenario: A fleet split between a serving reservation and a training base, static against lent.
  • DominantShareScenario: Dominant-resource fairness by progressive filling of indivisible bundles on capacity.
  • DpSgdRunScenario: Inputs a DP-SGD privacy accountant needs for one Poisson-sampled run on dataset_size examples; this type does not estimate epsilon.
  • DpTrainingPlanScenario: An illustrative differential-privacy planning scenario for one training run of base_cost.
  • DriftConfirmationScenario: Labeled outcomes that confirm a drop of drop from baseline_accuracy.
  • DriveBusScenario: site.machine’s shared drive bus: a coincident load step, and a stop from the drive limit into it.
  • DualBankUpdateScenario: An over-the-air update that keeps a second flash bank: two copies of model at bytes_per_param in the flash of device.
  • DuplicateRemovalScenario: Near-duplicate shares removed from run’s corpus: the training time and energy each share returns, against one read of the corpus and the MinHash signatures of its documents.
  • DutyCycledPowerScenario: An always-on detector, a stage active a share of the time, and a full model run per event.
  • DynamicReachScenario: A target target_distance from the gripper at cadence.machine’s TCP speed ceiling, against the cadence scenario’s evidence windows.
  • DynamicWindowScenario: machine’s base over one chunk renewal at site: its dynamic window and the classical admissible speed against the site’s clear distance and credible deceleration.

E

  • EfficiencyClaimGapScenario: A datasheet operating point against a sustained one: operations per watt at each, and their ratio.
  • ElasticResizeScenario: Three ways to run one job over window: start small, wait for the full size, or step up.
  • ElementwiseIntensityScenario: An unfused elementwise operation: flops_per_element FLOPs while operands_moved values of bytes_per_value each are read or written.
  • ElementwiseOpScenario: An element-wise or normalization kernel over elements values, run unfused.
  • EmbeddingGatherScenario: One recommender request on hardware: one row of width values per categorical feature of dataset is gathered, then the rows and the bottom MLP’s output interact by pairwise dot products.
  • EmbeddingTableScenario: tables embedding tables of rows rows, each width values wide, against hardware.
  • EmbodiedPaybackScenario: A replacement for device that does its work with saved_fraction less energy, on named grids.
  • EncoderRateScenario: An encoder of flop_per_frame on every site.machine navigation frame, against the host SoC’s dense FP16 peak.
  • EncryptedInferenceLatencyScenario: One inference at plaintext_latency, with symmetric encryption around it (encryption_overhead) and computed under homomorphic encryption (fhe_multiplier).
  • EnergyPerInferenceScenario: Energy per inference across tiers: solver-derived device energies plus stated anchors.
  • EnvelopeAmortizationScenario: calls sequential calls of work_per_call, with envelope setup paid per call or once.
  • EpochGapScenario: Named acquisition epochs in one cycle; the pair to fuse is projected across its gap at speed.
  • EpochOverheadScenario: One training epoch of network on a host CPU and an accelerator: work against fixed overhead.
  • EqualOpportunityPriceScenario: Utility per applicant of raising group’s TPR to reference_group’s by moving its threshold, when that move raises its FPR by fpr_increase: a repaid loan earns repay_margin, a default loses default_loss (both USD).
  • ErrorFeedbackScenario: A rounding compressor applied to a gradient sequence, with and without error feedback.
  • EstimatorConsistencyScenario: A qualification campaign of runs scalar estimates, and single-update gates against fusion’s misdated update.
  • EtherCatCycleScenario: num_axes drives on one EtherCAT chain, bytes_per_axis of process data each per cycle.
  • EventRetentionScenario: Event-triggered retention: pre_trigger before and post_trigger after each of events_per_day events, against keeping the whole operating_day.
  • EvidenceHorizonScenario: site’s conveyor target tracked by machine’s chunk policy: evidence horizon against renewal.
  • ExactScanScenario: An exact vector scan on one host: bytes, intensity, and the bandwidth floor.
  • ExpertWeightTrafficScenario: Weight bytes of the mixture-of-experts workload, resident and read per token, against a dense model of dense_parameters that reads every weight each step.
  • ExplanationLoadScenario: Reasons owed for every denial: decisions_per_day at denial_share, each explained by a sampling attributor of samples model evaluations or by exact Shapley over features.
  • ExplanationLogScenario: Logging an explanation with every prediction: predictions_per_day for days_per_month, each a map_size_low to map_size_high attribution map (or tabular attributions of attribution_bytes_low to attribution_bytes_high per feature).
