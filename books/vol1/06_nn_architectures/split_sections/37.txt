### Characteristic Bottlenecks

While @tbl-nn-architectures-machine-demands details the execution profile of each architecture, their core structural innovations typically expose characteristic system bottlenecks in representative regimes:

- **MLPs**: Streamed dense weights stress memory bandwidth at small batch sizes.
- **CNNs**: Convolutional reuse raises arithmetic intensity, stressing compute throughput.
- **RNNs**: Recurrence creates serial dependencies, limiting parallelization.
- **Transformers**: Dense attention computes quadratic score interactions and materializes large state, stressing compute or capacity depending on sequence length.
- **DLRM**: Large embedding tables exceed device memory, stressing capacity and sparse gather efficiency.

Batch size, precision, kernel implementation, and the target machine determine which of these demands binds in a deployment.
