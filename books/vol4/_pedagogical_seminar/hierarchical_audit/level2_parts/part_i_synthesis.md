## Part Narrative Arc
Part I establishes the foundational architecture of Physical AI by defining the stark separation between forgiving digital computation and irreversible physical consequences across the Causal Boundary. It mathematically grounds these consequences in the severe thermodynamic and kinematic limits of the physical Body, which rigidly constrain the open-world semantic capabilities of modern neural Brains. Finally, it resolves this computational-physical tension through a deterministic Nervous System that safely arbitrates and translates statistical neural proposals into strictly bounded physical execution.

## Unified Machine Archetypes & Case Studies
- **Articulated Robotic Arm**: Introduced to demonstrate the causal boundary of mass, it becomes a quantitative subject for thermal holding-torque budgets in the Body, acts as the physical target for semantic pick-and-place proposals from the Brain, and serves as the controlled plant managed by the deterministic joint limits of the Nervous System.
- **Autonomous Mobile Robot (AMR)**: Vividly illustrates unguided displacement and kinetic momentum, demonstrating why the Brain's computational latency actively consumes physical stopping distance and why the Nervous System must enforce strict watchdog lease durations bounded by clear distance.
