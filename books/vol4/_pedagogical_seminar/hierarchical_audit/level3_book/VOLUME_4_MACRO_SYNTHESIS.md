## 1. The Grand Narrative of Physical AI

Physical AI represents a fundamental paradigm shift from the forgiving, idempotent abstractions of pure digital machine learning to the irreversible, causally bound reality of physical space and time. Unlike digital systems where errors incur mere computational retries, Physical AI operates across the Causal Boundary, where stochastic neural outputs translate into permanent thermodynamic and kinetic consequences. This volume systematically establishes that true physical intelligence cannot be achieved by simply embedding a large foundation model into a chassis; it requires a structural reckoning with hardware budgets, temporal decay, and the uncompromising laws of physics.

The volume traces this evolution from the ground up, beginning with the Anatomy of the hardware where a rigid, deterministic Nervous System must act as an arbiter for the stochastic proposals of the neural Brain. It then explores the Teaching of these systems, revealing how physical data collection inherently incurs thermodynamic debt and compounding covariate shifts that break traditional open-loop validation paradigms. In the Running phase, these models are integrated into a multi-rate runtime pipeline where perception is not absolute truth, but an expiring epistemic belief, and where neural intents must be translated into kinematically feasible, continuously monitored trajectories enforced at the bare-metal silicon level.

Ultimately, deploying these machines requires a shift from static software releases to Governing via cryptographically revocable leases of physical authority. By mapping human handover constraints, rigorous fault injection, and the stark epistemic limits of open-world deployment, the volume charts the Frontier of embodied intelligence. It concludes that safe physical AI must systematically manage uncertainty through residual-claims registers and deterministic hardware interlocks, framing the future of the field not just as a software optimization problem, but as a deeply integrated cyber-physical engineering discipline.
