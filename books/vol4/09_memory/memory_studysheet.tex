\frontside
\begin{bigidea}
A robot keeps acting on a scene it can no longer see. Memory carries the last evidence forward,
but the hidden world keeps moving, so every remembered claim has a \hl{physical deadline} set by how
fast unseen things move and how much clearance the task allows. Past it the claim stops
authorizing motion: work across the \hl{causal boundary} cannot be recalled.
\end{bigidea}

\begin{leftcol}
\sheetsec{Key ideas}
\begin{ideas}
\idea{A belief is a dated claim with a growth law.}{Frame, evidence epoch $\EvidenceEpoch$ of the last
  measurement, initial error, and error growth valid inside a stated region.}
\idea{Prediction advances the estimate, never the evidence.}{Republishing or restamping keeps
  $\EvidenceEpoch$; only a measurement renews it. Age runs from $\EvidenceEpoch$.}
\idea{Covariance is a claim to test.}{A range stamped on arrival keeps a \val{sigfused}\,mm spread yet drags
  the estimate \val{misshift}\,mm. Test offline on ground truth, online on innovations.}
\idea{Occupancy before identity.}{An unconfirmed track blocks motion at once; a lost track's
  space becomes unknown, not free.}
\idea{Transforms age; the lever arm sets the error.}{A stale camera edge on the current wrist:
  $\ToolPositionError \approx \InitialTrackingError + \DriftVelocityBound\FiniteChange\TimeInstant
  + \LeverArmLength\JointAngularRate\FiniteChange\TimeInstant$. Compose edges at one epoch.}
\idea{Zero innovation is not zero error.}{If two states give one reading (force plus bias), no estimator
  separates them. Add a channel or excite a known state.}
\end{ideas}

\ans{2}{Quadratic ($\DisturbanceAccelerationBound$ nonzero): \val{halfa}$\FiniteChange\TimeInstant^2$ +

\begin{practicebox}\boxtitle{How long does one frame certify the aisle ahead?}
The camera saw the aisle empty; uprights hide the cross aisle. The parked hull is
$\ClearDistance$ = \val{dclear}\,m from the rack end; $\LocalizationBound$ = \val{dloc}\,mm,
$\ProtectiveClearanceMargin$ = \val{dmar}\,mm, $\HumanWalkingSpeedBound$ = \val{vh}\,m/s, side gap \val{side}\,mm. Frame age \val{age}\,ms; period \val{frame}\,ms.\par\vspace{5pt}

\begin{steps}
\item Usable depth: \val{dclearmm} $-$ \val{dloc} $-$ \val{dmar} = \res{\val{tolahead}\,mm}.
\item $\BeliefInvalidationHorizon$ = \val{tolahead}\,mm $\div$ \val{vh}\,m/s = \res{\val{tahead}\,ms}.
\item The age erodes \val{vh} $\times$ \val{age} = \val{eroded}\,mm: \res{\val{tleft}\,ms} left, about \val{framesleft} frames.
\item Abreast, side gap: \val{side} $-$ \val{dloc} $-$ \val{dmar} = \res{\val{tolabreast}\,mm}; certified for \val{tolabreast}\,ms.
\end{steps}
\takeaway{Memory adds a deadline, not distance: beside the rack end, see the cross aisle or keep people out.}
\end{practicebox}

\sheetsec{Common mistakes}
\mistake{``The transform is fresh: every message is under \val{fresh}\,ms old.''}%
  {The camera died; middleware restamps its last transform at \val{repub}\,Hz. The belt at \val{vbelt}\,m/s
   carries the mug \val{frozenerr}\,mm in \val{window}\,ms, past the \val{grasptol}\,mm grasp tolerance. Age runs from $\EvidenceEpoch$.}{}
\mistake{``A finer clock would fix the stale wrist chain.''}%
  {From $\InitialTrackingError$ = \val{ezero}\,mm, one stale frame (\val{wframe}\,ms) adds \val{drift}\,mm drift and \val{lever}\,mm lever
   swing (\val{rarm}\,mm, \val{omega}\,rad/s): \val{etool}\,mm in a \val{rtol}\,mm slot. Composed at one capture epoch: \val{esame}\,mm.}{}

\begin{qs}
\item \assumed{} dust doubles the lidar spread to \val{qsiglidar}\,mm; predicted prior \val{sigpred}\,mm.
  Gain and new spread? If the return arrived late, where is it applied?
\item \assumed{} the latch slot is machined to \val{qrtol}\,mm; $\InitialTrackingError$ = \val{ezero}\,mm,
  $\DriftVelocityBound$ = \val{vdrift}\,mm/s, $\DisturbanceAccelerationBound$ = \val{adist}\,mm/s$^2$. Which expiry form
  applies, and $\BeliefInvalidationHorizon$? What would a constant-velocity rule admit?
\item The permission controller: \val{tquery}\,$\mu$s per hull query, SRAM at \val{sramgbs}\,GB/s,
  \val{cellbytes}\,B cells. (a) Read budget? Does an occupancy scan fit for a hull of radius \val{rhull}\,m,
  $\ProtectiveClearanceMargin$ = \val{dmar}\,mm, at \val{pitchfivecm}\,cm? (b) Does a \val{slab}\,m$^3$ distance-field
  snapshot fit \val{srammib}\,MiB at \val{pitchfivecm}\,cm? At \val{pitchtwocm}\,cm?
\item A wrist gauge reads a steady \val{freading}\,N for \val{fspan}\,min, zero innovation, reported
  $\pm$\val{fsigma}\,N; a warm joint may drift its bias \val{frate}\,N/min. Is the force known? What observation decides?
\end{qs}
