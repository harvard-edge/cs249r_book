\frontside
\begin{bigidea}
Normal running almost never makes the protective response act.
Verification \hl{creates each fault on purpose}, measures the physical response with independent instruments, and scores it against conditions fixed in advance. Work across the
\hl{causal boundary} cannot be recalled, so every untested condition limits what release may grant.
\end{bigidea}

\begin{leftcol}
\sheetsec{Key ideas}
\begin{ideas}
\idea{The envelope couples outside and inside.}{Floor and speed meet bus voltage and load; danger sits at
  coupled corners one-variable sweeps miss.}
\idea{Build each fault as a five-element chain.}{Negated condition, injection point, detector, bounded
  response, witnessed pass; an unmeasurable link is a gap.}
\idea{Fix the oracle first, timed from the disrupted event.}{A stalled proposer makes no target, so F1
  starts at the last valid renewal, not at sensor capture.}
\idea{Four verdicts, none erased.}{Invalid and unobservable never become pass; an unmeasured field
  is unavailable, never zero.}
\idea{A rung tests only what it makes real.}{A simulator cannot sag a rail it does not model; route each
  fault to the lowest rung that contains its mechanism.}
\idea{Search finds counterexamples, not certificates.}{Failing search runs are not a rate without
  likelihood weights; a simulated margin counts only after its transfer discount.}
\end{ideas}

\vspace{9pt}
\sheetsec{Four verdicts}
\begin{sheettable}{@{}l L@{}}
\thead{Verdict} & \thead{Means}\\ \midrule
\texttt{PASS} & preconditions and injection verified; independent witnesses show the claimed path acted; every margin $\ge 0$\\
\texttt{FAIL} & a measured predicate went negative\\
\texttt{INVALID} & a precondition or the injection miscarried\\
\texttt{UNOBSERVABLE} & scoring telemetry was lost; the partial trace is kept\\
\end{sheettable}

\vspace{9pt}
\sheetsec{The qualification ladder}
\begin{sheettable}{@{}l L L@{}}
\thead{Rung} & \thead{Makes real} & \thead{Still a model}\\ \midrule
Software (SIL) & decision logic & timing, bus, supply, drives, floor\\
Processor (PIL) & target execution timing & bus; no supply transients\\
Hardware (HIL) & bus, supply, drives and brakes & contact and floor\\
Physical rig & the whole loop & nothing; slow, wears out\\
\end{sheettable}
\end{leftcol}%

\fcard{Signed margin}%
  {\StlRobustness = \text{bound} - \text{measured}}%
  {Positive: held with that much spare; negative: missed by that much. Keep onset (ms) and travel (mm)
   apart; to compare them, divide each by its bound, fixed before the run.}%
  {}{}
\fcard{Sim-to-real discount}%
  {\PhysicalStlRobustness \ge \SimulatedStlRobustness - \PredicateLipschitzConstant\,\TransferDiscrepancyBound}%
  {A simulated margin $\SimulatedStlRobustness$ counts only after subtracting the worst sim-to-real
   gap $\TransferDiscrepancyBound$ times its effect on the margin, $\PredicateLipschitzConstant$.}%
  {The gap bound holds only over the conditions where it was measured.}{}

  (b) \res{UNOBSERVABLE}: travel cannot be scored; it is unavailable, not zero, and stays counted.
  (c) \res{FAIL}: the claimed permission path never began powered deceleration (four verdicts).}

\sheetsec{Worked example}
\begin{practicebox}\boxtitle{What may an F1 trial at aisle speed travel?}
The chunk policy freezes after a renewal at \val{v}\,m/s. Lease \val{lease}\,ms, tick
\val{tick}\,ms, bus \val{bus}\,ms, drive response \val{act}\,ms; $\CredibleBrakingDeceleration$ = \val{a}\,m/s$^2$,
$\JerkLimit$ = \val{j}\,m/s$^3$, inset \val{inset}\,mm. The rack end is \val{clear}\,mm away.
\begin{steps}
\item Onset bound: \val{lease} + \val{tick} + \val{bus} + \val{act} = \res{\val{onset}\,ms}; travel
  \val{v}\,m/s $\times$ \val{onset}\,ms = \res{\val{pre}\,mm}.
\item Braking: $\val{v}^2/(2\times\val{a})$ = \val{brake}\,mm; ramp: $\val{v}\times\val{a}/(2\times\val{j})$ = \val{ramp}\,mm.
\item On time it travels \val{ontime}\,mm; plus the inset, \res{\val{bound}\,mm}.
\item The capture subtotal, \val{capture}\,mm, is \val{gap}\,mm longer: against it, onset could slip about \res{\val{gapms}\,ms} unnoticed.
\end{steps}
\takeaway{Measure from the event the fault disrupts, or the distance predicate hides a late onset.}
\end{practicebox}

\sheetsec{Common mistakes}
\mistake{``The base stopped well short of the rack, so the trial passed.''}%
  {A modeled trace brakes at \val{lateonset}\,ms: onset margin $\val{latemargin}$\,ms, yet travel
   \val{latetravel}\,mm keeps \val{latedist}\,mm and stops \val{lateshort}\,mm short. It fails.}{}
\mistake{``A million simulated passes prove the stop.''}%
  {A simulator cannot fail on a sag or slip it does not model.}{}
\mistake{``\val{failshare}\% of our search runs fail, so the base fails that often.''}%
  {Search crowds runs near failure. Weighted back, the chapter's queue-delay model gives
   $\val{pmant}\times10^{\val{pexp}}$ per stop.}{}
