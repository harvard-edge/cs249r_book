\begin{bigidea}
A learned proposer can stall while the machine keeps moving, \val{mmperms}\,mm every millisecond at the
drive limit. The \hl{permission path} alone writes the drives, never waits on the proposer, and
grants each admitted chunk a \hl{lease} on its own clock, because motion past the stopping budget cannot be recalled.
\end{bigidea}

\begin{leftcol}
\sheetsec{Key ideas}
\begin{ideas}
\idea{Proposal is not authority.}{The permission path alone writes the drive registers. If the
  host can too, one unchecked write reaches the motors.}
\idea{Independence is a hypothesis.}{The permission path must meet its bound under any proposer
  load; loaded tests, not separate chips, prove it.}
\idea{Rates come from physics.}{Each tier runs at least ten times faster than the one above, never
  waiting on it. Delay spends phase, so fast loops close in the drives.}
\idea{Freshest, not every.}{A proposal is state. A one-slot seqlock gives the tick one bounded
  read; a torn read keeps the last admitted chunk.}
\idea{Only an admitted chunk renews the lease.}{Authority ends at lease expiry, heartbeat timeout,
  stale evidence or a reflex; a heartbeat shows only that the host is alive.}
\idea{A bound, not a percentile.}{A run above a bound refutes it; none below proves it. Records
  label each value: requirement, proven bound, measured distribution.}
\end{ideas}

\begin{answers}
\ans{Finish}{(\val{clear} $-$ \val{over} $-$ \val{ybrake}) $\div$ \val{speed} $=$ \res{\val{yceiling} ms};
  \val{yceiling} $-$ \val{fixedpath} $-$ \val{ageadmit} $=$ \res{\val{yupper} ms}, under the \val{wfloor} ms
  floor. No lease fits: slow down (about \val{ykeep} m/s keeps \val{lease} ms), or cut the floor or the evidence age (lease rule).}
\ans{1}{\val{wcet} $+$ \val{blocking} $+$ \val{qinterf} $=$ \res{\val{qresp} $\mu$s} $\le$ \val{deadline}: meets,
  \val{qmargin} $\mu$s spare. No: it is a measured distribution. A run above the bound would refute it;
  runs below cannot prove it (bound idea).}
\ans{2}{\val{fullturn}$^\circ$ $\times$ \val{trim} Hz $\times$ \val{totaldelay} ms $=$ \res{\val{phasetrim}$^\circ$}: the trim fits.
  The joint loop needs \res{\val{phasejoint}$^\circ$}, over \val{allow}$^\circ$ (the bus alone spends \val{phasebus}$^\circ$):
  it closes in the drive (phase card).}
\columnbreak
\ans{3}{Were heartbeats still arriving? A crash stops them, so the heartbeat timer fires within
  \val{hbtimeout} ms unless the lease lapses first. A hung policy keeps them coming; only the lease ends
  authority (renewal idea).}
\ans{4}{The stiffness and damping gains: stiffness times the largest displacement the door imposes must stay
  under the latch's force limit. A position loop treats contact as error and ramps current until the
  drive saturates or the latch breaks (command table).}
\end{answers}

\sheetsec{Worked example}
\begin{practicebox}\boxtitle{How long may an admitted chunk drive the base?}
The loaded base runs at \val{speed}\,m/s toward a person \val{clearm}\,m ahead; dry floor,
$\CredibleBrakingDeceleration$ = \val{decel}\,m/s$^2$. Localization plus clearance margin \val{over}\,mm; tick
\val{tick}\,ms, bus \val{bus}\,ms, brake onset \val{onset}\,ms. Renewal every \val{renew}\,ms, jitter allowance
\val{jitter}\,ms; evidence \val{ageadmit}\,ms old at admission.
\begin{steps}
\item Braking: $\val{speed}^2/(2\times\val{decel})$ = \res{\val{wbrake}\,mm}.
\item Ceiling: (\val{clear} $-$ \val{over} $-$ \val{wbrake})\,mm $\div$ \val{speed}\,m/s = \res{\val{wceiling}\,ms}.
\item Upper limit: \val{wceiling} $-$ \val{tick} $-$ \val{bus} $-$ \val{onset} $-$ \val{ageadmit} = \res{\val{wupper}\,ms}.
  Floor: \val{renew} $+$ \val{jitter} = \res{\val{wfloor}\,ms}.
\item Choose \val{lease}\,ms, just above the floor: it spends \val{lease}\,ms $\times$ \val{speed}\,m/s = \res{\val{wcost}\,mm}.
\end{steps}
\takeaway{Size the lease from the stopping budget, then keep it near its floor.}
\end{practicebox}

\begin{rightcol}
\sheetsec{Common mistakes}
\mistake{``Lengthen the lease to ride out hiccups.''}%
  {It is a stopping-budget term: \val{longlease}\,ms spends \val{longcost}\,mm more clearance and leaves
   \val{longleft}\,ms under the upper limit.}{}
\mistake{``The watchdog is fed, so the safety loop is checking.''}%
  {Fed from a timer interrupt, it stays fed while the enforcer deadlocks. Feed it only when the tick's
   checks complete.}{}
\mistake{``Reliable delivery is safer: no command is lost.''}%
  {After a link blip it releases a burst of setpoints for a vanished state; the tick needs the
   freshest proposal.}{}
