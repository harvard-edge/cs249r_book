\frontside
\begin{bigidea}
At the edge the fleet is phones the operator cannot standardize. Learning must fit \hl{memory, battery,
heat and the foreground deadline}, and the devices that pass decide \hl{whose data the shared model learns}.
In C\textsuperscript{3} terms: compute fits each device, communication a thin uplink, coordination a represented population.
\end{bigidea}

\begin{leftcol}
\sheetsec{Key ideas}
\begin{ideas}
\idea{Learning multiplies the footprint.}{Gradients, optimizer state and saved activations make a full
  update \val{floormult}--\val{ceilingmult}$\times$ the weights; the background allowance, not RAM, binds.}
\idea{Bytes, not TOPS, set update time.}{A step moves many times an inference's bytes.}
\idea{Background learning is admitted, not assumed.}{Energy, heat and foreground latency must all pass;
  charging relaxes only energy.}
\idea{Each technique shrinks one term.}{Choose the smallest update space that captures
  the local change; fall back to the generic model when adaptation hurts.}
\idea{Admission selects the population.}{Newer, charged, connected devices pass and
  report first: the model learns the admitted fleet.}
\idea{Federation moves updates, not data.}{Updates still leak; secure aggregation
  and differential privacy protect them.}
\end{ideas}

\vspace{8pt}
\sheetsec{Learn on device only if}
Simpler alternatives (local features, lookup tables, central fine-tuning) cannot meet the privacy, connectivity or
quality need, and a measured gain pays the cost. A short deadline fixes where the model runs, not where it learns.\par

\vspace{8pt}
\sheetsec{Which method shrinks which term}
\begin{sheettable}{@{}l L L@{}}
\thead{Method} & \thead{Shrinks} & \thead{Fits when}\\ \midrule
Full & nothing & large shift\\
Bias-only & smallest $\TrainableStateMemory$; activations stay & calibration, tiny budget\\
Head-only & $\TrainableStateMemory$, most activations & new classes, wake word\\
LoRA & $\TrainableStateMemory$ (rank) & one per user, merged\\
Residual adapters & $\TrainableStateMemory$ & switching contexts\\
\end{sheettable}

\fcard{Background admission}%
  {\begin{aligned}&\AdaptationWindowEnergy \le \AdaptationEnergyAllowance \;\land\;
   \DevicePower + \AdaptationPowerIncrement \le \ThermalPowerLimit\\[2pt]
   \land\;&\ForegroundLatency + \AdaptationLatencyIncrease \le \CompletionDeadline\end{aligned}}%
  {Energy within the allowance, power under the thermal limit, foreground latency within its deadline.}%
  {Fails? Shorten the window, lower the duty cycle, or train fewer parameters.}{}
\fcard{Who the model learns from}%
  {\ModelParameters \leftarrow \sum_{\ClientIndex} \frac{\ClientSampleCount_{\ClientIndex}}{\RoundSampleCount}\,\ModelParameters_{\ClientIndex}}%
  {FedAvg weights each completing client by its examples $\ClientSampleCount_{\ClientIndex}$ ($\RoundSampleCount$: their total);
   a late client adds nothing. Coverage ratio = a cohort's share of completed updates (completion logs) $\div$ its fleet
   share (deployment inventory); below one, the cohort is underrepresented.}%
  {Stratify among eligible devices; reweighting cannot add devices never admitted.
   Over-select: target $\div$ completion share; secure aggregation needs a minimum count.}{}

\begin{answers}
\ans{Finish}{\val{weightsmb} + \val{gradsmb} + \val{actmb} = \val{sgdmb} MB, \val{sgdmult}$\times$ the weights: fits if
  workspace and keyboard state fit the \val{sgdspare} MB left. Activations are \val{sgdactshare}\% of it: cut them next
  (memory budget, method table).}
\ans{Upload}{\val{payloadmb} MB $\times$ \val{bitsperbyte} = \val{payloadmbit} Mb. At \val{linkslow} Mb/s, \val{uploadslows} s (\val{uploadslowmin} min):
  misses the deadline; at \val{linkfast} Mb/s, \val{uploadfasts} s. Slow-uplink cohorts drop out of $\RoundSampleCount$:
  underrepresented. Select \val{targetclients} $\div$
  \val{completefrac} = \val{selectraw} $\to$ \res{\val{selectclients}}; if fewer finish, the round is abandoned (who learns).}
\columnbreak
\ans{Coverage}{Budget \val{budgetdone} $\div$ \val{budgetfleet} = \val{budgetcover}; flagship \val{flagshipdone} $\div$
  \val{flagshipfleet} = \val{flagshipcover}. Completion logs over deployment inventory. No: weights cannot add
  absent devices; stratify among eligible ones (who learns).}
\ans{Methods}{(a) Head-only: cuts $\TrainableStateMemory$ and most activations. (b) Residual adapters: small $\TrainableStateMemory$,
  swapped per context. (c) Bias-only: same classes, so no new head; smallest $\TrainableStateMemory$, if its activations fit (method table).}
\ans{Admit}{Latency passes (\val{latms} + \val{dlatms} = \val{summs} $\le$ \val{deadlinems} ms), but \val{devicew} + \val{updatew} =
  \val{sumw} W exceeds \val{thermalw} W: reject. Charging raises only the energy allowance (admission card).}
\end{answers}

\sheetsec{Worked example}
\begin{practicebox}\boxtitle{Can a keyboard phone run a full background update?}
A \val{paramsmillion}M-parameter FP32 model, Adam, activations \val{actfactor}$\times$ the weights.
RAM \val{phoneramgib} GiB (\val{phoneramgb} GB); background allowance \val{allowancemb} MB; memory bandwidth \val{phonebw} GB/s.
\begin{steps}
\item Weights \val{paramsmillion}M $\times$ \val{bytesparam} B = \val{weightsmb} MB; gradients \val{gradsmb};
  Adam \val{moments} $\times$ \val{weightsmb} = \val{adammb}; activations \val{actmb}. Total \res{\val{totalmb} MB},
  \val{amplification}$\times$ the weights.
\item \val{totalmb} $-$ \val{allowancemb} = \res{\val{overmb} MB over}: the OS may evict
  it, though RAM is larger.
\item Bytes per step, one pass per tensor use: forward reads weights, writes activations (\val{fwdmb});
  backward reads both, writes gradients (\val{bwdmb}); Adam reads four arrays, writes three (\val{adamtrafficmb}). Total \res{\val{stepmb} MB}, \val{trafficratio}$\times$ an inference's weight reads.
\item Floor: \val{stepmb} MB $\div$ \val{phonebw} GB/s = \res{\val{stepms} ms} per step before any arithmetic.
\end{steps}
\takeaway{The allowance and bytes moved decide, not RAM or TOPS.}
\end{practicebox}

\begin{rightcol}
\sheetsec{Common mistakes}
\mistake{``A compressed update always uses less network than the data.''}%
  {A week of photos is \val{rawmb} MB; one update \val{updatemb} MB, so break-even is \val{breakeven} rounds a week.
   Hourly rounds (\val{hourlyrounds}) upload \val{hourlyupmb} MB, more than the photos.}{}
\mistake{``Raw data stays on the phone, so federated learning is private.''}%
  {Updates can be inverted to rebuild examples. Secure aggregation hides each update but blocks per-client
   poisoning filters.}{}
\mistake{``Inference must be local for latency, so training must be too.''}%
  {A \val{cameradeadlinems} ms camera deadline under \val{rttlowms}--\val{rtthighms} ms round trips forces local inference, but a fixed
   model runs offline too: learn locally only for a measured gain.}{}
