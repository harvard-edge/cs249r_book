All rows identify a treatment, learning goal, three-stage sequence, appearance,

Independent cross-review
Two additional passes reviewed the 16 first-wave proposals, plus 12 and 10 other
sampled entries respectively. The following refinements were incorporated:

- Matrix tiling: reuse can improve utilization but cannot guarantee peak rate;
  retain register pressure, occupancy, tile shape, and other bottlenecks.
- Communication overlap: gradient readiness is followed by the source's
  nonoverlappable GPU-occupying overhead before bulk transfer can overlap.
- PagedAttention: explicitly change from the A/B reservation scenario to the
  C/D fragmented scenario; do not teleport occupants in a continuous replay.
- Multi-rate mailbox: show a clean even/even exchange, identify receive interrupt
  as writer and tick as preempting reader, then distinguish rejected copies
  from proposer stall and lease expiry. Old data never renews its lease.
- Fail-closed release: authentication failures always refuse. Only the stated
  stale-calibration case can use a separately signed restricted case, subject
  to its own checks and the per-action permission path.
