Proposed actual-learner evaluation (not yet conducted)
Use a short moderated session on a desktop and a phone, including readers with
some systems background and readers encountering the operation for the first
time. Ask participants to think aloud; do not explain the controls first.
Record device, prior familiarity, first unprompted action, task completion,
requests for help, observed confusion, and their own explanation. Record a
prediction before revealing its answer; distinguish that from post-reveal
self-checks. Do not interpret interface completion alone as conceptual mastery.

1. Find Explore, play and pause convolution, then locate one specified output.
   Observe whether the reader can relate its input window, products, and sum.
2. Before opening the edge example, predict its sign and explain why; reveal
   the result and ask which filter terms account for it. Ask whether moving to
   the next window requires new filter weights and which input elements are
   reused, distinguishing shared parameters and reuse from arithmetic alone.
   Note revisions to the explanation without assigning an invented score.
3. Before showing the quantization comparison, ask which quantities should
   change when activation scale changes. Then inspect both runs and explain
   why different errors can coexist with the same six operand bytes.
4. In the paging lesson, locate logical page 2 physically, identify who owns
   slots 2 and 3, and explain why physical adjacency is unnecessary. Then ask
   whether E's pages can be reused while its final consumer is pending.
5. On the phone, move backward, change a declared case, and return to Diagram.
   Ask which information was difficult to keep in view and which control or
   explanation was unnecessary. Use this to choose the next revision.

Retention review — substantive mechanisms, not frame count
- tensor-memory-layout: Selected tensor cells map to different storage offsets; traversal exposes locality.
- matrix-tiling: Operand copies, numeric multiply-accumulate results, and final writes change as tiles execute.
- checkpointing: Activations are allocated, released, and recomputed, exposing the memory/work tradeoff.
- comm-compute-overlap: Readiness, simultaneous communication/computation, and the final exposed barrier change over the schedule.
- hierarchical-allreduce: Shard ownership and partial sums evolve into global results across the hierarchy.
- paged-attention: Allocation, growth, block-table entries, and release change cache ownership.
- storage-checkpoint-staging: Snapshot ownership and durable copies change before the manifest commits the checkpoint.
- v3-hierarchical-budget: Reserved, settled, and free balances change as child work and late bills consume a budget.
- v3-idempotency: Selectable replay, pending, conflict, unknown, and effect cases change operation outcomes.
- v3-intent-ordering: Selectable failure points change whether dispatch and durable intent survive.
- v3-paged-mapping: Block lookup, copy-on-write tail copying, and appending change the mapping and data.
- physical-multirate: Lease time, fallback, and mailbox-counter states change as producer and consumer progress.
- physical-ray-epochs: Rays, cells, and accumulated evidence actually change across epochs.
- physical-stopping-budget: Additive stopping distances, candidate speed, and dispatch charge change whether clearance suffices.

- convolution: A shared filter moves between windows; actual products and
  partial sums produce completed output values and reveal overlapping-input reuse.
- quantized-matmul: Float operands become INT8 representations, corrected INT32
  products/sums, and requantized output. A scale change exposes rounding error.
