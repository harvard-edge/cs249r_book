INSTRUCTOR ONLY: ANSWER KEY (READ AFTER THE SESSION)
1. At scale 0.10 and zero-point 3, activation 0.31 maps to real level 0.30,
   stored as code 6. The code labels a level; finite levels and rounding can
   lose information.
2. Pair 2 is activation -0.52 and weight -0.18; codes -2 and -4. Removing
   zero-points gives -5 and -4; product 20 joins retained total 24 to make 44.
   Sources remain available; the total is updated.
3. Products and sums can exceed an INT8 operand's range. Wider accumulation
   reduces overflow risk within its supported range and avoids rounding to
   the output format after each addition. Convert after the reduction.
   Here: total 84, output code 17, represented value 0.42 versus float
   reference 0.4086. The small total 84 does not itself require INT32;
   larger reductions motivate the wider format.
