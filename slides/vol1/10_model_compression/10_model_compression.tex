        \item The smallest model reaching a fixed MMLU score fell 93 times in three years. Will compressing frontier models, or training small models from scratch, close the phone gap first?
        \item A channel-pruned model runs faster on any processor; a 2:4 model runs faster only on some. Which would you ship to a fleet of mixed devices?
        \item Hardware rewards 2:4 patterns, INT8, and multiples of 8. Should models target today's datapaths, or should hardware follow what compresses best?
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
