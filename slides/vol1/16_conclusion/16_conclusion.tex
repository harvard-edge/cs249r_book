  \note{%
    \mlsysnotemeta{3.5 min}{Core}%
    Trace Chapter 1's voice request across four physical machines (Echo Dot board, smartphone, datacenter racks, H100) over five clicks. First, display the numbers for all four stages and ask students to write the limiting term under each. Second, wake word on the Echo board (200 KB INT8 in 1 MB flash, 20 MFLOP around the clock on milliwatts) is limited by power. Third, front-end speech processing on the phone NPU (600 MFLOP, 3.5 MB INT8 within 5 W) is limited by compute. Fourth, cloud DLRM ranking (100 GB embedding tables exceeding one 80 GB H100 before arithmetic) is limited by capacity. Fifth, GPT-2 reply decode (3 GB FP16 weights per token, 0.9 ms floor, 295x compute floor) is limited by bandwidth. Four limits in one request: running the method forward predicts every bottleneck before anything breaks.

    \notecue{Check}{After Step 1, have students write Power, Compute, Capacity, or Bandwidth under each stage from memory before revealing Steps 2 through 5.}
