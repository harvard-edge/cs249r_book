\begin{frame}{Takeaways}
  \normalsize
  \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.21cm}
    \item Learn on the phone only for a measured gain, and fit the update to the background allowance.
    \item Admit an update only when energy, heat, and the foreground deadline all have slack; charging relaxes energy only.
    \item A round waits on its slowest kept phone; local steps trade uplink rounds for client drift; masks hide each update.
    \item A federated model learns from admitted, fast phones; measure coverage per cohort and ship a gated, versioned policy.
  \end{itemize}
  \note{[1 min] [CORE] One line per segment. Ask which slide taught each line. If short on time, read the first and the last.}
\end{frame}
