\begin{frame}{Two jobs holding half a pool each make zero progress}
  \centering
  \mlsyssteps[width=0.96\textwidth,height=5.7cm,keepaspectratio]{gang_step}{3}
  \mlsyscredit{Fleet Orchestration, \S8.4.3 and figure 4; War Story 8.1 (Sigler 2021, OpenAI).}
  \note{[3 min] NARRATE: An 8-GPU pool, two jobs that each need all 8. Synchronous training needs every rank in every collective, so 4 of 8 GPUs complete zero steps. Step 1: placed one GPU at a time, J1 gets 4 and waits for the rest. Step 2: J2 gets the other 4 and also waits. Each holds what the other needs. This is a hold-and-wait deadlock, and all 8 GPUs burn power at zero progress. Step 3: gang scheduling grants J1 all 8 at once; J2 holds nothing until J1 ends. At the chapter's \$\val{gpu_hour_usd} per GPU-hour, a deadlock that strands a \val{fleet10k_gpus}-GPU cluster wastes \$\val{deadlock_usd_h} an hour. (Expected: no; priority orders requests, it does not stop partial holds.) WARN: Atomicity must cover every resource the group needs, including its NVLink domain and network share, or the deadlock moves one layer down. FLEX: [CORE]}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Backfill fills the wait without delaying the gang}
  \centering
  \mlsyssteps[width=0.96\textwidth,height=5.7cm,keepaspectratio]{backfill_step}{2}

\begin{frame}{Takeaways}
  \normalsize
  \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.12cm}
    \item A rigid gang gets every accelerator at once or holds none; reservation and enforced backfill keep it from starving the fleet or idling it.
    \item Fairness is computed over allocations a job can run and measured in waiting time; mixed job sizes make waits climb fast with load.
    \item Placement sets the communication term; the most frequent exchange gets the fastest tier, and elasticity trades a resize cost for an earlier start.
    \item Every preemption, spot loss, and reclaim pays lost work plus restart; discounts and borrowing pay only after those costs.
    \item Allocated, active, and productive capacity differ; idle GPUs beside a full queue usually mean an outdated policy.
  \end{itemize}
  \note{[1.5 min] NARRATE: One line per segment. Ask students which slide taught each line. If short on time, read the first and the last. FLEX: [CORE]}
\end{frame}
