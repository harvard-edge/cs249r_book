\begin{frame}{A rack draws 1.5 times its GPU power}
  \centering
  \mlsyssteps[width=0.98\textwidth,height=4.0cm,keepaspectratio]{rack_step}{3}\\[0.1cm]
  \uncover<3->{\small PUE (power usage effectiveness) $=$ facility power $\div$ IT power}\\[0.1cm]
  \uncover<3->{\footnotesize Losses upstream of the IT meter (transformer, UPS, distribution) raise PUE; server supplies and voltage regulators stay in IT load.}
  \mlsyscredit{Sustainable AI, table 7 (four-node DGX H100 rack budget, PUE about 1.09 allocation) and \S15.6.2.}
  \note{%
  \mlsysnotemeta{2.5 min}{Core}%
  Walk through one four-node DGX H100 rack (table 7). Step 1: 32 GPUs at 700 W total 22.4 kW. Step 3: facility overhead allocated at PUE about 1.09 adds 2.7 kW, reaching 33.5 kW, or 1.50$\times$ GPU power (GPUs are 67\% of the rack). \begin{frame}{Exposed communication and moved bytes both cost joules}
  \centering
  {\large $E_{\text{step}} \approx \bar{P} \times \Big(\dfrac{T_{\text{compute}}}{N} + T_{\text{comm}}(N) + T_{\text{sync}}(N) - T_{\text{overlap}}\Big)$}\\[0.05cm]
  {\footnotesize\textcolor{midgray}{Fleet Law step time $\times$ average fleet power $\bar{P}$ (equation 13)}}\\[0.2cm]
  \safeimg[width=0.92\textwidth,height=2.7cm,keepaspectratio]{energy_ladder.pdf}\\[0.05cm]
  {\small Batch-1 decode: 1 FLOP/byte against a 16 FLOP/byte energy crossover; moving bytes is 94\% of modeled dynamic energy.}
  \mlsyscredit{Sustainable AI, equation 13 (\S15.7.2), table 2 and equation 7 (\S15.4.2), \S15.7.3. Modeled coefficients only.}
  \note{%
  \mlsysnotemeta{1.5 min}{Optional}%
  Top: multiplying Lecture 1's Fleet Law by average fleet power $\bar{P}$ (equation 13) turns every exposed collective or barrier wait into wasted joules across $C^3$; overlap saves energy whenever power does not offset time saved. \begin{frame}{Takeaways}
  \normalsize
  \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.18cm}
    \item Power caps what runs at an instant; energy adds up over the run.
    \item Carbon is IT energy $\times$ PUE $\times$ grid factor, comparable only with five declared fields.
    \item Manufacturing is paid before boot; its share rises on clean grids.
    \item Placement and time-shifting change the grid factor, each at a price.
    \item Demand can outrun efficiency, so efficiency needs an absolute budget.
  \end{itemize}
  \note{%
  \mlsysnotemeta{1.5 min}{Core}%
  Review all five takeaways, one per segment: separate power ceilings from energy accounts, declare all five contract fields for carbon, account for pre-boot embodied carbon on clean grids, price placement, time-shifting, and water explicitly, and pair efficiency targets with absolute budgets against rebound. If short on time, stress the first and fifth bullets.

  \notecue{Next}{An optional slide raises three open policy and siting questions for discussion.}
}
\end{frame}
