SUMMARY
The mid-range OS1 lidar sensor features 90 m range on a dark 10% target,
a 42.4º vertical field of view, and high reliability for the most rugged
conditions. The OS1 is designed for all-weather environments and use in
industrial automation, autonomous vehicles, mapping, smart
infrastructure, and robotics.




HIGHLIGHTS
• Configurable Minimum Range and Return Ordering
• Low Data Rate Profile now available with Dual Returns
• Camera-grade near-infrared and signal data
• Multi-sensor crosstalk suppression
• Ouster Studio for pointcloud evaluation
• Ouster SDK, ROS, and C++ drivers for SW development
