The MCU instead detects the silent heartbeat within \val{heartbeat_timeout} ms and runs the resident S-curve stop: \val{t_stop_aisle} ms and \val{d_stop_aisle} mm from \val{v_aisle} m/s, the travel the budget reserved. Power is removed only after confirmed standstill. WARN: students who pick A treat STO as the strongest rung. For a moving base or a gravity-loaded arm, removing torque can make things worse; for a humanoid mid-stride it causes a fall. IEC 60204-1 calls these stop categories 0 (power off), 1 (controlled stop, then power off), and 2 (power kept). FLEX: [CORE].}
\end{frame}

Correct by the least change: the kinetic barrier makes the stop a half-space on the next torque, and the projection removes only the violating part. Respect contact force: at the latch the margin is force at brake onset, \val{f_cmd} against \val{f_latch} N; at the hand it is the geometry that excludes clamping.
